Terms of Service

How engagements with Woodex Interior work — what a quote binds, when numbers lock, and who owns the drawings. Plain terms, no fine print traps.

Effective 19 September 2026

The studio and these terms

Woodex Interior provides architecture, interior design, fit-out and construction, furniture and joinery (through Woodex Furniture™), and 3D visualization services from LG 90 Link Road, Model Town, Lahore. These terms apply to engagements unless your signed agreement or quotation says otherwise — in which case the signed document wins.

Quotations and Cost-Lock

Every scope is quoted after a site survey or a documented brief, as a fixed itemized BOQ (bill of quantities). The quoted price is locked for the scope and validity period stated on it. Work outside that scope is a variation: it is quoted separately and goes ahead only when you approve it in writing.

Indicative price ranges shown anywhere on this website are guides, not offers. The fixed quote is the only number that binds.

Timelines

Delivery timelines are confirmed in your project programme and assume timely design approvals and access to the site. Delays caused by approved variations or unavailable access are reflected in a revised programme, communicated before they happen.

Design files and intellectual property

Drawings, renders and BOQs prepared for your project are delivered to you as part of the engagement. The studio retains the right to be identified as the author of its design work. We show project work in our portfolio only with client approval, and named clients appear only with written agreement.

Site, approvals and regulations

Authority approvals (for example LDA or other development-authority submissions) are obtained through the agreed scope. Where a submission is part of your engagement, we prepare and manage it; statutory fees are passed through at cost. Regulatory compliance for your business operation — such as operating licences — remains yours.

Payments and warranty

Payment milestones are stated in the quotation. Title to manufactured furniture and joinery passes on full payment. Workmanship is warranted for the period stated in your contract; fair wear and tear and third-party alterations are excluded.

Liability

Our liability for any claim is limited to the fees paid for the affected scope. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot be excluded under Pakistani law.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Pakistan, and disputes are subject to the courts of Lahore.
