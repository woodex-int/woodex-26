## 1.1 Page Purpose

This page has **four jobs** running simultaneously:

| Job            | Purpose                                                                                             |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **SEO**        | Rank for "renovation company Lahore" and "interior renovation" searches                             |

Every section must serve at least one of these four jobs.

## SECTION 9 COPY — COST FACTORS

**H2:**

> What Affects the Cost of Renovation in Lahore?

**Intro:**

> Renovation costs in Pakistan vary significantly based on the type of space, the scope of work and the condition of what exists. Here is what drives your budget before Woodex prepares an itemized BOQ.

**Factor 1 — Type of Space:**
> Office, retail, healthcare, restaurant and residential renovations each carry different material, trade and compliance requirements. A pharmacy renovation requires different finishes than a restaurant, which is different again from a home.

**Factor 2 — Area and Scale of Work:**
> Total square footage and the number of zones, rooms or floors in scope determine material volume, trade-hours and project management time. Larger renovations scale predictably per square foot — but the rate varies with specification.

**Factor 3 — Condition of Existing Space:**
> The biggest cost variable. A well-maintained space needing cosmetic refresh is materially cheaper than a deteriorated space requiring structural repair, moisture remediation or MEP overhaul. The condition audit establishes this before any commitment.

**Factor 4 — Scope Type: Cosmetic vs Structural:**
> Cosmetic renovation — paint, ceilings, flooring, lighting — is fast and economical. Structural renovation — including MEP overhaul, new partitions, plumbing reconfiguration — costs more but delivers a fundamentally transformed space.

**Factor 5 — MEP Upgrades Required:**
> Electrical rewiring, HVAC replacement or upgrade, plumbing and ventilation work are the most variable cost elements. Older buildings often require more MEP work than assumed.

**Factor 6 — Material and Finish Specification:**
> Economy, mid-range and premium material and finish tiers carry very different costs. Every material is costed in the BOQ before you commit.

**Factor 7 — Phasing and Occupied-Space Complexity:**
> Phased renovation around occupied operations adds coordination cost and time. It is more expensive than full-site closure — but almost always worth it to protect revenue or domestic routine.

**Note box:**

> Renovation often delivers a modern, fully upgraded space at a fraction of the cost of moving or rebuilding. Woodex Interior prepares a detailed, itemized BOQ before any work begins — no lump sums with no breakdown.
