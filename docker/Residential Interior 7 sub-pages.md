### SECTION 10 COPY — COST FACTORS

**H2:** What Affects the Cost of a Kitchen?

**Intro:** Kitchen costs in Pakistan vary widely based on layout, specification, materials and appliances. Here is what drives the price of your kitchen before we prepare an itemized quotation.

**Factor 1 — Kitchen Size and Layout:** Linear, L-shape, U-shape and island layouts each use different cabinetry volumes, worktop lengths and installation time. Larger kitchens cost more — but layout efficiency matters more than size.

**Factor 2 — Cabinetry Specification Level:** The board, finish and hardware you choose — from economy PVC board to premium acrylic, veneer or lacquered MDF with soft-close systems — is the single largest cost driver.

**Factor 3 — Countertop Material:** Laminate, engineered quartz, granite and marble differ substantially in price per square foot and in fabrication complexity.

**Factor 4 — Appliance Package:** The number and specification of built-in appliances — hob, oven, extractor, fridge, dishwasher — and whether they are local or imported, changes the budget significantly.

**Factor 5 — Joinery Complexity:** Straight runs are fastest and most economical. Curved fronts, angled units, glass display cabinets, pull-out systems and island peninsulas add manufacturing and installation cost.

**Factor 6 — Timeline and Location:** Urgent timelines and projects outside Lahore add logistics and resource costs. These are confirmed in the quotation.

**Note box:** Woodex Interior does not publish fixed kitchen rate cards. Every quotation is based on your measured layout, chosen specification and selected package.

### SECTION 13 COPY — KITCHEN FAQ

**H2:** Kitchen Design — Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: What is the difference between modular and custom kitchens?**
> Modular kitchens use standardized cabinet sizes and systems, which are faster and more economical. Custom kitchens are designed to your exact dimensions and requirements, giving maximum flexibility for unusual layouts, premium materials and bespoke features. Woodex Interior designs and manufactures both — we recommend based on your space and budget.

**Q2: Does Woodex build the kitchen or only design it?**
> Both. Woodex Interior designs the kitchen and our sister brand Woodex Furniture™ manufactures the cabinetry in-house. Our own installation team fits and finishes the kitchen — so you have one accountable team from brief to handover.

**Q3: How long does a kitchen design and installation take?**
> A typical kitchen project takes 4–8 weeks from site measurement to handover: roughly 1–2 weeks for design and 3D approval, and 3–6 weeks for manufacturing and installation depending on size and specification.

**Q4: Can I see my kitchen in 3D before it is built?**
> Yes. Every Woodex kitchen includes photorealistic 3D visualization before manufacturing begins. You approve the design in 3D — layouts, cabinet style, colour, materials and lighting.

**Q5: Does Woodex provide kitchens for apartments and small spaces?**
> Yes. Small and open-plan kitchens are a core strength — we design compact layouts with clever storage, sliding elements and multi-function features to maximize every square foot.

**Q6: Can Woodex work on my kitchen from abroad?**
> Yes. Overseas Pakistani clients regularly commission kitchens through Woodex. We coordinate remotely with measurements, 3D approvals and photo/video progress reporting, then hand over a finished kitchen.

**Q7: What countertop materials does Woodex offer?**
> We offer the full range — laminate, engineered quartz, granite and marble — chosen to match your design, usage and budget. Countertops are supplied and fitted as part of the project.

**Q8: Is a design fee payable if I proceed with the kitchen?**
> Where the kitchen moves into manufacturing and installation, the design fee is typically adjusted or credited against the project. Our team confirms the fee structure clearly in your quotation before any work begins.
