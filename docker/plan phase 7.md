# PART 1 — PAGE STRATEGY & SEO SPECIFICATIONS

## 1.1 Page Purpose and Strategic Role

The Woodex 3D Studio™ page is strategically unlike every other service page on the site — for one critical reason:

**Every other Woodex service page targets one type of client.**
**The 3D Studio page targets two completely different markets simultaneously.**

```
MARKET 1 — INTERNAL CLIENTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Woodex Interior design and fit-out clients who receive
3D visualization as part of their design or turnkey package.
These clients do not search for "3D rendering" — they
search for interior design or fit-out and discover
3D visualization as a deliverable within the service.

→ This page must confirm to them that 3D is included
  and explain what it looks like and how it works.

MARKET 2 — EXTERNAL VISUALIZATION CLIENTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Architects, developers, real estate companies, contractors,
furniture companies, marketing agencies and international
design firms who need professional 3D visualization
independently — not as part of a design or fit-out service.

→ This page must convert these visitors as a standalone
  revenue stream — separate from interior design and
  fit-out entirely.
```

This dual-market positioning makes the 3D Studio page both a **service confirmation page** for existing Woodex clients and a **standalone conversion page** for the external visualization market.

---

## 1.2 The 3D Studio — Business Model Clarity

Before writing the page, the business model of Woodex 3D Studio™ must be clear:

```
WOODEX 3D STUDIO™ — REVENUE STREAMS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Stream 1: Included in Woodex Interior design packages
          → No separate fee — part of the service
          → Page confirms this to existing clients

Stream 2: Standalone 3D rendering for external clients
          → Architects, developers, real estate
          → Per-image, per-room or per-project fee
          → Independent of any Woodex fit-out contract

Stream 3: Visualization consultancy
          → For large developers and project teams
          → Ongoing visualization support
          → Retainer or per-project basis
```

The page must communicate all three revenue streams — without confusing existing Woodex clients (who receive 3D as part of their package) with external clients (who commission it independently).

> Photography can only capture what exists. 3D rendering shows a space exactly as it will look when completed — before construction begins, before fit-out starts and before a single material is ordered. **Reason 2 — Test Multiple Design Options**

> A photorealistic render can be produced in multiple design variants — different material palettes, different furniture selections, different lighting scenarios — allowing the client to compare and decide before any physical commitment is made. Photography offers no equivalent capability.

**Reason 3 — Approve Before You Build**

> The most expensive mistakes in interior design and construction happen when the client approves a drawing but not the reality that drawing produces. 3D visualization closes the gap — showing the client exactly what the design will produce, in a format they can evaluate without reading technical drawings.

**Reason 4 — Market Before Completion**

> Developers and real estate companies who use photorealistic renders and walkthrough animations in their pre-launch marketing consistently achieve earlier sales, stronger pre-launch engagement and higher price realization than those who wait for photography of the completed asset.

**Reason 5 — Specify with Confidence**

> When a render is produced from a fully resolved material schedule and furniture specification, the client approves the complete package — flooring, ceiling, wall finish, joinery, furniture and lighting — simultaneously. This accelerates procurement, reduces change orders and prevents the "it looked different in the drawing" conversation.

**Reason 6 — Share Without Physical Access**

> A 3D render, walkthrough animation or 360° virtual tour can be shared with stakeholders, investors, planning authorities or overseas clients without requiring a site visit. Remote decision-making becomes possible — and the project moves faster as a result.

## SECTION 9 COPY — COST FACTORS

**H2:**

> What Affects the Cost of 3D Rendering?

**Intro paragraph:**

> 3D rendering fees are determined by the complexity and scope of the project — not by a fixed per-image rate. The following seven factors directly affect the cost of your Woodex 3D Studio™ commission.

---

**Factor 1 — Render Type: Still, Animation or Virtual Tour**

> Still renders — individual photorealistic images at specific camera angles — are the simplest and most affordable output type. Animated walkthroughs require significantly more modelling, rendering time and post-production than still images. 360° virtual tours require specialized rendering and hosting setup. Each output type carries a different production cost, reflected in the quotation.

**Factor 2 — Number of Images or Scenes**

> The total number of camera angles, rooms or scenes required directly determines the total project cost. A single reception area render costs less than a complete floor visualization covering open-plan workstations, meeting rooms, a boardroom, a reception and breakout zones. Volume discounts apply on larger commissions — confirmed in the quotation.

**Factor 3 — Scene Complexity**

> A simple room with standard furniture and flat ceilings renders faster and costs less than a complex scene with curved ceilings, custom joinery, detailed material finishes, multiple light sources and densely furnished spaces. The geometric complexity of the scene is one of the primary drivers of modelling and rendering time.

**Factor 4 — Level of Detail and Realism**

> Photorealistic output requires significantly more modelling time, higher-quality material libraries and longer rendering computation than presentation-quality output. The level of realism specified — standard presentation or photorealistic — affects both production time and cost.

**Factor 5 — Material and Furniture Sourcing**

> Where material textures, finish samples or furniture models need to be sourced, modelled or licensed for the project — rather than drawn from the studio's existing library — this adds to the production time and cost. Providing material samples, product files or reference images at the brief stage reduces this variable significantly.

**Factor 6 — Revision Rounds Required**

> Each quotation includes a standard number of revision rounds — typically two. Additional revisions beyond the agreed scope are charged at a confirmed per-revision rate. Providing a clear, complete brief and consolidated feedback at each review stage minimizes revision requirements and keeps the project on schedule.

**Factor 7 — Turnaround Time**

> Standard turnaround timelines are confirmed at the quotation stage based on current studio capacity. Projects requiring expedited delivery — where the standard timeline must be compressed to meet a pitch deadline, presentation date or marketing launch — carry a rush fee that is confirmed before work begins.
