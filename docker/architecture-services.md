# PART 1 — PAGE STRATEGY & SEO SPECIFICATIONS

## 1.1 Page Purpose

This page has **three jobs**:

| Job | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **SEO** | Rank for "architecture services Lahore", "house design Pakistan", "commercial building architect Lahore" — the highest-value intent: a visitor looking for someone to design their building from the ground up |
| **Education** | Help the visitor understand what architecture services cover (concept design, working drawings, elevations, authority submissions, master planning) and why Woodex is different (buildable designs, PCATP-certified architects, integration with interior + fit-out) |
| **Conversion** | Move the visitor from reading to requesting a consultation — and route them to the correct sub-page (residential vs commercial vs elevation-only vs master planning) |

### Critical Service Differentiation

```
/architecture/                  → ARCHITECTURE (building design + planning)
"I need a building designed from scratch — concept through authority approval"

/residential-interior-design/   → INTERIOR DESIGN (space planning + finishes)
"I need the interior of my existing building planned and styled"

/office-fit-out/                → FIT-OUT (shell completion)
"I have a shell/unfinished space that needs building out"

/office-renovation/             → RENOVATION (existing space upgrade)
"My existing space needs upgrading"

/turnkey-design-build/          → TURNKEY (one contract, end-to-end)
"I want one team to handle everything"
```

**Anti-cannibalisation rule:** Architecture = "I need a building designed and planned." This page must never overlap with interior design (planning interiors), fit-out (completing shells), renovation (upgrading existing), or turnkey (one-contract bundles). Every paragraph must reinforce that architecture = building design, from concept to authority approval.

### Architecture vs Interior Design — The Difference

| Architecture | Interior Design |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Building design — structure, layout, elevations | Interior layout — furniture, finishes, lighting |
| Authority submissions (LDA, CDA, PCATP) | No authority involvement |
| Working drawings for construction teams | Design drawings for fit-out teams |
| Structural coordination with engineers | MEP coordination with contractors |
| New buildings + major extensions | Existing or new-build interiors |

### The Woodex Architecture Differentiators

This page has **five unique selling points**:

1. **PCATP-certified architects** — Pakistan Council of Architects & Town Planners registration; designs meet professional and regulatory standards (where required)
2. **Buildable designs, not just pretty pictures** — every concept is developed to working-drawing stage with AutoCAD/Revit, ready for construction teams to execute
3. **Integration with interior design + fit-out** — the same team that designs your building can plan the interior and physically build it out; seamless transition, no re-briefing
4. **Authority submissions handled** — LDA, CDA, and other authority submissions managed as part of the service; not an afterthought
5. **3D visualization in-house** — photorealistic renders and walkthroughs produced in Woodex's own 3D Studio; no third-party delays

## SECTION 2 — WHAT ARCHITECTURE SERVICES COVER

**H2:** What Architecture Services Cover

Architecture is the first step of any building project. Before interiors, before fit-out, before a single wall is painted — someone has to design the building itself. That is what Woodex Architecture does.

We take your brief — the number of rooms, the plot size, the way you want to live or work — and turn it into a complete set of construction-ready drawings. Concept sketches. 3D visualizations. Detailed working drawings in AutoCAD and Revit. Front elevations. And, when needed, authority submissions to LDA, CDA or other local bodies.

Every design we produce is meant to be built. Not just admired on paper. Our architects work closely with structural engineers and MEP consultants from the early concept stage, so the designs we deliver are structurally sound, spatially efficient and ready for construction teams to execute — without redesign halfway through the build.

**Service grid:**

| Service | What It Includes |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Concept Design (2D & 3D)** | Floor plans, spatial layout, photorealistic 3D renders; 2 revision rounds included |
| **Working Drawings** | Full construction drawings — plans, sections, elevations, details, schedules — in AutoCAD / Revit |
| **Elevations & 3D Views** | Front, rear and side elevations; photorealistic 3D exterior renders |
| **Authority Submissions** | LDA, CDA, and other authority submission preparation and follow-up |
| **Structural Coordination** | Liaison with structural engineers to ensure buildability from concept stage |

**Difference callout box:**

> **Architecture** = building design + authority approval
> **Interior Design** = space planning + finishes (inside the building)
> **Fit-Out** = shell completion + construction scope
> **Renovation** = upgrade of existing occupied space
>
> Need everything under one contract? → **[Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/)**

## SECTION 3 — RESIDENTIAL ARCHITECTURE

**H2:** Residential Architecture

Your home starts with a design. Whether you are building on a 5 Marla plot in Bahria Town or a 2 Kanal property in DHA, Woodex designs houses that are practical, beautiful and ready to build.

Our residential architecture service covers everything from the first concept sketch through to authority-approved working drawings. We design for the way you live — bedroom placement, kitchen flow, natural light, privacy from neighbours, parking, storage, servant quarters, rooftop access — all resolved at the design stage, before construction begins.

Every residential design includes 3D visualization so you can see your home before a single brick is laid. ## SECTION 7 — OUR ARCHITECTURE PROCESS — 8 STEPS

**H2:** Our Architecture Process — 8 Steps

| Step | Title | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 01 | **Brief & Site Survey** | We meet you, understand your requirements, visit the site, take measurements and check zoning and bylaws. This is where we ask the questions that prevent problems later. |
| 02 | **Site Survey** | Detailed site analysis — boundaries, levels, access points, utilities, neighbouring structures. The site survey informs every design decision. |

| 08 | **Final Handover** | Complete drawing set delivered — digital files, printed sets if required. Ready for your construction team or contractor to execute. |

## SECTION 11 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Architecture — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What is the difference between an architect and a civil engineer?**

An architect designs the building — the layout, the elevations, the spatial experience, the look and feel. A civil or structural engineer designs the structural system — the beams, columns, foundations that hold the building up. Both are needed for a complete building project. Woodex handles the architectural design and coordinates with structural engineers as part of our service.

---

**Q2: Do I need PCATP registration for my house design?**

For most residential projects in Lahore (individual houses on private plots), PCATP registration is not mandatory — but it is a mark of professional quality. For commercial buildings, multi-unit developments or authority submissions that require an architect of record, PCATP registration may be required. Woodex has PCATP-certified architects on our team and can advise you on what is required for your specific project.

---

**Q3: How long does a house design take?**

A typical 5–10 Marla house design takes 4–6 weeks from brief to final working drawings. A 1–2 Kanal house takes 6–10 weeks. Front elevation design takes 5–10 working days. Authority approval times are separate and depend on the local authority (typically 2–6 weeks for LDA).

---

**Q4: What is included in the architecture fee?**

The standard architecture fee includes: concept design (2D floor plans + 3D renders), design development, working drawings (AutoCAD or Revit), and authority submission preparation. Authority submission government fees are passed through at cost. Structural engineering coordination is included for full-package projects.

---

**Q5: Can I just get a front elevation designed?**

Yes. Our front elevation design service is a standalone offering — PKR 50,000–150,000 — that delivers a photorealistic 3D render and 2D elevation drawing of your building's front facade. It is one of our most popular individual services, ideal if your floor plans are already finalised but you want a professional, designed exterior.

---

**Q6: Do you work on projects outside Lahore?**

Yes. While our studio is in Lahore (Model Town), we have delivered architecture projects across Punjab and in other cities. Site visits for projects outside Lahore are arranged separately. Remote design collaboration (3D renders, working drawings, virtual meetings) makes distance manageable for most projects.

---

**Q7: Can Woodex design my building AND do the interior design and fit-out?**

Yes — and this is one of Woodex's key advantages. We are one of the few firms in Lahore that handles architecture, interior design, fit-out and furniture (via Woodex Furniture™) under one roof. The architect who designs your building works directly with the interior designer and fit-out team — no re-briefing, no communication gaps, no conflicting drawings. For a fully integrated experience, our [Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/) service covers everything under one contract.

---

**Q8: What if I need changes after the design is approved?**

Minor changes during design development are included (two revision rounds are standard). Once working drawings are issued, changes are charged based on the scope of revision — typically 15–25% of the original fee for significant changes. We always encourage clients to invest time in the concept stage — it is far cheaper to change a drawing than a building under construction.
