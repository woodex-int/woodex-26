## SECTION 2 — WHAT OFFICE BUILDING DESIGN COVERS

**H2:** What Office Building Design Covers

Office building design is the architectural process of planning a new building for business use — from the initial concept through to construction-ready drawings. Unlike renovating or fitting out an existing space, office building architecture starts with the structure itself: how many floors, how the floor plate is shaped, where the lift core goes, how parking is handled, what the facade looks like, and how the building meets every authority requirement.

Woodex designs office buildings from concept to final working drawings. We deliver concept floor plans and 3D visualizations showing the building's form, entrance, lobby and facade — so you can see what your building will look like before a single brick is laid. Working drawings in AutoCAD and Revit — plans, sections, elevations, structural details, MEP coordination drawings, schedules — the complete technical package your contractor builds from. Front and facade elevations designed for brand presence, street context and LDA/CDA requirements. And authority submissions: LDA, CDA, fire safety, environmental — prepared and submitted as part of the service.

Every office building Woodex designs is planned around its specific use: floor plate efficiency (usable area vs circulation), vertical circulation (lifts, stairs, service core), parking ratio (car spaces per 1,000 sq ft), lobby and entrance design (first impression for tenants and visitors), MEP strategy (HVAC, electrical, fire protection, data infrastructure), and facade (energy performance, aesthetics, context).

**Checklist:**

- ✓ Floor plate design (efficiency, flexibility, column-free spans)
- ✓ Vertical circulation planning (lifts, stairs, service core)
- ✓ Parking layout (basement, stilt, or open — per LDA/CDA requirement)
- ✓ Lobby and entrance design
- ✓ Facade and elevation design
- ✓ Authority submissions (LDA, CDA, fire, environmental)

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Office Building Design — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What is the difference between office building architecture and office interior design?**

Office building architecture is the design of the building itself — structure, facade, core, parking, lobby, authority approvals. Office interior design is planning the space inside a tenancy — partitions, finishes, furniture, lighting. You need architecture for a new building or major redevelopment; you need interior design for fitting out a space within an existing building.

---

**Q2: How many floors can Woodex design?**

Woodex designs office buildings from 1 floor (a standalone office building) to 20+ floors (multi-storey corporate towers). The process is the same — the difference is in structural complexity, lift planning, fire strategy, and authority requirements, all of which scale with height.

---

**Q3: What is the typical parking requirement for an office building in Lahore?**

LDA typically requires 1 car space per 50–100 sq m of office area, depending on the plot size, location and building type. The exact ratio is confirmed during authority consultation. Woodex includes parking layout in the concept design and manages the approval process.

---

**Q4: How long does office building architecture take?**

A typical office building takes 8–16 weeks from brief to final working drawings, depending on floors and complexity. Authority approval times are separate: LDA/CDA (4–12 weeks), fire safety (2–4 weeks), environmental (4–8 weeks). We manage all submissions in parallel where possible.

---

**Q5: Can you design a mixed-use building with offices and retail?**

Yes. Mixed-use buildings are a strength — we design the building with integrated but distinct zones for retail (ground/low levels) and offices (upper levels), with separate circulation, services and regulatory requirements in one coherent building.

---

**Q6: What if I only have a plot and need everything designed from scratch?**

That is exactly what office building architecture is. We start from the plot — assessing its dimensions, access, bylaws, and context — and design the building from concept to construction-ready drawings. You bring the plot and the brief; we handle the rest.

---

**Q7: Can Woodex design the building AND do the interior design and fit-out?**

Yes — and this is one of Woodex's key advantages. We handle office building architecture, office interior design, office fit-out and furniture under one roof. The architect works directly with the interior designer and fit-out team — no re-briefing, no conflicting drawings. For everything under one contract: [Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/).

---

**Q8: What if I only need a front elevation for an office building?**

Woodex offers front elevation design as a standalone service — PKR 50,000–150,000. You receive a photorealistic 3D render and 2D elevation drawing. [View Front Elevation Design →](/front-elevation-design/)
