## SECTION 2 — WHAT RESTAURANT BUILDING DESIGN COVERS

**H2:** What Restaurant Building Design Covers

Restaurant and cafe building design is the architectural process of planning a building purpose-built for food and beverage — from the initial concept through to construction-ready drawings. Unlike converting an existing space, restaurant building architecture starts with the requirements that make a restaurant work: the kitchen core, ventilation, seating layout, health department compliance, and the facade that draws customers in.

Woodex designs restaurants and cafes from concept to final working drawings. We deliver concept floor plans and 3D visualizations showing the building's form, dining areas, kitchen position, facade and brand expression — so you can see your restaurant before construction starts. Working drawings in AutoCAD and Revit — plans, sections, elevations, structural details, MEP coordination drawings, schedules — the complete technical package your contractor builds from. Facade elevations designed for street presence and brand identity. And authority submissions: health department, LDA/CDA, fire safety — prepared and submitted as part of the service.

Every restaurant building Woodex designs is planned around the fundamentals of food service: kitchen design (a kitchen that flows — receiving, prep, cooking, plating, washing — with no cross-contamination), ventilation (adequate exhaust and fresh air for cooking smoke, fumes and heat), seating efficiency (covers per square foot, table mix, circulation), and health department compliance (materials, finishes, wash-up areas, pest control, food storage).

**Checklist:**

- ✓ Kitchen design (flow, zoning, hygiene, wash-up areas)
- ✓ Ventilation and exhaust design (cooking fumes, heat, fresh air)
- ✓ Seating layout and cover efficiency
- ✓ Health department compliance (materials + processes)
- ✓ Facade and brand expression
- ✓ Authority submissions (health dept., LDA, CDA, fire)

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Restaurant Building Design — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What is the difference between restaurant architecture and restaurant interior design?**

Restaurant architecture is the design of the building itself — structure, kitchen core, ventilation, facade, health compliance, authority approvals. Restaurant interior design is planning the space inside — seating, lighting, decor, branding. You need architecture for a standalone restaurant building or major redevelopment; interior design for a restaurant within an existing building.

---

**Q2: Do you design the commercial kitchen as part of the architecture?**

Yes. Kitchen design is core to our restaurant architecture service. We plan kitchen flow (receiving → prep → cooking → plating → washing), zone hygiene (clean vs dirty flows), equipment layout, ventilation and exhaust, gas supply, grease traps and wash-up areas — all coordinated with the building's structure, MEP and health department requirements.

---

**Q3: How many covers can I plan per square foot?**

As a rule of thumb, a casual dining restaurant plans roughly 10–12 sq ft of total area per cover (including kitchen, storage, washrooms). A cafe with minimal food servery can plan tighter, around 8–10 sq ft per cover. Table mix (2-seaters, 4-seaters, larger groups) is worked out in the concept design to maximize covers while keeping circulation comfortable.

---

**Q4: What does health department approval involve?**

A commercial kitchen must meet standards for hygienic materials (stainless steel counters, sealed floors, washable wall finishes), correct flows (no cross-contamination of raw/cooked), wash-up facilities, pest control, food storage and ventilation. Woodex designs to these standards and prepares the drawings and documents for health department submission.

---

**Q5: How long does restaurant building architecture take?**

A typical restaurant or cafe takes 6–12 weeks from brief to final working drawings, depending on type and kitchen complexity. Authority approval times are separate: health department (4–8 weeks), LDA/CDA (4–12 weeks), fire (2–4 weeks). We manage all submissions in parallel where possible.

---

**Q6: Can Woodex design the building AND do the interior design and kitchen fit-out?**

Yes — and this is one of Woodex's key advantages. We handle restaurant building architecture, restaurant interior design, restaurant fit-out and furniture under one roof. The architect works directly with the interior designer and fit-out team — no re-briefing, no conflicting drawings. For everything under one contract: [Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/).

---

**Q7: Can you design for a restaurant franchise group with multiple outlets?**

Yes. Woodex designs restaurant buildings for single brands and franchise groups across Pakistan. We develop a repeatable building prototype — consistent kitchen layout, seating, facade and brand expression — that can be adapted to different plots while keeping compliance and brand standards consistent.

---

**Q8: What if I only need a front elevation for a restaurant?**

Woodex offers front elevation design as a standalone service — PKR 50,000–150,000. You receive a photorealistic 3D render and 2D elevation drawing. [View Front Elevation Design →](/front-elevation-design/)
