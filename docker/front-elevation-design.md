## SECTION 2 — WHAT FRONT ELEVATION DESIGN INCLUDES

**H2:** What Front Elevation Design Includes

Front elevation design is the process of creating the visual design for the face of your building — the part the world sees. It includes a photorealistic 3D render showing exactly what your house or building will look like once built — materials, colors, textures, windows, doors, balconies, gates, lighting and street presence — plus a 2D elevation drawing showing the front facade to scale.

This service is for homeowners and developers who already have a building (or approved building plans) and want the facade designed or refreshed. You may have a structure under construction that needs a facade. You may have an existing building you want to renovate visually. You may have floor plans approved but need the front designed.

Woodex delivers front elevation design from our in-house 3D Studio — 3ds Max, AutoCAD and professional rendering. You receive: a photorealistic 3D render of your front elevation (the hero image for approvals, marketing or your own reference), a 2D front elevation drawing to scale (useful for your contractor), and two rounds of revisions to get the design exactly right.

**What you receive:**

- ✓ Photorealistic 3D render of front elevation
- ✓ 2D front elevation drawing (to scale)
- ✓ Two rounds of revisions included
- ✓ Material and color suggestions for construction

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Front Elevation Design — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What is the difference between front elevation design and full architecture?**

Front elevation design is the visual design of the building's facade only — how it looks from the front. Full architecture is the complete building design — layout, structure, MEP, authority approvals. Front elevation design is for buildings that already exist or have approved plans; full architecture is for buildings that need to be designed from scratch.

---

**Q2: Do I need floor plans to get a front elevation designed?**

Floor plans help but are not required. If you have floor plans (approved or draft), they make the elevation design more accurate. If you only have a sketch or description of the building, Woodex can design the elevation based on that — the 3D render will show what the front looks like, and you can use it as a guide for your contractor.

---

**Q3: How many revisions are included?**

Two rounds of revisions are included in the standard fee. Most clients are satisfied after 1–2 rounds. Additional revision rounds are available at a small additional fee if needed.

---

**Q4: Can I use the 3D render for authority submission?**

The 3D render is a visual representation, not a technical drawing. For LDA/CDA authority submission, you typically need a 2D elevation drawing (which is included) plus working drawings (which are part of full architecture services). The 3D render is excellent for marketing, social media, contractor guidance and personal reference.

---

**Q5: How long does front elevation design take?**

Most projects are delivered within 5 working days from brief to final delivery. Larger or more complex projects may take up to 10 working days. Rush delivery (3 working days) is available for an additional fee.

---

**Q6: Can I get side and rear elevations as well?**

Yes. Additional views (side, rear, perspective) are available at PKR 25,000–40,000 per view, each including a 3D render and 2D elevation drawing.

---

**Q7: What style of elevation can Woodex design?**

Any style — modern, contemporary, classical, minimalist, traditional Pakistani, or a blend. Share reference images of styles you like, and Woodex will design an elevation tailored to your building, context and preferences.

---

**Q8: What if I realize I need a full building designed, not just the elevation?**

No problem. The front elevation design fee can be credited toward full architecture services if you engage Woodex for the complete building design within 3 months. [View Architecture Services →](/architecture/)
