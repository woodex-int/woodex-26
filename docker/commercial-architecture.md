### Critical Keyword Differentiation

```

"I need a commercial building designed from scratch"

/office-interior-design/          → OFFICE INTERIOR DESIGN (space planning)
"I need my office interior planned"

/office-fit-out/                  → OFFICE FIT-OUT (shell completion)
"I have a shell space that needs building out"

/turnkey-design-build/            → TURNKEY (one contract, all-in-one)
"I want one team to handle everything"
```

**Anti-cannibalisation rule:** Commercial architecture = "I need a commercial building designed." This page must never overlap with office interior design (interiors), fit-out (shell completion), or turnkey (one-contract bundles). Every paragraph must reinforce that commercial architecture = building design for new construction or major redevelopment.

### Commercial Architecture Differentiators

1. **Building types expertise** — Office towers, retail plazas, restaurants, healthcare facilities, educational buildings — each with distinct functional and regulatory requirements
2. **Authority submissions handled** — LDA, CDA, health department, education department, fire safety — all managed as part of the service
3. **Structural + MEP coordination** — Commercial buildings require complex structural and MEP systems; Woodex coordinates from concept stage
4. **In-house 3D visualization** — Photorealistic renders and walkthroughs for marketing, approvals and stakeholder buy-in
5. **Integration with fit-out + interior design** — The same team designs the building, plans the interior and builds it out — seamless transition

## SECTION 2 — WHAT COMMERCIAL ARCHITECTURE COVERS

**H2:** What Commercial Architecture Covers

Commercial architecture is the design of buildings for business use — offices, retail, restaurants, healthcare facilities, educational buildings. Unlike residential architecture, commercial buildings have distinct functional requirements, stricter regulations, larger structural and MEP systems, and different authority approval processes.

Woodex designs commercial buildings from concept through to construction-ready drawings. We deliver concept floor plans and 3D visualizations showing the building's form, facade and spatial layout. Working drawings in AutoCAD and Revit — plans, sections, elevations, structural details, MEP coordination drawings, schedules — the complete technical package your construction team builds from. Front and facade elevations designed for brand presence and context. And authority submissions: LDA, CDA, health department, education department, fire safety, environmental — prepared and managed as part of the service.

Every commercial design is developed with its specific building type in mind: office towers need efficient floor plates and vertical circulation; retail plazas need shop frontage, anchor tenant planning and customer flow; restaurants need kitchen ventilation, seating efficiency and health compliance; healthcare facilities need clinical workflows, infection control and departmental adjacencies; educational buildings need classroom clusters, circulation and safety.

**Checklist:**

- ✓ Concept design (2D plans + 3D renders)
- ✓ Working drawings (AutoCAD / Revit)
- ✓ Structural details and coordination
- ✓ MEP coordination drawings
- ✓ Facade and elevation design
- ✓ Authority submissions (LDA, CDA, sector-specific)

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Commercial Architecture — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What is the difference between commercial architecture and office interior design?**

Commercial architecture is the design of the building itself — the structure, facade, core, vertical circulation, and authority approvals. Office interior design is the planning of the space inside a tenancy — partitions, finishes, furniture, lighting. You need architecture for a new building or major redevelopment; you need interior design for fitting out a space within an existing building.

---

**Q2: Do you handle authority submissions for commercial buildings?**

Yes. Commercial buildings require multiple approvals: LDA/CDA for the building permit, health department for healthcare and F&B, education department for schools, fire safety for all commercial buildings, and sometimes environmental approvals. Woodex prepares and submits all required drawings and documents as part of the architecture service.

---

**Q3: How long does commercial architecture take?**

A typical commercial building takes 8–16 weeks from brief to final working drawings, depending on size and complexity. Authority approval times are separate and vary: LDA/CDA (4–12 weeks), health department (4–8 weeks), education department (6–12 weeks), fire safety (2–4 weeks). We manage all submissions in parallel where possible.

---

**Q4: What does the commercial architecture fee include?**

The fee includes: concept design (2D plans + 3D renders), design development, working drawings (AutoCAD/Revit), structural coordination, MEP coordination, facade detailing, and authority submission preparation. Government submission fees are passed through at cost.

---

**Q5: Can you design a mixed-use building (retail + offices + residential)?**

Yes. Mixed-use buildings are one of Woodex's strengths — we design the building with integrated but distinct zones for retail (ground/low levels), offices (mid levels), and residential (upper levels), each with separate circulation, services and regulatory requirements. The architecture integrates these into one coherent building.

---

**Q6: Do you work on projects outside Lahore?**

Yes. While our studio is in Lahore, we have delivered commercial architecture projects across Punjab and in other cities. Site visits are arranged; remote collaboration via 3D renders and virtual meetings keeps distance manageable.

---

**Q7: Can Woodex design the building AND do the interior design and fit-out?**

Yes — and this is one of Woodex's key advantages. We handle commercial architecture, office interior design, office fit-out and office furniture under one roof. The architect works directly with the interior designer and fit-out team — no re-briefing, no conflicting drawings. For everything under one contract: [Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/).

---

**Q8: What if I only need a front elevation for a commercial building?**

Woodex offers front elevation design as a standalone service — PKR 50,000–150,000 — for commercial buildings as well as residential. You receive a photorealistic 3D render and 2D elevation drawing. [View Front Elevation Design →](/front-elevation-design/)
