## SECTION 2 — WHAT IS KITCHEN RENOVATION?

**H2:** What Is Kitchen Renovation?

Kitchen renovation is the upgrading of your existing kitchen — new cabinets, replaced countertop, updated backsplash, modern sink and faucet, new lighting, refreshed flooring, or a complete redesign of the layout. Unlike full home renovation (which covers every room), kitchen renovation focuses on the most technically complex room in the house — where plumbing, electrical, ventilation and storage all converge.

Woodex delivers kitchen renovation from design to handover. We survey the kitchen, design the renovation (including 3D visualization so you see the result before we start), build and finish the space, and hand over a kitchen that is beautiful, functional and built to last — typically completed in 3–4 weeks.

The kitchen is the room that adds the most value to any home. A well-renovated kitchen transforms daily life — cooking becomes a pleasure, storage is organised, and the space becomes the heart of the home.

**What kitchen renovation covers:**

- ✓ Cabinet replacement or refacing (upper, lower, pantry, island)
- ✓ Countertop replacement (granite, marble, quartz, solid surface)
- ✓ Backsplash installation (tile, stone, glass, marble slab)
- ✓ Sink and faucet replacement (undermount, farmhouse, single/double bowl)
- ✓ Lighting design (task, ambient, under-cabinet LED)
- ✓ Flooring replacement (tile, vinyl, wood)
- ✓ Plumbing upgrades (hot/cold water, drainage, filtration)
- ✓ Electrical upgrades (outlets, switches, appliance circuits)

## SECTION 5 — KITCHEN RENOVATION PROCESS — 10 STEPS

**H2:** Kitchen Renovation Process — 10 Steps

| Step | Title | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 01 | **Kitchen Survey** | We measure the kitchen, photograph existing conditions, understand your cooking habits, storage needs and style preferences. |
| 02 | **Brief & Scope** | You tell us what you want changed — cabinets, countertop, sink, layout. We define scope and workflow. |
| 03 | **Concept Design** | Layout options, cabinet configurations, material palettes, 3D renders of the finished kitchen. |
| 04 | **Design Refinement** | Two revision rounds — adjust cabinet layout, material choices, sink position, lighting plan. |
| 05 | **Costing & Agreement** | Fixed-price quotation — itemised by component. No surprises. Agreement signed. |
| 06 | **Material Procurement** | Cabinets, countertop slab, backsplash tiles, sink, faucet, lighting, flooring ordered and delivered. |
| 07 | **Preparation** | Existing cabinets, countertop, backsplash removed. Plumbing and electrical rough-in. Walls patched. |
| 08 | **Construction** | Cabinets installed, countertop templated and fitted, backsplash tiled, plumbing connected, flooring laid. |
| 09 | **Finishes & Fixtures** | Sink and faucet installed, lighting fitted, handles and hardware attached, paint touch-ups, deep clean. |
| 10 | **Snag & Handover** | Full inspection — plumbing check, electrical check, alignment, finish quality. Corrections, handover — kitchen ready to cook in. |

## SECTION 7 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Kitchen Renovation — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: How long does kitchen renovation take?**

Most kitchen renovations are completed in 3–4 weeks from design approval to handover. Cabinet refacing: 1–2 weeks. Full renovation: 3–4 weeks.

---

**Q2: Do you manufacture cabinets in-house?**

Yes — through sister brand WOODEX FURNITURE™. Custom cabinets are designed around your cooking and storage needs, then manufactured and installed by our team.

---

**Q3: What countertop materials do you offer?**

Granite, marble, quartz, solid surface (Corian-style) and engineered stone. We present options based on your budget, style and maintenance preferences.

---

**Q4: Can I use the kitchen during renovation?**

Not during full kitchen renovation. We recommend setting up a temporary kitchen elsewhere in the home. For cabinet-only work, partial use may be possible.

---

**Q5: How much does kitchen renovation cost?**

Cabinet refacing: PKR 300K–700K. Full renovation: PKR 800K–2.5M. Exact pricing after free survey.

---

**Q6: Can you reconfigure the kitchen layout?**

Yes. Moving the sink, repositioning the hob, adding an island — we can reconfigure the layout to improve workflow. Plumbing and electrical costs are included in the quotation.

---

**Q7: Do you handle plumbing and electrical?**

Yes. All plumbing (sink, faucet, water lines, drainage, filtration) and electrical (outlets, appliance circuits, lighting) is designed and executed in-house.

---

**Q8: Do you offer a warranty?**

Yes. 2-year warranty on all cabinets, countertop, fixtures, plumbing and electrical work.
