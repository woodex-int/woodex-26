## SECTION 11 COPY — COST FACTORS

**H2:**

> What Affects the Cost of Office Interior Design?

**Intro paragraph:**

> Office interior design fees in Pakistan vary depending on the project scope, size, specification level and service package selected. The following factors directly influence the cost of your design project.

---

**Factor 1 — Project Size (sq. ft.)**

> Larger offices require more time for survey, planning, concept development, 3D visualization and technical documentation. Design fees are typically calculated on a per-square-foot or fixed-project basis depending on scope.

**Factor 2 — Design Package Scope**

> A design-only package covering concept and 3D renders costs less than a full design-and-documentation package that includes working drawings, BOQ and coordination drawings. Woodex Interior offers both.

**Factor 3 — Number of Zones and Rooms**

> An office with a reception, open-plan workstation area and one meeting room requires less design time than a project with executive cabins, multiple meeting rooms, a boardroom, server room, breakout zones and prayer facilities.

**Factor 4 — Specification Level**

> Economy, mid-range and premium material specifications carry very different costs. Woodex Interior presents options at each level so you can align design quality with your budget before committing.

**Factor 5 — 3D and Visualization Requirements**

> Standard 3D rendering is included in most packages. Additional deliverables — such as walkthrough animations, 360° virtual tours or multiple design option comparisons — carry additional fees.

**Factor 6 — Timeline and Urgency**

> Standard design timelines are planned around realistic delivery schedules. Compressed or urgent timelines may require additional resource allocation and are costed accordingly.
