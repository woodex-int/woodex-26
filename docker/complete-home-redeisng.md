## SECTION 2 — WHAT IS A COMPLETE HOME REDESIGN?

**H2:** What Is a Complete Home Redesign?

A complete home redesign is the renovation of every room in your existing home — kitchen, bedrooms, living room, bathrooms, and any other spaces — under one project, one team, and one contract. Unlike doing rooms individually (which means multiple contractors, different timelines and inconsistent finishes), a complete redesign ensures your entire home has a cohesive design language, consistent material quality, and a single point of coordination.

Woodex delivers complete home redesign from concept to handover. We survey every room, develop a unified design concept (including 3D visualization of every space), then execute the renovation room-by-room in a phased sequence — so your daily life is disrupted as little as possible. The result: a home that feels like it was designed as one — because it was.

**Why complete home redesign beats individual room renovation:**

- ✓ **One design language** — consistent materials, colours and style throughout the home
- ✓ **One project, one contract** — single point of contact, single payment schedule
- ✓ **Phased execution** — room-by-room, so you can continue living at home
- ✓ **Better pricing** — economies of scale across materials, labour and procurement
- ✓ **Faster overall** — one team manages all rooms simultaneously, no gaps between contractors
- ✓ **Cohesive result** — every room flows into the next, unified design vision

> **Difference: Complete Home Redesign vs Individual Room Renovation vs Turnkey**
>
> | | Complete Home Redesign | Individual Room Renovation | Turnkey |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | **Scope** | Every room, one project | One room at a time | Design + build + furnish, one contract |
> | **Design** | Unified concept for entire home | Room-by-room, may not be cohesive | Full service from concept to furnishing |
> | **Best for** | Homeowners wanting the whole home transformed | Specific room priorities | Want everything handled — design, build, furniture |
> | **Cost** | PKR 2M–7M+ | PKR 500K–2.5M per room | PKR 3M–10M+ |

## SECTION 6 — COMPLETE HOME REDESIGN PROCESS — 10 STEPS

**H2:** Complete Home Redesign Process — 10 Steps

| Step | Title | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 01 | **Whole-Home Survey** | We visit every room, photograph existing conditions, measure everything, understand your lifestyle and priorities. |
| 02 | **Brief & Scope** | You tell us what you want changed — room by room, priority by priority. We help define realistic scope and budget. |
| 03 | **Unified Concept Design** | One cohesive design concept — material palette, colour scheme, lighting style — applied across every room. 3D renders of key spaces. |
| 04 | **Room-by-Room Design** | Detailed design for each room — layouts, specifications, material selections. Two revision rounds included. |

| 08 | **Phase 2: Living Spaces** | Living room, bedrooms — flooring, walls, lighting, wardrobes, feature walls. |
| 09 | **Phase 3: Finishes** | Final paint, lighting fixtures, hardware, touch-ups, deep clean — every room completed. |
| 10 | **Snag & Handover** | Full home inspection — room by room. Defects corrected. Handover — home ready to enjoy. |

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Complete Home Redesign — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: How long does complete home redesign take?**

For a 5 Marla home: 6–10 weeks. For 10 Marla: 8–14 weeks. For larger homes: 10–16 weeks. Timelines are agreed before work begins.

---

**Q2: Can I live in my home during the redesign?**

Yes — we phase work room-by-room. You can typically continue living in the home while we renovate one area at a time.

---

**Q3: Is it cheaper to renovate all rooms at once?**

Yes. Economies of scale on materials, labour and procurement. One project also avoids repeated mobilisation costs that come with doing rooms separately.

---

**Q4: What is the difference between complete home redesign and turnkey?**

Complete home redesign = renovating your existing home. Turnkey = design + build + furnish under one contract. Turnkey includes furniture; redesign focuses on the built space.

---

**Q5: Can I choose different styles for different rooms?**

Yes — but we recommend a cohesive design language. Different colour palettes per room within a unified style creates flow. We help you achieve variety without chaos.

---

**Q6: Do I need interior design for a complete home redesign?**

Strongly recommended. A unified design concept ensures every room connects. We include design as part of the complete home redesign service.

---

**Q7: How do you handle rooms I don't want renovated?**

We can exclude specific rooms from the scope. The quotation is itemised by room — you choose what gets included.

---

**Q8: Do you offer a warranty?**

Yes. 2-year warranty on all construction, fixtures and finishes — every room.
