```
"I have an existing healthcare space that needs upgrading"

```

**Anti-cannibalisation rule:** Healthcare fit-out = "I'm building out a shell or new space into a clinic, diagnostic lab or healthcare facility." This page must never overlap with pharmacy fit-out (dispensary-specific), commercial renovation (existing space), or healthcare renovation (existing healthcare upgrade). Every paragraph must reinforce that healthcare fit-out = new healthcare facility build-out, and link to pharmacy fit-out for dispensary openings.

### The Healthcare Fit-Out Difference

| Factor | Healthcare Fit-Out | Pharmacy Fit-Out | Commercial Renovation |

| **Facility type** | Clinics, diagnostic labs, OPDs, wards | Dispensing pharmacy only | General commercial |

| **HVAC** | Negative-pressure zones for labs, isolation | Temperature-controlled for medication | Standard AC |

| **Lighting** | 5000K, shadow-free, task-critical for procedures | 4000K–5000K, glare-free for labels | Standard 4000K |

## SECTION 2 — WHAT HEALTHCARE FIT-OUT INCLUDES

**H2:** What Healthcare Fit-Out Includes

Healthcare fit-out is the build-out of a bare shell or empty unit into a fully operational clinic, diagnostic lab, outpatient department or healthcare facility — every element designed around clinical workflow, infection control, hygiene standards and patient experience. Unlike general commercial fit-out, every material, every surface and every system in a healthcare space must meet specific health and safety requirements.

Woodex has fitted out private clinics, diagnostic laboratories, hospital OPDs and healthcare facilities across Lahore and Pakistan. Our healthcare fit-out service covers the complete scope: reception and waiting area design, consultation room layout, diagnostic lab build-out (pathology, radiology), cleanable hygienic finishes (antibacterial paint, seamless vinyl flooring, floor-to-wall coving), 5000K shadow-free task lighting, negative-pressure HVAC for laboratory zones, plumbing and wet-area construction (sinks, handwashing stations, eyewash points), and medical supply storage systems.

Every Woodex healthcare fit-out is developed in AutoCAD and visualised in photorealistic 3D before construction begins — you see exactly how your clinic or lab will look and function before a single partition goes up. For multi-location healthcare operators, we develop a repeatable prototype that can be deployed across multiple facilities with consistent quality and faster timelines.

- ✓ Reception and waiting area design

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Healthcare Fit-Out — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: How much does it cost to fit out a clinic in Lahore?**

A basic clinic fit-out (1,000–1,500 sq ft) costs PKR 9M–12M. A mid-spec clinic with cleanable finishes and custom joinery costs PKR 12M–15M. A premium diagnostic lab costs PKR 15M–18M. All prices depend on facility type, specification and laboratory requirements. A free site survey provides an accurate fixed quote.

---

**Q2: How long does a healthcare fit-out take?**

A standard private clinic takes 5–7 weeks from brief to handover. A diagnostic lab takes 7–10 weeks due to lab-grade HVAC and plumbing requirements. Larger hospital departments and multi-room facilities may take longer. Timelines assume timely design approval and equipment procurement.

---

**Q3: What makes healthcare fit-out different from general commercial fit-out?**

Healthcare fit-out requires specific materials and systems: seamless vinyl flooring with floor-to-wall coving for infection control, antibacterial paint, 5000K shadow-free lighting for clinical tasks, negative-pressure HVAC for labs, and specialized plumbing for wet areas and handwashing. These are not optional upgrades — they are compliance requirements that must be designed in from the start.

---

**Q4: Can you build out a diagnostic laboratory?**

Yes. Woodex builds diagnostic labs — pathology, radiology, microbiology — with the specific requirements these facilities demand: controlled airflow and negative-pressure zones, seamless and chemical-resistant surfaces, lab-grade plumbing, precise lighting, equipment mounting, and storage. We design around your workflow and equipment list.

---

**Q5: What is included in a healthcare fit-out versus a renovation?**

A fit-out is a complete build-out of a bare shell into a functioning healthcare facility — reception, consultation rooms, lab, finishes, HVAC, plumbing, storage. A renovation is an upgrade of an existing occupied healthcare space — finishes refresh, layout adjustments, equipment replacement — usually without shutting down entirely. If your facility already exists and you want to upgrade it, see [Healthcare Renovation](/healthcare-renovation/). If you're starting from a shell, this is the right page.

---

**Q6: Can Woodex handle clinical joinery and medical storage?**

Yes. Our sister brand Woodex Furniture™ manufactures custom reception desks, consultation room joinery, lab cabinetry, medical supply storage and equipment housing — all custom-fitted to your clinical workflow and space dimensions. This eliminates third-party delays and ensures joinery matches the overall design and compliance requirements.

---

**Q7: Do you work on healthcare projects outside Lahore?**

Yes. While our studio is in Lahore (Model Town), we have delivered healthcare fit-outs across Punjab and in other cities including Islamabad, Karachi and Faisalabad. Site visits for projects outside Lahore are arranged separately. Remote design collaboration (3D renders, virtual meetings) keeps the process efficient.

---

**Q8: What if I'm opening a pharmacy — not a clinic or lab?**

Pharmacy fit-out has specific requirements — dispensing counter layout, controlled drug storage, DGDA/PBMC licensing compliance — that are different from general healthcare. See [Pharmacy Fit-Out](/pharmacy-fit-out/) for a service built around dispensary workflow and pharmacy licensing.
