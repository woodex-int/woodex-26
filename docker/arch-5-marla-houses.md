### 5 Marla — The Design Challenge

A 5 Marla plot (250 sq yards / ~2,250 sq ft) is the most common residential plot size in Lahore's newer developments. It is compact — and that creates specific design challenges:

- **Limited width** — typically 25–35 ft frontage; every inch of floor plan matters
- **Multi-storey necessity** — most 5 Marla houses are 2–3 storeys to get enough floor area
- **Light and ventilation** — neighbouring walls on three sides mean fewer windows; creative light-well and ventilation design is critical
- **Parking** — fitting a car (or two) on a narrow plot without wasting ground-floor space
- **Privacy** — bedrooms stacked close together; acoustic and visual separation between floors
- **Storage and utilities** — servant quarter, laundry, store room — all competing for the same small footprint

Woodex designs 5 Marla houses that solve these challenges — maximising space, light and liveability on a compact plot.

## SECTION 3 — DESIGNING A HOUSE ON A 5 MARLA PLOT

**H2:** Designing a House on a 5 Marla Plot

A 5 Marla plot — 250 square yards, roughly 25 to 35 feet wide — is the most common residential plot size in Lahore's newer developments. It is also one of the most challenging to design well. The constraints are real: limited frontage, walls on three sides from neighbouring properties, and the need to fit a functional family home into a compact footprint.

The answer is vertical design. Most 5 Marla houses are two or three storeys: ground floor for parking, drawing room and kitchen; first floor for bedrooms and family lounge; second floor (if allowed by bylaws) for additional bedrooms, terrace or servant quarter. The challenge is making each floor feel spacious, well-lit and liveable — not like a narrow corridor with rooms attached.

Woodex's 5 Marla designs solve these problems through intelligent floor planning. Light wells and internal courtyards bring natural light into rooms that have no external wall. Staircase placement is optimised to minimise wasted corridor space. Kitchen and bathroom layouts are designed for full functionality within tight dimensions. Parking is integrated into the ground floor without compromising the living areas.

**Challenge cards:**

| Challenge | How Woodex Solves It |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **Limited Width** | Optimised floor plans that maximise usable area on 25–35 ft frontage |
| **Multi-Storey Design** | Vertical layouts (2–3 storeys) planned as a coherent whole, not stacked floors |
| **Light & Ventilation** | Light wells, internal courtyards and strategic window placement |
| **Parking** | Ground-floor car space integrated without wasting living area |
| **Privacy** | Acoustic and visual separation between stacked bedrooms |

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** 5 Marla House Design — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What can I build on a 5 Marla plot?**

A 5 Marla plot (250 sq yd) typically supports a 2–3 storey house with 3–4 bedrooms, kitchen, drawing room, dining area, bathrooms, parking (1 car) and a servant quarter. The exact layout depends on bylaws, setback requirements and your family's specific needs. Our concept design shows you exactly what is possible on your plot.

---

**Q2: How much does a 5 Marla house design cost?**

Woodex charges PKR 200,000–400,000 for a complete 5 Marla house design — concept plans, 3D renders, working drawings, elevations and authority submission preparation. Front elevation only is PKR 50,000–150,000. Fees are lump sum — no hidden charges.

---

**Q3: Can I build 3 storeys on a 5 Marla plot?**

Whether 3 storeys are allowed depends on local bylaws and the specific location in Lahore. LDA regulations vary by zone and street width. We check bylaws during the initial site visit and design accordingly. If 3 storeys are permitted, we maximise the floor area within the allowed envelope.

---

**Q4: How long does the design take?**

A 5 Marla house design typically takes 4–6 weeks from the initial brief to final working drawings. Authority approval (LDA / CDA) is separate and typically takes 2–6 weeks.

---

**Q5: Do I need an architect for a 5 Marla house?**

You can build without one — but on a compact 5 Marla plot, every design decision matters more. A well-designed 5 Marla house maximises space, light and ventilation; a poor one feels cramped and dark. Our 5 Marla design service (PKR 200K–400K) ensures your home is well-designed from the start.

---

**Q6: Can I use my own contractor?**

Yes. Our working drawings are contractor-independent — they follow standard architectural conventions and include enough detail for any qualified construction team to execute. You are not obligated to use Woodex for construction.

---

**Q7: What about parking on a 5 Marla plot?**

Most 5 Marla designs integrate one car parking space into the ground floor — typically under a cantilevered upper floor or within the building footprint. The design ensures parking does not compromise the living areas. If you need space for two cars, we can explore options (tandem parking, street-facing carport) during the concept stage.

---

**Q8: Can Woodex design my 5 Marla house AND do the interior design?**

Yes. Woodex handles architecture, interior design, fit-out and furniture (via Woodex Furniture™) under one roof. The architect who designs your house works directly with the interior designer — no re-briefing, no gaps. For everything under one contract: [Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/).
