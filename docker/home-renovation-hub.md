## SECTION 2 — WHAT HOME RENOVATION COVERS

**H2:** What Home Renovation Covers

Home renovation is the upgrading of an existing home — remodelling a room, reconfiguring a layout, replacing finishes, modernising fixtures, or completely transforming a space. Unlike interior design (planning the layout first) or fit-out (building a new space from scratch), renovation is about working with what you already have and making it better.

Woodex delivers home renovation from assessment to handover. We visit your home, understand what you want changed, design the renovation (including 3D visualization so you see the result before we start), handle all construction, finishes and fixtures, and deliver a home that feels new — without the cost or disruption of moving.

Every home renovation Woodex delivers is planned around two priorities: your lifestyle (the renovation must work for how you actually live) and minimal disruption (we phase work to keep your daily routine as normal as possible).

**What home renovation covers:**

- ✓ Room remodelling (bedroom, kitchen, living room, bathroom, study)
- ✓ Layout reconfiguration (walls, doors, flow)
- ✓ Flooring replacement (tile, wood, vinyl, carpet)
- ✓ Painting and wall finishes
- ✓ Lighting upgrades
- ✓ Fixture and fitting replacement (cabinets, wardrobes, vanities)
- ✓ Plumbing and electrical upgrades
- ✓ Complete room redesigns

## SECTION 7 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Home Renovation — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What is the difference between home renovation and interior design?**

Interior design is the planning — layout, materials, colours, 3D visualization. Renovation is the actual construction — building the changes. You need interior design for the plan; you need renovation to build it. Many projects use both.

---

**Q2: Can I live in my home during renovation?**

Yes — we phase work room-by-room to minimize disruption. You can continue living in the house while we renovate one area at a time.

---

**Q3: How long does a single room renovation take?**

Most single-room renovations are completed in 2–4 weeks from design approval to handover. Kitchen and bathroom renovations tend toward 3–4 weeks due to plumbing work.

---

**Q4: How long does a complete home redesign take?**

For a 5 Marla home, typically 6–10 weeks. For 10 Marla, 8–14 weeks. Timelines are agreed before work begins.

---

**Q5: Do I need interior design before renovation?**

It is recommended but not required. If you already know what you want, we can proceed directly. If you are unsure about layout, materials or finishes, interior design first will save you money and ensure the result is right.

---

**Q6: What is the difference between renovation and refurbishment?**

Renovation is a broader scope — may include structural changes (walls, layout). Refurbishment is lighter — refreshing finishes, replacing fixtures, updating surfaces without major structural work.

---

**Q7: How much does a single room renovation cost?**

A single bedroom or living room renovation: PKR 500,000–1,500,000. Kitchens: PKR 800,000–2,500,000. Bathrooms: PKR 600,000–1,800,000. Exact pricing after a free survey.

---

**Q8: Do you offer a warranty on renovation work?**

Yes. All Woodex renovation work carries a 2-year warranty — construction, fixtures and finishes.
