## SECTION 2 — WHAT IS BEDROOM RENOVATION?

**H2:** What Is Bedroom Renovation?

Bedroom renovation is the upgrading of your bedroom — new wardrobes, replaced flooring, upgraded lighting, a feature headboard wall, refreshed paint, or a complete redesign of the room. Unlike full home renovation (which covers every room), bedroom renovation focuses on making one room exactly right.

Woodex delivers bedroom renovation from design to handover. We survey the room, design the renovation (including 3D visualization so you see the result before we start), build and finish the space, and hand over a bedroom that feels new — typically completed in 2–3 weeks.

**What bedroom renovation covers:**

- ✓ Custom wardrobes (built-in, walk-in, sliding-door)
- ✓ Flooring replacement (wood, tile, vinyl, carpet)
- ✓ Feature headboard wall (panelling, panelling, wallpaper, cladding)
- ✓ Lighting design (ambient, task, accent, smart controls)
- ✓ Painting and wall finishes
- ✓ Storage solutions (under-bed, shelving, nightstands)
- ✓ Electrical upgrades (outlets, switches, USB charging)
- ✓ Curtain and blind systems

## SECTION 5 — BEDROOM RENOVATION PROCESS — 10 STEPS

**H2:** Bedroom Renovation Process — 10 Steps

| Step | Title | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 01 | **Room Survey** | We measure the bedroom, photograph existing conditions, understand your lifestyle and storage needs. |
| 02 | **Brief & Scope** | You tell us what you want changed — wardrobe style, flooring, lighting, headboard. We define scope. |
| 03 | **Concept Design** | Layout, wardrobe design, material palette, lighting plan, 3D renders of the finished bedroom. |
| 04 | **Design Refinement** | Two revision rounds — adjust materials, wardrobe configuration, lighting positions. |
| 05 | **Costing & Agreement** | Fixed-price quotation — itemised. No surprises. Agreement signed. |

| 07 | **Preparation** | Existing wardrobe removed (if applicable), flooring prepared, walls patched and primed. |
| 08 | **Construction** | Flooring installed, wardrobe fitted, electrical work, wall finishes applied. |
| 09 | **Finishes & Fixtures** | Paint, lighting installed, hardware fitted, headboard wall completed, deep clean. |
| 10 | **Snag & Handover** | Full inspection, corrections, handover — bedroom ready to enjoy. |

## SECTION 7 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Bedroom Renovation — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: How long does bedroom renovation take?**

Most bedroom renovations are completed in 2–3 weeks from design approval to handover. Wardrobe-only: 1–2 weeks. Full bedroom renovation: 2–3 weeks.

---

**Q2: Do you manufacture wardrobes in-house?**

Yes — through sister brand WOODEX FURNITURE™. Custom wardrobes are designed around your clothing and accessories, then manufactured and installed by our team.

---

**Q3: Can I use the bedroom during renovation?**

For wardrobe-only work, partially yes. For full bedroom renovation (flooring, walls, lighting), it is better to vacate the room for 2–3 weeks.

---

**Q4: How much does bedroom renovation cost?**

Paint + lighting: PKR 150K–400K. Full renovation with wardrobe: PKR 500K–1.5M. Exact pricing after free survey.

---

**Q5: What flooring is best for bedrooms?**

Engineered wood or vinyl plank are the most popular — warm underfoot, quiet, easy to maintain. Tile is durable but cold. Carpet is soft but harder to clean. We help you choose based on your preferences and budget.

---

**Q6: Can you add a walk-in wardrobe to my bedroom?**

Yes — if space allows. We can reconfigure the layout to create a walk-in wardrobe area, or design a large built-in wardrobe that maximises storage.

---

**Q7: Do you handle lighting design?**

Yes. We design layered lighting — ambient (general), task (reading), accent (headboard wall, shelves) — with dimmer controls for different moods.

---

**Q8: Do you offer a warranty?**

Yes. 2-year warranty on all construction, wardrobes, fixtures and finishes.
