## FAQ (full set for `/faq`)

### Process & Cost
**Q: How does the Cost-Lock work?**
A: The total project cost is locked at Gate 4 — after design, visualization, and detailed planning (BOQ + budget). If the cost doesn't work for you, you walk away with every design file. The build is optional. Your design is always yours.

**Q: What happens if the budget changes mid-build?**
A: The Cost-Lock protects you from surprise. Any change order is documented and approved by you before work continues — no silent costs, no assumptions.

**Q: How long does a full project take?**
A: A standard residential project (design → deliver) typically takes 12–20 weeks depending on scope and site conditions. The 7-Gate timeline is shared at consultation.

**Q: Do I need to hire a separate contractor and designer?**
A: No. Woodex is one accountable partner — design + construction + furniture + 3D, all under one roof. One decision-maker. One standard.

**Q: Can I start with just 3D renders and decide later?**
A: Yes. The Stop-Gate privilege lets you approve your space visually before any construction begins. If the vision or numbers change after Gate 3, you own every 3D file. Construction is entirely optional.

### Design
**Q: What services do you offer?**
A: Four integrated services: Architectural Design, Interior Design, Construction Fit-Outs, and Furniture Design & Manufacturing. Every project uses all four wings as needed — one partner, from concept to completion.

**Q: What is the design process like?**
A: We follow the 7-Gate Process — discover, design, visualize, plan (cost lock), build, install, deliver. You are involved at every decision point, and you approve every surface before construction.

**Q: Do you design 3D renders before starting work?**
A: Yes. The 3D Studio creates photo-realistic renders, walkthroughs, and material boards so you approve a visual — not a guess — before construction begins.

### Construction
**Q: What does "turnkey fit-out" mean?**
A: Turnkey means we handle everything — design, construction, furniture, installation, and final delivery. You receive a finished space, not a list of tasks.

**Q: Do you work outside Lahore?**
A: Yes. We deliver projects across the Rawalpindi to Multan corridor, including Karachi and Islamabad. `[CONFIRM]` Our studios are in Lahore (head office), Karachi, and Islamabad.

### Furniture
**Q: Do you build custom furniture?**
A: Yes. Woodex Furniture™ is our in-house manufacturing arm. We build bespoke pieces to your approved designs — the same quality standard as the room itself.

**Q: Can I just buy furniture without full interior design?**
A: Yes. Woodex Furniture™ accepts individual commissions for custom pieces — wardrobes, cabinetry, built-ins — with or without full studio services.
