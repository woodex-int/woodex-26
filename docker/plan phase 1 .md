> Interior design only, fit-out, renovation and full turnkey delivery each have different scope, materials and labour requirements.

**Factor 2 — Covered Area**

> The total square footage of the space directly affects material quantities, labour time and overall project cost.

**Factor 3 — Specification Level**

> A brand-new shell space requires different preparation than an occupied renovation requiring demolition, protection and phased execution.

**Factor 5 — Custom Furniture and Joinery**

> Custom-manufactured furniture, reception counters, kitchens and wardrobes are costed separately from the main fit-out scope.

**Factor 6 — MEP Requirements**
