# PART 1 — PAGE STRATEGY & SEO SPECIFICATIONS

## 1.1 Page Purpose

| Job | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **SEO** | Rank for "office interior design Lahore", "corporate office fit-out Lahore" — the intent: a business owner, facilities manager, or corporate decision-maker seeking to design, renovate or fit-out a professional office |
| **Education** | Help the visitor understand what Woodex does for corporate offices — design, renovation, fit-out, turnkey — and which service fits their need |
| **Conversion** | Route the visitor to the relevant service page (interior design, renovation, fit-out or turnkey) and into a consultation |

### Critical Keyword Differentiation

```
/sectors/corporate-offices/     → SECTOR PAGE (overview)
"corporate office interior design Lahore" (broad intent)

/office-interior-design/        → SERVICE PAGE (interior design)
"I need the office layout planned"

/office-renovation/             → SERVICE PAGE (renovation)
"I have an existing office that needs upgrading"

/office-fit-out/                → SERVICE PAGE (fit-out)
"I have a shell space — I need it built out"

/turnkey-design-build/          → SERVICE PAGE (turnkey)
"I want one team for everything"
```

**Anti-cannibalisation rule:** The sector page is an **overview** — it introduces Woodex's capabilities for corporate offices and routes the visitor to the specific service they need. It does NOT target service-specific keywords. Every paragraph must end with a link to the appropriate service page.

## SECTION 6 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Corporate Office — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What does Woodex do for corporate offices?**

Woodex handles interior design, renovation, fit-out and turnkey design & build for corporate offices in Lahore — from layout planning through construction, furnishing and handover.

---

**Q2: How much does corporate office interior design cost in Lahore?**

Interior design fees start from PKR 250,000 for small offices and scale with area and complexity. Renovation and fit-out are quoted separately after a free site survey.

---

**Q3: How long does a corporate office fit-out take?**

Most office fit-outs are completed in 4–8 weeks from design approval to handover. Larger projects or multi-floor offices may take 8–12 weeks.

---

**Q4: Can Woodex handle a multi-floor office fit-out?**

Yes. Woodex has experience with single-floor offices and multi-floor corporate headquarters. We manage the project across floors with phased delivery to minimize disruption.

---

**Q5: Do you handle IT infrastructure and server rooms?**

We build the physical space for IT — raised flooring, cable management, power distribution, cooling. Actual IT equipment and network installation are your responsibility or handled by your IT team.

---

**Q6: Can we continue working during renovation or fit-out?**

For renovation of an occupied office, we can phase the work floor-by-floor or zone-by-zone to minimize disruption. For shell fit-outs, the space is typically unoccupied.

---

**Q7: What is the difference between office renovation and office fit-out?**

Renovation is for existing offices that need upgrading — new finishes, reconfigured layout. Fit-out is for shell/empty spaces — building the office from scratch. [Office Renovation](/renovation/) | [Office Fit-Out](/fit-out/)

---

**Q8: Do you offer turnkey solutions for corporate offices?**

Yes. [Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/) — one team, one contract, from design to handover. One team, one point of contact.
