### SECTION 8 — COST FACTORS

**H2:**

> What Affects the Cost of a Corporate Office Interior in Lahore?

**Factor 1 — Total Floor Area**

> The total square footage of the office directly determines material quantities, trade hours and project management scope.

**Factor 2 — Fit-Out Type**

> Design only, fit-out only or complete turnkey — each carries a different cost structure. A turnkey contract covering design through handover carries the full project cost in one agreement.

**Factor 3 — Specification Level**

> Economy corporate, mid-range professional and premium executive specification levels carry significantly different material and installation costs.

**Factor 4 — Number of Enclosed Rooms and Zones**

> Executive cabins, boardrooms, meeting rooms, server rooms, prayer facilities and specialty zones each add to the cost relative to a simple open-plan floor.

**Factor 5 — Executive Suite Requirements**

> Premium executive areas — private offices, executive bathrooms, dedicated meeting suites and VIP reception areas — carry a higher specification cost than standard workstation areas.

**Factor 6 — MEP Scope**

> Electrical panel upgrades, HVAC systems, UPS provisions and data infrastructure coordination add to the MEP scope beyond a standard office configuration.

**Factor 7 — Furniture and Custom Joinery**

> Reception counters, custom workstations, meeting tables, executive furniture and branded joinery are costed separately from the structural fit-out scope.

### SECTION 8 — COST FACTORS

**H2:**

> What Affects the Cost of a Software House Interior in Lahore?

**Factor 1 — Total Floor Area**

> Engineering teams require generous workstation spacing for ergonomic comfort and monitor configurations. Area per person is typically higher in a software house than in a standard office — and total area drives total project cost.

**Factor 2 — Zone Complexity**

> A floor with open-plan workstations only costs less than a floor with multiple collaboration zones, video call pods, a client meeting room, a server room, a pantry and a reception area.

**Factor 3 — Server Room Provisions**

> Server room requirements — precision cooling, dedicated UPS circuits, raised flooring, fire suppression coordination and access control — are a significant separate cost item in software house projects.

**Factor 4 — Ergonomic Furniture Specification**

> Premium ergonomic chairs, height-adjustable workstations, monitor arms and acoustic panels carry a higher specification cost than standard commercial furniture. Specification level is the client's choice — and is confirmed in the BOQ before the contract is signed.

**Factor 5 — Acoustic Treatment**

> Open-plan software development floors require active acoustic management — ceiling acoustic panels, wall absorption, privacy screens and sound-masking provisions — which add to the fit-out cost but are critical to developer productivity.

**Factor 6 — Technology Integration**

> Video conferencing systems, screen projection, integrated AV and meeting room technology are typically coordinated by Woodex Interior with specialist AV suppliers — adding to the project scope and cost.

### SECTION 8 — COST FACTORS

**H2:**

> What Affects the Cost of a Residential Interior Project in Lahore?

**Factor 1 — Property Size and Number of Rooms**

> Total covered area and the number of rooms being designed or renovated directly determines the total project cost.

**Factor 2 — Project Type**

> Interior design only — for a new home with existing finishes — costs less than a complete renovation covering all surfaces, MEP modification, kitchen and bathrooms.

**Factor 3 — Specification Level**

> Economy, mid-range and premium specification levels carry substantially different costs at every stage — flooring, ceiling, wall finishes, joinery and sanitary ware selection all affect the total budget.

**Factor 4 — Kitchen Scope**

> Kitchen renovation is the single highest-cost room in most residential projects — because it combines joinery, stone countertops, plumbing, electrical, tiling and appliance provisions in a confined space.

**Factor 5 — Bathroom Renovation Scope**

> Each bathroom being renovated adds to the project cost — waterproofing, sanitary ware, tiling, plumbing and electrical are all required per bathroom.

**Factor 6 — Custom Joinery Extent**

> Custom wardrobes, media walls, bespoke storage and kitchen cabinetry are separately costed — with material and finish options at multiple price points through Woodex Furniture™.

**Factor 7 — MEP Modification Required**

> Civil repair, electrical panel upgrade, plumbing rerouting and HVAC modification add to the project cost where existing systems require upgrading.
