# PART 1 — PAGE STRATEGY & SEO SPECIFICATIONS

## 1.1 Page Purpose

This page has **three jobs** running simultaneously:

| Job | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **SEO** | Rank for "restaurant renovation Lahore" and related searches — distinct from Commercial Renovation (property-level) and Retail Renovation (trading shops/showrooms) |
| **Education** | Help restaurant, café and food-court owners understand what hospitality renovation covers — dining-area redesign, kitchen-ventilation coordination, trade-preserving scheduling and hospitality-grade durable finishes |
| **Conversion** | Move the visitor from reading to requesting a venue assessment or consultation |

### Critical Keyword Differentiation

```
/commercial-renovation/      → PROPERTY-LEVEL RENOVATION (landlords, lobbies, common areas)
"I own a building — need to upgrade it"

/retail-renovation/          → TRADING / CUSTOMER-FACING RETAIL SPACES
"I have a shop or showroom I need to renovate while trading"

/restaurant-renovation/      → HOSPITALITY VENUES — DINING, KITCHEN, GUEST EXPERIENCE
"I have a restaurant or café I need to modernize"
```

Every paragraph on this page must reinforce that this service is for **restaurants, cafés and hospitality venues** — not property-level commercial work (see Commercial Renovation) or retail shops (see Retail Renovation).

### The Restaurant Renovation Differentiators

This page has **three unique selling points** that no other renovation page shares:

1. **Closed-day / out-of-hours phasing** — restaurants can lose an entire evening's revenue if closed during peak hours. Phasing is scheduled around trade hours — closed days, overnight or split-service — not just zone-by-zone.
2. **Kitchen-ventilation coordination** — grease extraction, gas lines, exhaust and hood systems are coordinated alongside interior works. This is a specialized MEP scope that most renovation contractors do not handle.
3. **Hospitality-grade durability** — restaurant finishes must withstand grease, heat, moisture, heavy foot traffic and daily cleaning chemicals. Material specification is driven by hospitality performance, not just appearance.

## SECTION 2 COPY — WHAT IS RESTAURANT RENOVATION

**H2:**

> What Is Restaurant Renovation?

**Paragraph 1:**

> Restaurant renovation is the process of upgrading, remodeling and modernizing an existing, operating restaurant, café or hospitality venue — without closing for an extended period. It improves how the space looks, how guests experience the venue, how seating and flow work and how effectively the environment supports your brand, menu and service style. Renovation covers everything from a cosmetic refresh (paint, lighting, seating) to a full venue remodel (new layout, finishes, kitchen-adjacent coordination, custom seating and brand integration).

**Paragraph 2:**

> Restaurant renovation is fundamentally different from office or retail renovation because the space generates revenue during every service. Closing for weeks means lost bookings, lost revenue and lost guest loyalty. Woodex Interior structures restaurant renovation around your trading schedule — closed days, overnight work or split-service phasing — so your venue keeps welcoming guests while the renovation progresses. This phasing strategy is planned and documented before the contract is signed.

**Paragraph 3:**

> Beyond trade preservation, restaurant renovation requires hospitality-specific expertise: guest-flow design that moves diners from entrance to table to exit naturally, seating-capacity planning that maximizes covers without crowding, kitchen-ventilation coordination that handles grease extraction, gas and exhaust alongside interior works, and material specification that withstands the daily assault of grease, heat, moisture and chemical cleaning. Woodex Interior brings all of this together under one contract — from venue survey through custom seating, finishes and formal handover.

**Step 04 — Design and 3D Visualization:**

> Space plans, material palettes and 3D renders of the renovated venue are presented for approval. You see the finished restaurant or café — seating, lighting, finishes, ambiance — before any physical work begins.

**Step 05 — BOQ and Itemized Cost Plan:**

> A complete, zone-by-zone, trade-by-trade cost plan is prepared — every item costed individually. Seating, lighting, kitchen-adjacent and brand-graphic costs are separated for clarity. No lump sums.

**Step 06 — Contract and Programme (trade-aligned):**

> A formal contract and construction programme are issued — with milestones, payment schedule and phased execution plan that confirms which zones are affected when, and which service hours are preserved. Closed-day and overnight shifts are scheduled where required.

**Step 08 — MEP First-Fix:**

## SECTION 8 COPY — COST FACTORS

**H2:**

> What Affects the Cost of Restaurant Renovation in Lahore?

**Intro:**

> Restaurant renovation costs vary based on venue size, kitchen-adjacent scope, seating complexity and the phasing required to protect trading hours. The following are the main factors Woodex Interior evaluates when preparing your BOQ.

**Factor 1 — Dining Area vs Kitchen Scope:**

> A dining-area-only renovation — finishes, seating, lighting — is materially less costly than a project that includes kitchen-adjacent works — grease extraction, exhaust, gas, service finishes. The kitchen-adjacent scope is often the most variable cost element.

**Factor 2 — Seating-Capacity Redesign:**

> Changing seating capacity — adding covers, reconfiguring layout or introducing booths and banquettes — requires structural, electrical and finishing coordination. Custom booths and banquettes carry a higher cost than standard chair-and-table layouts but deliver a significant ambiance and capacity upgrade.

**Factor 3 — Material Durability Specification:**

> Hospitality-grade materials — grease-resistant panels, commercial vinyl or porcelain flooring, moisture-resistant ceilings — carry a specification premium over standard residential finishes. The premium is justified by durability and maintainability in a hospitality environment.

**Factor 4 — Lighting and Ambience Design:**

> Feature and ambient lighting — pendants, track systems, LED strip, dimming circuits — is a cost driver that directly impacts guest experience and atmosphere. Lighting design is specified per zone: dining, bar, entrance, private areas.

**Factor 5 — Ventilation and Gas Requirements:**

> Grease extraction, exhaust ventilation and gas-line coordination are significant and variable cost elements. Existing systems may need upgrading, extending or replacing. These are coordinated during the MEP first-fix and costed as separate BOQ items.

**Factor 6 — Trade-Preserving Phasing vs Full Closure:**

> Phased renovation that preserves trading hours is more expensive than full-venue closure — overnight shifts, zone protection, temporary screens and extended programme duration all add cost. For most venues, the revenue preserved far outweighs the incremental cost.

**Note box:**

> Restaurant renovation is an investment in guest experience, ambiance and revenue — not just a visual refresh. A well-renovated venue with optimized layout, lighting, seating and brand expression typically delivers measurable uplift in guest satisfaction, dwell time and spend-per-head. Woodex prepares a detailed, itemized BOQ before any work begins — every item costed individually.
