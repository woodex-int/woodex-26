**Anti-cannibalisation rule:** Restaurant fit-out = "I'm building out a shell or new space into a restaurant or café." This page must never overlap with retail fit-out (shop/showroom), restaurant renovation (existing F&B space), or restaurant architecture (building design). Every paragraph must reinforce that restaurant fit-out = new F&B build-out, and link to restaurant renovation for existing operations and restaurant architecture for a new building.

## SECTION 2 — WHAT RESTAURANT FIT-OUT INCLUDES

**H2:** What Restaurant & Café Fit-Out Includes

Restaurant fit-out is the build-out of a bare shell or empty unit into a fully operational restaurant or café — every element designed around kitchen efficiency, customer experience, durability and health compliance. Unlike general commercial fit-out, a restaurant works harder than almost any other space: it handles heat, steam, grease, heavy traffic and daily deep cleaning. Every material and every layout decision must be chosen accordingly.

Woodex has fitted out cafés, fine-dining restaurants, QSR outlets and cloud kitchens across Lahore and Pakistan. Our restaurant fit-out service covers the complete scope: kitchen layout and equipment coordination (with chef consultations), dining-area design (seating, bar, lounge), ambient/task/decorative lighting design, anti-slip durable flooring, acoustic and fire-rated ceilings, custom joinery (bar counters, display units, service stations), ventilation and exhaust (kitchen hoods, ducting, fresh-air intake), and plumbing and wet-area construction.

Every Woodex restaurant fit-out is developed in AutoCAD and visualised in photorealistic 3D before construction begins — you see exactly how your dining room will feel and how your kitchen will flow before a single partition goes up. For chains, we develop a repeatable prototype that can be deployed across multiple locations with consistent quality and faster timelines.

**What's included:**

- ✓ Kitchen layout and equipment coordination (with chef consultations)
- ✓ Dining-area design — seating, bar, lounge
- ✓ Ambient, task and decorative lighting design
- ✓ Anti-slip, easy-to-clean flooring
- ✓ Acoustic and fire-rated ceilings
- ✓ Custom joinery — bar counters, display units, service stations
- ✓ Ventilation and exhaust — kitchen hoods, ducting, fresh-air intake
- ✓ Plumbing and wet-area construction

## SECTION 4 — WHAT DOES RESTAURANT FIT-OUT COST IN LAHORE?

**H2:** What Does Restaurant Fit-Out Cost in Lahore?

Restaurant fit-out costs in Lahore depend on the type of establishment, the specification level, the size of the space, and the complexity of the kitchen and ventilation systems. Woodex provides a fixed lump-sum quote after an initial site survey — no hidden charges, no cost surprises.

## SECTION 5 — OUR RESTAURANT FIT-OUT PROCESS — 6 STEPS

**H2:** Our Restaurant Fit-Out Process — 6 Steps

| Step | Title | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 01 | **Site Survey & Chef Brief** | We visit your site, measure the space, check structural conditions and utilities. We discuss your concept, menu and kitchen workflow with you — and where possible, your chef or kitchen consultant. We map the kitchen workflow before design begins. |
| 02 | **Kitchen + Dining Design & 3D** | We prepare kitchen layouts and dining-area floor plans, and photorealistic 3D renders — seating, bar, lighting, materials. The kitchen is designed around your menu and equipment list. You approve before we proceed. Two rounds of revisions included. |
| 03 | **PFA Compliance Check** | We review your design against Punjab Food Authority (PFA) standards — ventilation and exhaust, drainage and wet areas, food-safe finishes, hand-washing stations, waste handling — and make adjustments before construction. |
| 04 | **Construction & MEP** | Partitions, ceilings, flooring, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, kitchen ducting and hoods — all executed by Woodex site teams under project management. Kitchen-specific MEP (exhaust, ventilation, grease traps, water supply) coordinated from design stage. |
| 05 | **Equipment & Finishing** | Kitchen equipment installation coordination, final finishes — anti-slip flooring, counter and bar installation, lighting focus, acoustic panels, branding and signage. |
| 06 | **Final Handover & Licensing Support** | Complete snagging walkthrough, corrections, and handover. We support your PFA inspection readiness — clean, compliant, and ready to open. |

**Timeline:** A standard café (1,000–1,500 sq ft) takes 6–8 weeks from brief to handover. A full restaurant (1,500–3,000 sq ft) takes 8–12 weeks. New-build kitchen installations and large chain rollouts may take longer. Timelines assume timely design approval and equipment procurement.

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Restaurant & Café Fit-Out — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: How much does it cost to fit out a restaurant in Lahore?**

A basic café fit-out (1,000–1,500 sq ft) costs PKR 14M–18M. A mid-spec restaurant costs PKR 18M–24M. A premium restaurant with fine-dining interiors costs PKR 24M–32M. All prices operate on a per-sq-ft basis and depend on size, specification and kitchen complexity. A free site survey provides an accurate fixed quote.

---

**Q2: How long does a restaurant fit-out take?**

A standard café takes 6–8 weeks from brief to handover. A full restaurant takes 8–12 weeks. New-build kitchen installations and large chain rollouts take longer. Timelines assume timely design approval, kitchen equipment procurement and utility connections.

---

**Q3: Do you coordinate with our chef or kitchen consultant?**

Yes. Our kitchen layouts are designed around your menu and equipment list, in consultation with your chef or kitchen consultant where available. We map the full prep → cooking → plating → service workflow before design begins, so the kitchen works when you open.

---

**Q4: Do you handle Punjab Food Authority (PFA) compliance?**

Yes. Woodex designs and builds restaurants to meet Punjab Food Authority standards — ventilation and exhaust, drainage and wet areas, food-safe finishes, hand-washing stations and waste handling. We review compliance at the design stage and support your PFA inspection readiness at handover.

---

**Q5: Can you fit out multiple locations for an F&B chain?**

Yes. We develop a repeatable restaurant prototype — kitchen layout, dining design, finishes, lighting — that can be deployed across multiple locations with consistent quality and faster timelines. The prototype is designed once, approved once, and executed everywhere.

---

**Q6: Can Woodex handle bar counters, display joinery and furniture?**

Yes. Our sister brand Woodex Furniture™ manufactures custom bar counters, display units, service stations, dining furniture and seating joinery — all custom-fitted to your F&B concept. This eliminates third-party delays and ensures joinery matches the overall design.

---

**Q7: Do you work on restaurants outside Lahore?**

Yes. While our studio is in Lahore (Model Town), we have delivered restaurant and café fit-outs across Punjab and in other cities including Islamabad, Karachi and Faisalabad. Site visits for projects outside Lahore are arranged separately. Remote design collaboration keeps the process efficient.

---

**Q8: What if my project is a cloud kitchen, not a dining restaurant?**

That's a fit-out Woodex does well. Cloud kitchens are kitchen-first: no dining room, maximum efficiency. We fit out production spaces — prep lines, cooking stations, packaging areas, storage, ventilation and insulation — for delivery operators and investors. Tell us your menu and order volume and we will design around them.
