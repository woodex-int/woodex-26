# PART 1 — PAGE STRATEGY & SEO SPECIFICATIONS

## 1.1 Page Purpose

This page has a different primary job than the Office Interior Design page.

| Page                   | Primary visitor intent                                          |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Office Interior Design | "I need someone to plan and design my office"                   |
| **Office Fit-Out**     | **"I need someone to physically build and complete my office"** |

The Office Fit-Out visitor is typically further along in their buying journey. They may already have drawings or a design. They want to know who will execute the work, how it is managed, what is included and what the process looks like from site mobilization to handover.

This page must answer all of those questions — and convert.

## SECTION 7 COPY — THE FIT-OUT PROCESS

**H2:**

> The Office Fit-Out Process — Step by Step

**Subtext:**

> Every Woodex Interior office fit-out project follows the same structured 14-step delivery process — transparent, documented and managed from first site visit to signed handover.

---

**Step 01 — Site Visit and Brief**

> Our project team visits your site, takes measurements and conducts a thorough condition review. We discuss your requirements, timeline, specification level and budget expectations.

**Step 02 — BOQ and Cost Planning**

> A detailed bill of quantities is prepared based on the confirmed design or agreed scope. You receive an itemized cost breakdown — room by room, trade by trade — before any work begins.

**Step 03 — Design Approval**

> Where the design phase is included, all space plans, 3D renders and technical drawings are reviewed and approved by you before procurement or site mobilization begins.

**Step 04 — Contract and Programme**

> A formal project contract and construction programme are issued — confirming scope, milestones, payment schedule and project completion date.

**Step 05 — Procurement and Lead-Time Management**

> All materials, fittings, joinery and furniture items are procured in accordance with the BOQ and construction programme. Lead times are tracked to prevent site delays.

**Step 06 — Site Mobilization**

> The site is set up for execution — hoarding, protection of existing areas, contractor access, site safety measures and material storage are all organized before works begin.

**Step 07 — Structural and Civil Works**

> Demolition of existing elements where required, civil repair, surface preparation, block or stud-wall construction and structural modifications are completed as the first trade on site.

**Step 08 — MEP First-Fix**

> Electrical conduit, HVAC ductwork, plumbing rough-in and data conduit are installed inside the structure before ceilings and walls are closed.

**Step 09 — Partitions and Ceilings**

> Drywall partitions, glass partition systems and false-ceiling frameworks are installed and prepared for finishing.

**Step 10 — Flooring and Wall Finishes**

> Flooring is laid — tiles, vinyl, wood or epoxy — and wall finishes are applied, including paint, panels, cladding or wallpaper as specified in the design.

**Step 11 — MEP Second-Fix**

> Electrical fittings, light fixtures, air conditioning units, grilles, socket outlets, switches and plumbing fixtures are installed and tested.

**Step 12 — Joinery and Furniture Installation**

> Custom joinery — reception counters, storage, cabinets and feature elements — is installed, followed by workstations, chairs, tables and all furniture items.

**Step 13 — Snagging and Quality Inspection**

> A thorough snagging inspection is conducted. All defects, unfinished items and quality issues are identified, documented and resolved before any handover conversation begins.

**Step 14 — Final Cleaning and Handover**

> The completed space is deep-cleaned, final photographs are taken, handover documentation is prepared and the site is formally handed over to the client.

## SECTION 10 COPY — COST FACTORS

**H2:**

> What Affects the Cost of Office Fit-Out in Lahore?

**Intro paragraph:**

> Office fit-out costs in Pakistan are influenced by multiple variables — from the size and condition of the space to the specification level, number of rooms and MEP complexity. The following factors directly determine what your fit-out will cost before Woodex Interior prepares your BOQ.

---

**Factor 1 — Total Covered Area (sq. ft.)**

> The total square footage of the space is the primary driver of fit-out cost. Larger spaces require more materials, more trades and more project management time. Cost is typically calculated per square foot — but the rate varies based on scope and specification.

**Factor 2 — Fit-Out Type: Cat A, Cat B or Turnkey**

> A bare-shell space being converted to Cat A costs less per square foot than a fully branded Cat B environment. A turnkey design-and-build contract includes design fees, procurement management and project coordination in addition to fit-out execution.

**Factor 3 — Specification Level**

> Economy, mid-range and premium specification levels carry very different material and labour costs. The choice of flooring, ceiling system, glass partition type, lighting brand and joinery finish has a significant impact on the overall project budget.

**Factor 4 — Condition of Existing Space**

> A brand-new shell space requires different preparation than an occupied office undergoing renovation. Strip-out, demolition, civil repair and protection of existing elements all add to the scope and cost.

**Factor 5 — Number of Rooms, Zones and Partitions**

> An open-plan office with a reception and one meeting room costs significantly less than a project with multiple private cabins, a boardroom, a server room, prayer facilities, a pantry, multiple washrooms and dedicated collaboration zones.

**Factor 6 — MEP Scope and Complexity**

> Electrical, lighting, HVAC and plumbing scope varies significantly by sector and space type. Healthcare, laboratory and food-and-beverage fit-outs typically require more intensive MEP coordination and specialist systems than standard office environments.

**Factor 7 — Furniture and Joinery Requirements**

> Custom-manufactured joinery — reception counters, storage walls, executive cabinets and branded millwork — is a separate cost item from the structural fit-out scope. Furniture specification — from economy workstations to imported executive systems — also varies significantly in cost.

---

**Note box:**

> Woodex Interior prepares a detailed, itemized BOQ before any work begins. You will not receive a lump-sum figure with no breakdown. Every line item is costed separately so you can make informed decisions about scope and specification before the contract is signed.
