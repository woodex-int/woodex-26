# PART 1 — PAGE STRATEGY & SEO SPECIFICATIONS

## 1.1 Page Purpose

This page has **three jobs** running simultaneously:

| Job | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **SEO** | Rank for "3D interior rendering Pakistan", "3D visualization Lahore" and related searches — the high-margin, architect-and-developer-facing market |
| **Education** | Show the full range of 3D visualization Woodex produces — photorealistic renders, animations, walkthroughs, virtual tours, sciography and visualization consultancy — and who each serves |
| **Conversion** | Convert architects, developers and homeowners into visualization enquiries — and feed design-forward clients into Woodex mainline services |

### Critical Keyword Differentiation

```
/3d-visualization/           → 3D STUDIO HUB (all 6 sub-services)
"I need a render, animation, walkthrough, virtual tour or sciography study"

/3d-studio/                  → (alias, redirects to /3d-visualization/)

/office-interior-design/     → DESIGN SERVICE (planning)
"I want my space designed — 3D is included in the design"

/residential-interior-design/ + sub-pages → DESIGN SERVICES
"I want my home designed — renders are part of the package"
```

Every paragraph must reinforce that Woodex 3D Studio is a **standalone, buyable service** offered also to **external clients** — architects, interior designers, and developers who need visualization but do not necessarily need Woodex to build. In-house 3ds Max and AutoCAD expertise, fast turnaround (2–5 days for basic renders), and marketing-ready outputs are signature differentiators.

### The 3D Studio Differentiators

This page has **five unique selling points**:

1. **In-house 3ds Max and AutoCAD expertise** — not outsourced; the same studio that visualizes every Woodex project.
2. **Fast turnaround** — 2–5 days for basic renders, with confirmed delivery dates on every brief.
3. **Marketing-ready outputs** — renders, animations and virtual tours built for client approvals, pre-sales and marketing use.
4. **Serves external clients** — architects, interior designers and developers commission Woodex 3D Studio even when Woodex is not executing their build.
5. **Full-service visualization range** — photorealistic renders, animations, 360° virtual tours, sciography shadow studies and visualization consultancy under one roof.
