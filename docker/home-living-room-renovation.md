## SECTION 2 — WHAT IS LIVING ROOM RENOVATION?

**H2:** What Is Living Room Renovation?

Living room renovation is the upgrading of your living or drawing room — new flooring, a feature wall, updated ceiling design, modern lighting, a media unit, refreshed paint, or a complete redesign of the space. Unlike full home renovation (which covers every room), living room renovation focuses on the room where your family spends the most time and guests form their first impression.

Woodex delivers living room renovation from design to handover. We survey the room, design the renovation (including 3D visualization so you see the result before we start), build and finish the space, and hand over a living room that is the centrepiece of your home — typically completed in 3–4 weeks.

**What living room renovation covers:**

- ✓ Feature wall design (panelling, cladding, wallpaper, stone, LED-backlit)
- ✓ Flooring replacement (marble, tile, wood, vinyl)
- ✓ Ceiling design (false ceiling, tray ceiling, LED cove lighting)
- ✓ Lighting design (chandeliers, recessed, accent, smart controls)
- ✓ Media / TV wall unit (built-in, floating, backlit)
- ✓ Painting and wall finishes
- ✓ Curtains, drapes and window treatments
- ✓ Electrical upgrades (outlets, switches, smart home wiring)

## SECTION 5 — LIVING ROOM RENOVATION PROCESS — 10 STEPS

**H2:** Living Room Renovation Process — 10 Steps

| Step | Title | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 01 | **Room Survey** | We measure the living room, photograph existing conditions, understand your style preferences and how you use the space. |
| 02 | **Brief & Scope** | You tell us what you want changed — feature wall, flooring, ceiling, lighting, media unit. We define scope. |
| 03 | **Concept Design** | Layout, material palette, colour scheme, feature wall options, 3D renders of the finished room. |
| 04 | **Design Refinement** | Two revision rounds — adjust materials, feature wall design, lighting positions, media unit configuration. |
| 05 | **Costing & Agreement** | Fixed-price quotation — itemised. No surprises. Agreement signed. |
| 06 | **Material Procurement** | Flooring, feature wall materials, ceiling materials, lighting fixtures ordered and delivered. |
| 07 | **Preparation** | Existing flooring removed (if applicable), walls patched, ceiling framework installed. |
| 08 | **Construction** | Flooring, ceiling, feature wall, electrical, media unit framework — the room takes shape. |
| 09 | **Finishes & Fixtures** | Paint, lighting installed, media unit completed, curtains fitted, deep clean. |
| 10 | **Snag & Handover** | Full inspection, corrections, handover — living room ready to enjoy. |

## SECTION 7 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Living Room Renovation — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: How long does living room renovation take?**

Most living room renovations are completed in 3–4 weeks from design approval to handover. Paint + lighting only: 1–2 weeks. Full renovation with feature wall and ceiling: 3–4 weeks.

---

**Q2: What feature wall options do you offer?**

Wood panelling, stone cladding, LED-backlit panels, wallpaper, fabric panels, marble slabs, or custom-designed feature walls. We present options based on your style and budget.

---

**Q3: Can I use the living room during renovation?**

For minor work (paint, lighting), partially yes. For full renovation (flooring, ceiling, feature wall), it is better to vacate the room for 3–4 weeks.

---

**Q4: How much does living room renovation cost?**

Paint + lighting: PKR 200K–500K. Full renovation: PKR 1M–2M+. Exact pricing after free survey.

---

**Q5: Do you design false ceilings?**

Yes. Tray ceilings, drop ceilings, LED cove lighting — designed and built to complement the room's style and lighting plan.

---

**Q6: Can you build a media / TV wall unit?**

Yes. Custom-built floating or floor-standing media units — designed around your TV size, with hidden cable management, storage and ambient backlighting.

---

**Q7: Do you handle curtains and window treatments?**

Yes. We design and install curtains, drapes, blinds and motorised curtain systems as part of the living room renovation.

---

**Q8: Do you offer a warranty?**

Yes. 2-year warranty on all construction, fixtures and finishes.
