### 10 Marla — The Family Home Sweet Spot

A 10 Marla plot (500 sq yards / ~4,500 sq ft) is the most popular plot size for family homes in Lahore's established neighbourhoods — DHA, Bahria Town, Gulberg, Model Town. It offers enough width (35–50 ft) for generous room layouts, separate drawing and dining rooms, a proper kitchen, multiple bathrooms, servant quarters, and parking for 1–2 cars — without the cost and complexity of a 1+ Kanal estate.

Most 10 Marla houses are two storeys: ground floor for public and service spaces (drawing, dining, kitchen, guest bathroom, parking, servant quarter); first floor for private family spaces (bedrooms, family lounge, laundry). Some designs add a second floor for additional bedrooms or a rooftop terrace.

The design challenge on a 10 Marla plot is different from a 5 Marla: you have more space, but you also have more choices — and more ways to get the layout wrong. Room proportions, circulation flow, natural light distribution across a wider floor plate, and the relationship between public and private zones all need careful planning. This is where a professional architect earns their fee.

## SECTION 3 — DESIGNING A FAMILY HOME ON A 10 MARLA PLOT

**H2:** Designing a Family Home on a 10 Marla Plot

A 10 Marla plot — 500 square yards, roughly 35 to 50 feet wide — is the plot size most Lahore families choose for their permanent home. It sits in the sweet spot between compact and expansive: enough width for generous room layouts, enough depth for a proper back garden or courtyard, and enough floor area for a genuine family home across two storeys.

Most 10 Marla houses in Lahore are two storeys. The ground floor typically holds the drawing room, dining room, kitchen, guest bathroom, servant quarter and parking (1–2 cars). The first floor holds the bedrooms (usually 3–4), family lounge, laundry and store. Some designs add a second floor for additional bedrooms or a rooftop terrace.

The design challenge at this scale is proportion and flow. You have more space than a 5 Marla plot — but you also have more decisions. How wide should the drawing room be? Where does the dining room sit relative to the kitchen? How do you separate the public entertaining zone from the private family zone? Where does the staircase go without wasting corridor space? These are the questions that a well-designed 10 Marla house answers before construction begins.

**Design priority cards:**

| Priority | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------------|
| **Room Proportions** | 10 Marla gives you space — but rooms must be proportioned for real furniture and real use, not just "big" |
| **Public-Private Flow** | Drawing/dining (public) on the ground floor; bedrooms (private) on the first — clear separation |
| **Light & Ventilation** | Wider floor plate means some rooms may be far from external walls; light wells and window placement matter |
| **Kitchen Design** | Full-sized kitchen with pantry, proper ventilation, and connection to dining — the heart of a family home |
| **Parking & Access** | 1–2 car parking integrated into the ground floor without compromising living spaces |
| **Outdoor Space** | Back garden or courtyard, servant entry, boundary wall design — all planned at concept stage |

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** 10 Marla House Design — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What can I build on a 10 Marla plot?**

A 10 Marla plot (500 sq yd) typically supports a 2-storey family home with 3–4 bedrooms (most with en-suite bathrooms), drawing room, dining room, kitchen with pantry, guest bathroom, servant quarter, laundry, store, and parking for 1–2 cars. Some designs include a home office, study or rooftop terrace depending on your requirements.

---

**Q2: How much does a 10 Marla house design cost?**

Woodex charges PKR 400,000–600,000 for a complete 10 Marla house design — concept plans, 3D renders, working drawings, elevations and authority submission preparation. Front elevation only is PKR 50,000–150,000. Fees are lump sum — no hidden charges.

---

**Q3: How is a 10 Marla house different from a 5 Marla house?**

A 10 Marla plot (500 sq yd) is double the size of a 5 Marla plot (250 sq yd). The key differences: separate drawing and dining rooms (instead of combined), a full-sized kitchen with pantry, larger bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms, dedicated servant quarters, parking for 1–2 cars, and outdoor space (garden or courtyard). The design fee is PKR 400K–600K vs PKR 200K–400K for 5 Marla.

---

**Q4: How long does the design take?**

A 10 Marla house design typically takes 5–8 weeks from the initial brief to final working drawings. The extra time (vs 5 Marla's 4–6 weeks) reflects the larger floor area, more rooms, and greater design complexity. Authority approval is separate (2–6 weeks for LDA).

---

**Q5: Can I add a third floor to my 10 Marla house?**

Whether a third floor is allowed depends on local bylaws, setback requirements and street width. LDA regulations vary by zone. We check bylaws during the initial site visit and design accordingly. If a third floor is permitted, it adds significant floor area — useful for additional bedrooms, a home theatre or a guest suite.

---

**Q6: Do you design for specific housing societies (DHA, Bahria, etc.)?**

Yes. We design for all major Lahore housing societies — DHA, Bahria Town, Gulberg, Model Town, Wapda Town, and others. Each society has its own bylaws, setback requirements and approval processes. We are familiar with these and design accordingly, including handling the specific authority submission required by each society.

---

**Q7: Can I use my own contractor?**

Yes. Our working drawings are contractor-independent — they follow standard architectural conventions and include enough detail for any qualified construction team. You are not obligated to use Woodex for construction.

---

**Q8: Can Woodex design my 10 Marla house AND do the interior design?**

Yes. Woodex handles architecture, interior design, fit-out and furniture (via Woodex Furniture™) under one roof. The architect works directly with the interior designer and fit-out team — no re-briefing. For everything under one contract: [Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/).
