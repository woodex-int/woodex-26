## SECTION 2 — WHAT IS HOME REFURBISHMENT?

**H2:** What Is Home Refurbishment?

Home refurbishment is the refreshing of an existing home — new paint, updated flooring, replaced light fixtures, new door hardware, refreshed bathroom fittings, updated kitchen surfaces — without major structural changes. Unlike renovation (which may involve wall removal, plumbing relocation or layout reconfiguration), refurbishment works within the existing structure to make your home look and feel new.

Woodex delivers home refurbishment from assessment to handover. We survey your home, understand what you want refreshed, design the scope (including 3D visualization for larger projects), execute the work, and hand over a home that looks like it was just redecorated — typically completed in 1–3 weeks depending on scope.

Refurbishment is the fastest and most cost-effective way to transform your home. It is ideal when the structure is sound, the layout works, but the finishes are tired, outdated or worn.

**What home refurbishment covers:**

- ✓ Full home repainting (walls, ceilings, doors, trim)
- ✓ Flooring replacement (tile, vinyl, wood, carpet)
- ✓ Light fixture replacement (ceiling, wall, pendant, recessed)
- ✓ Door and cabinet hardware (handles, hinges, locks)
- ✓ Bathroom fittings (faucets, shower heads, towel rails, mirrors)
- ✓ Kitchen surface updates (paint, hardware, backsplash refresh)
- ✓ Window treatments (curtains, blinds, tracks)
- ✓ Minor electrical (outlet replacement, switch plates, USB outlets)

> **Difference: Home Refurbishment vs Renovation vs Interior Design**
>
> | | Home Refurbishment | Home Renovation | Interior Design |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | **What it covers** | Refreshing finishes — paint, flooring, fixtures, surfaces | Upgrading structure — may include wall removal, plumbing, layout | Planning the layout, materials, colours (the blueprint) |
> | **Structural changes** | No | Yes (may include) | N/A (planning only) |
> | **Timeline** | 1–3 weeks | 2–10 weeks | 2–4 weeks (design only) |
> | **Best for** | Home is sound but looks tired | Home needs structural or layout changes | Need to plan before any work begins |
> | **Cost** | PKR 200K–2M | PKR 500K–7M+ | PKR 200K–500K |

> **Need more than refurbishment?**
> → **[Home Renovation](/home-renovation/)** — structural changes, layout reconfiguration, deeper scope.

> **Need the plan first?**
> → **[Interior Design](/interior-design/)** — layout, materials, colours, 3D visualization.

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Home Refurbishment — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: How long does home refurbishment take?**

Most home refurbishments are completed in 1–3 weeks. Full repaint only: 1–2 weeks. Paint + flooring + fixtures: 2–3 weeks.

---

**Q2: Can I live in my home during refurbishment?**

Yes. Refurbishment is less disruptive than renovation. We work room-by-room and maintain clean work areas.

---

**Q3: What is the difference between refurbishment and renovation?**

Refurbishment refreshes finishes (paint, flooring, fixtures) without structural changes. Renovation may include wall removal, plumbing, layout changes. See the comparison table above.

---

**Q4: How much does home refurbishment cost?**

Full repaint: PKR 200K–500K. Paint + flooring + fixtures: PKR 600K–1.5M. Full refurbishment: PKR 800K–2M. Exact pricing after free survey.

---

**Q5: Do you supply the materials?**

Yes. We source paint, flooring, fixtures, hardware and fittings. You choose from options we present; we procure and install.

---

**Q6: Can refurbishment increase my property value?**

Yes. A fresh repaint, new flooring and updated fixtures are the highest-ROI improvements for property sales. Refurbishment is the most cost-effective way to increase appeal.

---

**Q7: Can I refurbish just one room?**

Yes. Many clients refurbish one or two rooms — we scope and price per room. You do not need to refurbish the entire home.

---

**Q8: Do you offer a warranty?**

Yes. 2-year warranty on all work, materials and fixtures.
