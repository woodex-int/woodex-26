```

"I need someone to plan and design my office"

"I need someone to build out a shell or empty office"

"I need someone to modernize my current office without moving"
```

Every paragraph on this page must reinforce that this service is for **existing, occupied spaces** — not new leases or shell conversions.

**Tab contents:**

**Tab 1 — Cosmetic Refresh:**
> The fastest, most cost-effective renovation. Updates surfaces and finishes without moving walls or touching MEP systems — paint, ceiling tiles, flooring, lighting, branding and joinery refresh. Ideal when the layout works but the space looks dated.

> **Typically includes:**
> - Paint, texture or wallpaper refresh
> - Ceiling tile replacement or repair
> - New flooring — vinyl, carpet tile or laminate
> - LED lighting upgrade
> - Reception and joinery refresh
> - Branding and wall graphics update

> **Best for:** Offices that need a visual lift quickly and at minimal cost.

**Tab 2 — Reconfiguration:**
> Changes the layout of the office — new partitions, glass systems, different zone arrangement. Open plan to more cabins, or cabins to open plan. Includes cosmetic refresh plus partition work, MEP adjustments and re-zoning.

> **Typically includes:**
> - Everything in Cosmetic Refresh, plus:
> - New partition layout — drywall or glass
> - Electrical and data re-location
> - Ceiling and lighting reconfiguration
> - New zone designation and wayfinding
> - Storage and joinery re-positioning

> **Best for:** Growing teams, changing departments or adopting hybrid working.

**Tab 3 — Expansion:**
> Takes on additional space — an adjacent floor, an extension or a neighboring unit — and connects it to your existing office. Includes structural coordination, MEP connection, new finishes and a unified design.

> **Typically includes:**
> - Everything in Reconfiguration, plus:
> - Structural assessment of connection points
> - New floor / unit integration
> - Full MEP connection to existing services
> - Unified finishes and design across old and new
> - Floor-level phasing plan

> **Best for:** Growing businesses taking on more space within the same building.

**Tab 4 — Full Structural Renovation:**
> A complete interior renovation of an occupied office — new ceilings, floors, walls, MEP systems, joinery, furniture and finishes. The office is fully transformed, trade by trade, while phasing preserves occupancy.

> **Typically includes:**
> - Everything in Expansion, plus:
> - Full MEP overhaul — electrical, HVAC, plumbing
> - Ceiling replacement — gypsum or grid
> - Full flooring replacement
> - Complete joinery and furniture replacement
> - Comprehensive phasing and out-of-hours scheduling

> **Best for:** Offices in older buildings requiring a complete interior refresh.

## SECTION 9 COPY — COST FACTORS

**H2:**

> What Affects the Cost of Office Renovation in Lahore?

**Intro:**

> Office renovation costs vary based on scope, condition and specification. The following are the main factors Woodex Interior evaluates when preparing your BOQ.

**Factor 1 — Total Area (sq. ft.):**
> The total square footage of the office in scope is the primary cost driver. Larger spaces require more materials, more trade-hours and more project management time. Cost scales predictably per square foot — but the rate varies with specification and complexity.

**Factor 2 — Renovation Type: Cosmetic vs Structural:**
> A cosmetic refresh — paint, flooring, lighting, branding — is the most economical route. Full structural renovation — including MEP overhaul, new partitions, new ceilings and complete reconfiguration — costs significantly more but delivers a fundamentally transformed workspace.

**Factor 3 — Condition of Existing Office:**
> The condition of your current office determines how much must be replaced versus upgraded. A well-maintained space needing cosmetic updates is materially cheaper than a deteriorated office requiring structural repair, moisture remediation or MEP overhaul.

**Factor 4 — Partition and Layout Changes:**
> Adding, removing or changing partition layout — especially glass partitions — is a significant cost item. The more partitions change, the more electrical, data and ceiling work is required.

**Factor 5 — MEP Overhaul Required:**
> Electrical rewiring, HVAC upgrade, data re-cabling and plumbing adjustments are the most variable cost elements. Older buildings almost always require more MEP work than initially assumed.

**Factor 6 — Material and Finish Specification:**
> The board, flooring, partition system, lighting, joinery finish and furniture tier you choose have a material impact on the total budget. Economy, mid-range and premium options are presented at each stage.

**Factor 7 — Phasing and Occupied-Space Complexity:**
> Phased renovation around an occupied team adds coordination cost and time. It is more expensive than full-site closure — but almost always worth it to protect productivity and avoid the cost of temporary relocation.

**Note box:**

> Office renovation typically costs significantly less than moving to a new space or fitting out a new shell. Woodex Interior prepares a detailed, itemized BOQ before any work begins — no lump sums with no breakdown. Every line item is costed separately so you can make informed decisions about scope and specification.
