# PART 1 — PAGE STRATEGY & SEO SPECIFICATIONS

## 1.1 Page Purpose

This page has **three jobs** running simultaneously:

| Job | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **SEO** | Rank for "turnkey interior design Lahore", "design and build company Lahore" and related searches — the highest-value intent: a visitor ready to hand over their entire project |
| **Education** | Help the visitor understand what turnkey means — design, build, furnish under one contract with one team — and why it is the least-risk, least-hassle way to complete a project |
| **Conversion** | Move the visitor from reading to requesting a proposal — and position turnkey as the premium, zero-hassle alternative to managing multiple vendors |

### Critical Keyword Differentiation

```
/turnkey-design-build/       → TURNKEY (end-to-end under one contract)
"I want one team to handle everything — design through handover"

/office-interior-design/     → DESIGN ONLY (planning)
"I only need the design; I will manage execution"

/office-fit-out/             → FIT-OUT (shell/new-build completion)
"I need a shell space completed — I will arrange the design"

/office-renovation/          → RENOVATION (existing space upgrade)
"My existing space needs upgrading"

/residential-interior-design/→ RESIDENTIAL DESIGN (home planning)
"I need my home designed"
```

Every paragraph must reinforce that turnkey is **one contract, one team, one responsibility** — from first sketch to final handover. The visitor pays once, manages nothing, and receives a finished space. This is the highest-value, lowest-friction service Woodex offers.

### The Turnkey Differentiators

This page has **five unique selling points**:

1. **One contract, one team, one responsibility** — design, build, furniture, MEP, handover — all under one agreement. No multi-vendor coordination.
2. **Zero-hassle delivery** — the client does not manage trades, procurement or sequencing. Woodex owns every trade and every decision.
3. **Single-point accountability** — one project manager, one programme, one budget, one point of contact from brief to snagging.
4. **Furniture included via Woodex Furniture™** — custom joinery, kitchens, wardrobes, workstations and millwork are manufactured in-house and integrated into the project — not outsourced.
5. **Visible 3D before any work** — the entire project is visualized in photorealistic 3D before construction begins, reducing risk and eliminating surprises.

## SECTION 2 COPY — WHAT IS TURNKEY

**H2:**

> What Is Turnkey Design and Build?

**Paragraph 1:**

> Turnkey design and build is an end-to-end delivery model where one company — one contract, one team — handles every stage of a project from first concept to final handover. Design, engineering, construction, MEP, joinery, furniture, finishes and snagging all run under a single agreement. The client pays once, manages nothing, and receives a finished, ready-to-use space.

**Paragraph 2:**

> Turnkey is different from design-only, fit-out-only or renovation-only because it bundles every scope into one managed package. With design-only you receive plans but manage execution yourself. With fit-out you manage the design or procure it separately. With turnkey, the same team that designs your space also builds it, manufactures the furniture and hands it over complete — one point of contact, one budget, one programme.

**Paragraph 3:**

> Woodex Interior delivers turnkey projects for offices, homes, retail stores, restaurants, healthcare facilities and commercial spaces across Lahore and Pakistan. Custom joinery, kitchens, wardrobes and workstations are manufactured in-house through our sister brand Woodex Furniture™ and integrated directly into the project — not outsourced, not separate, not someone else's problem.

> **Turnkey** → Design + Build + Furniture — one contract, one team
> **Design Only** → Planning and visualization — no execution
> **Fit-Out** → Shell or new-build completion — construction scope
> **Renovation** → Upgrade of existing occupied space
>
> Woodex offers all four — independently or combined. Turnkey bundles everything into a single, managed agreement.

## SECTION 6 COPY — THE PROCESS

**H2:**

> The Turnkey Process — 12 Steps

**Intro:**

> Every Woodex turnkey project follows a structured 12-step process — divided into Design (Steps 1–6) and Build (Steps 7–12). Everything is documented, costed and approved before construction begins.

**Phase A — Design:**

**Step 01 — Brief and Site Survey:**

> We meet you on-site, discuss your goals, measure accurately and photograph existing conditions. We understand your brief — how you want the space to look, feel and function — and confirm what is structurally and technically possible.

**Step 02 — Concept Design and Mood Boards:**

> Design concepts are developed based on your brief — styles, palettes, material directions and spatial character. Mood boards and initial concept options are presented for discussion.

**Step 03 — Space Planning and Layouts:**

> Detailed floor plans, furniture layouts and circulation routes are drawn — confirming how every zone works and every element is positioned.

**Step 04 — 3D Visualization:**

> Photorealistic 3D renders of your space are presented for approval. You see exactly what your finished space will look like — rooms, materials, finishes, lighting — before any physical work begins.

**Step 05 — Material and Finish Specification:**

> Every material, finish and fixture is specified — flooring, walls, ceilings, lighting, sanitaryware, joinery hardware. Options at different specification tiers are presented with cost implications.

**Step 06 — BOQ and Cost Plan:**

> A complete, itemized cost plan is produced — every trade, every material, every labour item costed individually. You approve the scope and budget before the contract is signed.

**Phase B — Build:**

**Step 07 — Contract and Mobilisation:**

> A formal contract and construction programme are issued — with milestones, payment schedule and scope confirmation. Site teams are mobilised.

**Step 08 — Strip-Out and Structural Prep:**

> Any existing elements marked for removal are stripped out. Surface preparation and structural adjustments (if approved) are completed.

**Step 09 — MEP First-Fix:**

> Electrical relocations, plumbing, HVAC rough-ins and data infrastructure are installed before ceilings and walls are closed.

**Step 10 — Finishes, Joinery and Furniture:**

> New ceilings, walls, flooring, joinery, custom furniture and fixtures are installed — built to the approved 3D and specification. Woodex Furniture™ joinery is manufactured in-house and delivered on schedule.

**Step 11 — Snagging, Cleaning and Quality Inspection:**

> Every element is inspected against the approved specification. Snags are listed, corrected and re-inspected. The space is deep-cleaned.

**Step 12 — Formal Handover and Documentation:**

> Final photography, as-built documentation, warranty certificates and handover are completed. The space is ready for use.

**One-contract callout:**

> **One Contract. One Team. One Responsibility.**
> Every step above runs under a single agreement with Woodex. You manage nothing; we own every trade and every decision. One project manager, one programme, one budget, one point of contact — from brief to handover.
