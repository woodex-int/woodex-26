```

"retail interior design Lahore" (broad intent)

/retail-interior-design/        → SERVICE PAGE (interior design)
"I need the retail layout planned"

"I have an existing shop that needs upgrading"

/retail-fit-out/                → SERVICE PAGE (fit-out)
"I have a shell space — I need it built out"

"I want one team for everything"
```

**Anti-cannibalisation rule:** This sector page is an **overview** of Woodex's capabilities for retail. It does NOT target service-specific keywords like "retail fit-out" or "retail renovation" — those belong to the individual service pages. Every paragraph must end with a link to the appropriate service page.

## SECTION 2 — WHAT WE DO FOR RETAIL

**H2:** What We Do for Retail

Woodex designs and builds retail spaces across Lahore — from single boutiques and showrooms to brand stores and multi-location chain rollouts. Every retail space we create is built around one goal: selling. The shopfront must stop foot traffic, the display must showcase the product, the lighting must flatter the merchandise, and the layout must move customers from entrance to checkout.

A retail space is your brand made physical. Woodex delivers the complete package — shopfront design, display planning, branded finishes, feature lighting, checkout areas and chain-standard fittings. For brand operators, we develop prototype designs that replicate consistently across every location — one design, standardized execution, faster rollout.

## SECTION 6 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Retail — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What types of retail does Woodex design?**

Fashion boutiques, electronics showrooms, pharmacies, furniture showrooms, jewellery stores, beauty salons, bakeries, groceries, bank branches — and chain rollouts across formats.

---

**Q2: How much does retail interior design cost in Lahore?**

Design fees start from PKR 150,000–250,000 for small shops and scale with area and complexity. Renovation and fit-out are quoted separately after a free site survey.

---

**Q3: Do you manufacture display units in-house?**

Yes — through sister brand WOODEX FURNITURE™. Custom display units, counters and feature walls are produced in-house, designed around your product.

---

**Q4: Can you standardize fit-out for chain rollouts?**

Yes. We develop a prototype design and standard specification, then replicate it across every location with consistent execution and brand compliance.

---

**Q5: How long does a retail fit-out take?**

Most retail fit-outs are completed in 4–8 weeks from design approval to handover. Larger showrooms or complex shopfronts may take longer.

---

**Q6: Can we continue trading during renovation?**

Yes. We phase work zone-by-zone or after-hours to minimize disruption to your trading. For shell fit-outs, the space is typically unoccupied.

---

**Q7: What is the difference between retail renovation and retail fit-out?**

Renovation upgrades an existing store. Fit-out builds a shell space from scratch. [Retail Renovation](/retail-renovation/) | [Retail Fit-Out](/retail-fit-out/)

---

**Q8: Do you offer turnkey solutions for retail?**

Yes. [Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/) — one team, one contract, from design to handover.
