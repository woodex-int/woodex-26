## SECTION 2 — WHAT EDUCATIONAL BUILDING DESIGN COVERS

**H2:** What Educational Building Design Covers

Educational building design is the architectural process of planning a building for learning — from the initial concept through to construction-ready drawings. Unlike a generic commercial building, an educational building is designed around how children and students learn, move and stay safe: classroom clusters, corridor circulation, assembly and recreation spaces, playgrounds, washroom ratios, acoustics, and the safety requirements of a school environment.

Woodex designs educational buildings from concept to final working drawings. We deliver concept floor plans and 3D visualizations showing the building's form, classroom layout, circulation, assembly and recreation areas, and facade — so you can see your school before construction starts. Working drawings in AutoCAD and Revit — plans, sections, elevations, structural details, MEP coordination drawings, schedules — the complete technical package your contractor builds from. Facade elevations designed for a welcoming, safe and professional presence. And authority submissions: education department, LDA/CDA, fire safety — prepared and submitted as part of the service.

Every educational building Woodex designs is planned around learning requirements: classroom design (natural light, cross-ventilation, acoustics, furniture layout), classroom clusters (adjacent classes grouped by level, teacher stations, storage), circulation (safe corridors, staircase design for children, no dead-ends), safety (non-slip floors, safe railings, fire escape routes, easy supervision from staff positions), and assembly/recreation (hall, playground, court — sized to student volume).

**Checklist:**

- ✓ Classroom design (natural light, ventilation, acoustics, furniture layout)
- ✓ Classroom clusters (grouping by level, teacher stations, storage)
- ✓ Safe circulation (corridors, staircases sized for children, no dead-ends)
- ✓ Assembly + recreation (hall, playground, court)
- ✓ Safety + supervision (non-slip, railings, fire escape, sightlines)
- ✓ Authority submissions (education dept., LDA, CDA, fire)

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Educational Building Design — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What is the difference between educational building architecture and school interior design?**

Educational building architecture is the design of the building itself — structure, classroom clusters, circulation, safety, playground, authority approvals. School interior design is planning the space inside — classrooms, finishes, branding. You need architecture for a new educational building or major redevelopment; interior design for a school within an existing building.

---

**Q2: How do you design classrooms for natural light and ventilation?**

Classroom design balances window placement, orientation and cross-ventilation. South-facing windows with appropriate shading give good light without glare; cross-ventilation keeps rooms fresh in Lahore's climate; acoustic insulation between classrooms keeps noise from bleeding between lessons. Windows are placed so light comes over the shoulder of students, not straight into their eyes.

---

**Q3: What safety measures do you design into a school building?**

Non-slip floor finishes, safe handrails and balustrades (child-height where appropriate), corridor widths and staircase designs sized for children, fire escape routes and exits, no dead-end circulation, and sightlines that let staff supervise corridors from classroom doorways. Safety is designed into the building, not added after.

---

**Q4: How long does educational building architecture take?**

A typical educational building takes 8–16 weeks from brief to final working drawings, depending on school type and student volume. Authority approval times are separate: education department (6–12 weeks), LDA/CDA (4–12 weeks), fire (2–4 weeks). We manage all submissions in parallel where possible.

---

**Q5: What education department approvals are required?**

Schools, academies and colleges require education department approval and registration. Woodex prepares the building layout drawings, safety and compliance documentation, and manages the submission process as part of the architecture service. Requirements vary by province and institution type.

---

**Q6: Can you design a multi-storey school building?**

Yes. Multi-storey school buildings are designed with additional care: staircase design (sized and placed for student flow during breaks), washroom ratios per floor, senior and junior zones on separate floors, and fire escape strategies for children. The same safety principles apply at every floor level.

---

**Q7: Can Woodex design the building AND do the interior and fit-out?**

Yes — and this is one of Woodex's key advantages. We handle educational building architecture, school interior design, fit-out and furniture under one roof. The architect works directly with the interior designer and fit-out team — no re-briefing, no conflicting drawings. For everything under one contract: [Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/).

---

**Q8: What if I only need a front elevation for a school building?**

Woodex offers front elevation design as a standalone service — PKR 50,000–150,000. You receive a photorealistic 3D render and 2D elevation drawing. [View Front Elevation Design →](/front-elevation-design/)
