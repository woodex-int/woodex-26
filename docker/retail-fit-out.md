### Critical Keyword Differentiation

```
/retail-fit-out/            → RETAIL FIT-OUT (new shell → retail/showroom)
"I'm opening a new shop or showroom — I need it fitted out"

/restaurant-fit-out/        → RESTAURANT FIT-OUT (F&B-specific)
"I'm opening a café or restaurant"

/office-fit-out/            → OFFICE FIT-OUT (workspace)
"I'm fitting out a new office"

/commercial-renovation/     → COMMERCIAL RENOVATION (existing space upgrade)
"I have an existing retail space that needs upgrading"
```

**Anti-cannibalisation rule:** Retail fit-out = "I'm building out a shell or new space into a retail store or showroom." This page must never overlap with restaurant fit-out (F&B), office fit-out (workspace), or commercial renovation (existing space upgrade). Every paragraph must reinforce that retail fit-out = new retail/showroom build-out, and link to restaurant fit-out for F&B businesses.

### The Retail Fit-Out Difference

| Factor | Retail & Showroom Fit-Out | Office Fit-Out | Restaurant Fit-Out |
|--------|---------------------------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Primary goal** | Drive product sales through display and experience | Drive employee productivity | Drive F&B service and dining experience |
| **Layout focus** | Customer flow, product zones, display | Workstations, meeting rooms, cabins | Kitchen workflow, dining area, bar |
| **Lighting** | Spotlights, track, ambient — product-focused | 4000K LED — task/ambient | Ambient, decorative — mood-focused |
| **Flooring** | Vinyl, tiles, wood-look — durable, branded | Carpet, vinyl, tiles | Anti-slip, wet-area rated |
| **Key element** | Shopfront & display units (WOODEX FURNITURE™) | Reception, cabins, workstations | Kitchen exhaust, hoods, plumbing |

## SECTION 2 — WHAT RETAIL FIT-OUT INCLUDES

**H2:** What Retail & Showroom Fit-Out Includes

Retail fit-out is the build-out of a bare shell or empty unit into a fully branded, customer-ready retail store or showroom — every element designed around product display, customer flow, brand identity and sales conversion. Unlike general commercial fit-out, every material, every layout decision and every lighting choice in a retail space serves a specific merchandising or brand purpose.

Woodex has fitted out independent shops, brand stores, showrooms and multi-location retail chains across Lahore and Pakistan. Our retail fit-out service covers the complete scope: customer-flow space planning, shopfront and entrance design, custom display units and shelving (manufactured by Woodex Furniture™), spotlight and track lighting for product focus, feature and backlit ceiling panels, branded wall graphics and signage, vinyl or wood-look flooring, and back-of-house storage and stockroom build-out.

Every Woodex retail fit-out is developed in AutoCAD and visualised in photorealistic 3D before construction begins — so you see exactly how your store will look and function before a single partition goes up. For chain operators, we develop a repeatable prototype that can be deployed across multiple locations with consistent brand identity and faster timelines.

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Retail & Showroom Fit-Out — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: How much does it cost to fit out a retail shop in Lahore?**

A basic retail fit-out (1,000–1,500 sq ft) costs PKR 7.5M–10.5M. A mid-spec retail space with custom display units and branded graphics costs PKR 10.5M–13.5M. A luxury showroom costs PKR 15M–22.5M. All prices depend on unit size, specification and material selection. A free site survey provides an accurate fixed quote.

---

**Q2: How long does a retail fit-out take?**

A standard retail unit (1,000–2,000 sq ft) takes 5–7 weeks from brief to handover. A chain rollout prototype takes 7–10 weeks. Once the prototype is established, subsequent locations take 4–6 weeks each. Timelines assume timely design approval and material procurement.

---

**Q3: Can you fit out multiple retail locations for a brand?**

Yes — and this is one of Woodex's key strengths. We develop a repeatable retail prototype — shopfront, display system, lighting, finishes, signage — that can be deployed across multiple locations. The prototype is designed once, approved once, and replicated at each site with consistent brand identity and faster timelines.

---

**Q4: Can you match our brand's design guidelines?**

Yes. Woodex works within your brand guidelines — colours, materials, logo application, signage specifications, display standards. We have experience executing brand-compliant fit-outs for local and international retail brands. Send us your brand book and we will design to specification.

---

**Q5: What is included in a retail fit-out versus a renovation?**

A fit-out is a complete build-out of a bare shell or new-build unit into a functioning retail store — shopfront, display, lighting, flooring, branding, storage. A renovation is an upgrade of an existing occupied retail space — display refresh, finishes, layout adjustments, usually without shutting down entirely. If your retail space already exists and you want to upgrade it, see [Commercial Renovation](/commercial-renovation/). If you're starting from a shell, this is the right page.

---

**Q6: Can Woodex handle display units and furniture?**

Yes. Our sister brand Woodex Furniture™ manufactures custom display units, shelving systems, counters, feature walls and back-of-house storage — all custom-fitted to your retail space and product type. This eliminates third-party delays and ensures display furniture matches the overall design and brand identity.

---

**Q7: Do you work on retail projects outside Lahore?**

Yes. While our studio is in Lahore (Model Town), we have delivered retail fit-outs across Punjab and in other cities including Islamabad, Karachi and Faisalabad. Site visits for projects outside Lahore are arranged separately. Remote design collaboration (3D renders, virtual meetings) keeps the process efficient.

---

**Q8: What if I only need a shopfront — not a full fit-out?**

Woodex Furniture™ can manufacture and install a custom shopfront — entrance design, fascia, signage, glass, doors — as a standalone scope. Contact us for a shopfront-only quote. For a full retail fit-out including display, lighting, flooring, branding and storage, this is the right page.
