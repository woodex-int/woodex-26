## SECTION 2 — WHAT WE DO FOR F&B

**H2:** What We Do for F&B

Woodex designs and builds food and beverage spaces across Lahore — restaurants, cafes, bakeries, cloud kitchens, food court outlets and lounges. Every F&B space we create is built around two priorities: the kitchen must work (workflow, exhaust, plumbing, compliance) and the dining area must feel right (atmosphere, brand, customer experience).

F&B fit-out is one of the most technically demanding interior projects. It requires specialist knowledge of kitchen workflow, exhaust and ventilation systems, grease traps, drainage, food-grade materials, and health department compliance (PFA standards). Woodex handles all of this — and delivers the front-of-house atmosphere that keeps customers coming back.

**What Woodex does for F&B:**

- ✓ Kitchen layout and workflow design
- ✓ Exhaust and ventilation systems (hood, ductwork, fresh air)
- ✓ Plumbing and drainage (hot/cold water, grease trap, waste)
- ✓ Dining area design and atmosphere
- ✓ Bar and counter design
- ✓ Branded finishes and materials
- ✓ Storage, dry store and cold room spaces
- ✓ PFA-compliant elements (ventilation, wet areas, cleanable finishes)

## SECTION 6 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Food & Beverage — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What types of F&B businesses does Woodex design?**

Fine dining, casual dining, cafes, coffee houses, bakeries, QSR, cloud kitchens, food court outlets, bars and lounges.

---

**Q2: How much does restaurant interior design cost in Lahore?**

Design fees start from PKR 200,000 for cafes and scale with concept complexity. Fit-out costs range from PKR 2M–32M depending on type and spec. Quoted after free survey.

---

**Q3: Do you handle kitchen exhaust and ventilation?**

Yes. Kitchen exhaust — hood, ductwork, fresh air intake, grease filtration — is a core part of our F&B fit-out. Designed and installed to meet PFA and safety standards.

---

**Q4: Do you handle PFA compliance?**

We design and build the physical elements PFA requires: ventilation, grease traps, hand-wash stations, food-safe flooring, drainage, and cleanable finishes. The actual PFA inspection and license are your responsibility.

---

**Q5: How long does a restaurant fit-out take?**

Most restaurant fit-outs are completed in 6–12 weeks from design approval to handover. Cafes and cloud kitchens: 6–8 weeks. Full-service restaurants: 8–12 weeks.

---

**Q6: Can you design a cloud kitchen (delivery-only)?**

Yes. Cloud kitchens need efficient kitchen layouts, minimal front-of-house, and delivery staging areas. We design and build cloud kitchens optimized for delivery operations.

---

**Q7: What is the difference between restaurant renovation and fit-out?**

Renovation upgrades an existing restaurant. Fit-out builds a shell space from scratch. [Restaurant Renovation](/restaurant-renovation/) | [Restaurant Fit-Out](/restaurant-fit-out/)

---

**Q8: Do you offer turnkey solutions for F&B?**

Yes. [Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/) — one team, one contract, from design to handover.
