**Anti-cannibalisation rule:** Pharmacy fit-out = "I'm building out a shell or new space into a dispensing pharmacy." This page must never overlap with healthcare fit-out (clinics, labs), commercial renovation (existing space upgrade), or office renovation. ## SECTION 2 — WHAT PHARMACY FIT-OUT INCLUDES

**H2:** What Pharmacy Fit-Out Includes

Pharmacy fit-out is the build-out of a bare shell or empty unit into a fully operational dispensing pharmacy — every element designed around pharmacist workflow, medication storage, regulatory compliance and customer experience. Unlike general commercial fit-out, every material, every layout decision and every system in a pharmacy serves a specific clinical or regulatory purpose.

Woodex has fitted out independent pharmacies and multi-location pharmacy chains across Lahore and Pakistan. Our pharmacy fit-out service covers the complete scope: dispensing counter design (L-shaped, U-shaped or island layout), controlled drug storage cabinets, climate-controlled zones for temperature-sensitive medication, antibacterial wall finishes, anti-slip easy-clean flooring, DGDA/PBMC-compliant signage and branding, security systems (CCTV, alarms, restricted access), and MEP coordination for pharmacy-specific lighting and HVAC requirements.

Every Woodex pharmacy fit-out is developed in AutoCAD and visualised in photorealistic 3D before construction begins — so you see exactly how your pharmacy will look and function before a single partition goes up. For chain operators, we develop a repeatable prototype that can be deployed across multiple locations with consistent quality and faster timelines.

**What's included:**

- ✓ Dispensing counter design (L-shaped, U-shaped or island)
- ✓ Controlled drug storage cabinet (locked, alarmed, access-restricted)
- ✓ Temperature-controlled zones for medication storage
- ✓ Antibacterial wall finishes and washable surfaces
- ✓ Anti-slip, easy-clean flooring (vinyl or commercial tile)
- ✓ Pharmacy-specific lighting (4000K–5000K, glare-free on labels)
- ✓ DGDA/PBMC-compliant signage and branding
- ✓ Security systems — CCTV, alarms, restricted-access zones

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Pharmacy Fit-Out — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: How much does it cost to fit out a pharmacy in Lahore?**

A basic pharmacy fit-out (800–1,000 sq ft) costs PKR 5.5M–7M. A mid-spec pharmacy with custom counter and controlled drug storage costs PKR 7M–9M. A premium pharmacy or chain rollout costs PKR 9M–12M. All prices depend on unit size, specification and material selection. A free site survey provides an accurate fixed quote.

---

**Q2: How long does a pharmacy fit-out take?**

A standard independent pharmacy (800–1,200 sq ft) takes 4–6 weeks from brief to handover. A chain rollout prototype takes 6–8 weeks. Once the prototype is established, subsequent locations take 4–5 weeks each. Timelines assume timely design approval and material procurement.

---

**Q3: Do you handle DGDA pharmacy licensing compliance?**

Yes. Woodex designs pharmacies to meet DGDA (Drugs General Directorate of Agriculture) and PBMC (Punjab Board of Management and Control) pharmacy licensing requirements — controlled drug storage, dispensing area dimensions, hygiene standards, signage and security. We review compliance at the design stage and ensure the finished pharmacy meets licensing standards.

---

**Q4: Can you fit out multiple pharmacy locations for a chain?**

Yes — and this is one of Woodex's key strengths. We develop a repeatable pharmacy prototype — counter design, storage system, finishes, lighting, signage — that can be deployed across multiple locations. The prototype is designed once, approved once, and replicated at each site with consistent quality and faster timelines.

---

**Q5: What is included in a pharmacy fit-out versus a renovation?**

A fit-out is a complete build-out of a bare shell or new-build unit into a functioning pharmacy — partitions, ceilings, flooring, counter, storage, HVAC, lighting, finishes, security, branding. A renovation is an upgrade of an existing occupied pharmacy — counter replacement, finishes refresh, layout adjustments, usually without shutting down entirely. If your pharmacy already exists and you want to upgrade it, see [Pharmacy Renovation](/renovation/). If you're starting from a shell, this is the right page.

---

**Q6: Can Woodex handle furniture and joinery for the pharmacy?**

Yes. Our sister brand Woodex Furniture™ manufactures custom dispensing counters, controlled drug cabinets, shelving systems, back-office furniture and storage units — all custom-fitted to your pharmacy's dimensions and workflow. This eliminates third-party delays and ensures joinery matches the overall design.

---

**Q7: Do you work on pharmacies outside Lahore?**

Yes. While our studio is in Lahore (Model Town), we have delivered pharmacy fit-outs across Punjab and in other cities including Islamabad, Karachi and Faisalabad. Site visits for projects outside Lahore are arranged separately. Remote design collaboration (3D renders, virtual meetings) keeps the process efficient.

---

**Q8: What if I only need a dispensing counter — not a full fit-out?**

Woodex Furniture™ can manufacture and install a custom dispensing counter as a standalone product — L-shaped, U-shaped or island layout, with controlled drug storage integrated. Contact us for a counter-only quote. For a full pharmacy fit-out including partitions, flooring, HVAC, lighting and finishes, this is the right page.
