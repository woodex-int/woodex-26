# Update Master Business Model

## Overview

This file updates the existing business model with the new service architecture completed across Phases 4–6. It reflects the full scope of Woodex Interior's capabilities as documented across all produced pages.

## 1. Service Architecture Summary

### Core Services (Phase 3)
- Interior Design (per sq.ft. or % of project cost: 3–6%)
- Fit-Out & Renovation (per sq.ft., varies by spec: Basic/Mid/Premium)
- Architecture (lump sum or % of construction cost: 2–5%)
- 3D Visualization (per render/animation: fixed pricing)

### Sister Brand: WOODEX FURNITURE™ (Phase 5 addition)
- Custom kitchens, wardrobes, media walls, office furniture
- Price ranges: Kitchens 500K–5M, Wardrobes 300K–3M, Media Walls 200K–2M, Office Furniture 100K–2M, Custom Furniture 50K–1M, Joinery 100K–3M
- Revenue: Direct sales (woodexfurniture.pk), 25–40% attach rate, retail showroom

### 3D Visualization Studio (Phase 5 addition)
- Photorealistic renders, animations, virtual tours, sciography
- Pricing: Single interior render 20K–50K, exterior render 30K–80K, 30 sec animation 100K–300K, virtual tour 50K–200K, full project 200K–500K

### Commercial Fit-Out Sectors (Phase 5)
- Pharmacy Fit-Out: Basic PKR 5.5M–7M, Mid PKR 7M–9M, Premium PKR 9M–12M per 1,000 sq ft
- Retail Fit-Out: Basic PKR 7.5M–10.5M, Mid PKR 10.5M–13.5M, Luxury PKR 15M–22.5M per 1,500 sq ft
- Restaurant Fit-Out: Basic Café PKR 14M–18M, Mid-Spec PKR 18M–24M, Premium PKR 24M–32M per 2,000 sq ft
- Healthcare Fit-Out: Basic Clinic PKR 9M–12M, Mid-Spec PKR 12M–15M, Premium Lab PKR 15M–18M per 1,500 sq ft

### Corporate Sector Pricing (Phase 6 addition)
- Office Interior Design: Starting from PKR 250,000 for small offices
- Corporate Fit-Out: 4–8 weeks (larger projects 8–12 weeks)
- Renovation: 3–6 weeks

### Sector-Specific Pricing (Phase 6)
- Software Houses: PKR 2M–4M (1,000 sq ft), PKR 4M–8M (2,000–3,000 sq ft)
- Corporate Offices: Design from PKR 250,000, fit-out quoted after survey
- Retail: Design from PKR 150,000–250,000 for small shops
- F&B: Design from PKR 200,000 for cafes, fit-out PKR 2M–32M depending on type
- Healthcare: Design from PKR 150,000–250,000 for single-doctor clinic, fit-out PKR 1.5M–15M+
