## SECTION 2 — WHAT IS APARTMENT RENOVATION?

**H2:** What Is Apartment Renovation?

Apartment renovation is the upgrading of an existing apartment — remodelling the kitchen, updating bathrooms, replacing flooring, reconfiguring layouts, refreshing finishes, or completely transforming the space. Unlike interior design (planning the layout first) or fit-out (building from shell), apartment renovation works within the existing structure to make your home better.

Woodex delivers apartment renovation from assessment to handover. We survey your apartment, understand what you want changed, design the renovation (including 3D visualization so you see the result before we start), handle all construction, finishes and fixtures, and deliver an apartment that feels new — without the cost or hassle of moving.

Every apartment renovation Woodex delivers is planned around two priorities: your lifestyle (the renovation must work for how you actually live) and minimal disruption (we phase work to keep your daily routine — and your neighbours' — as normal as possible).

**What apartment renovation covers:**

- ✓ Kitchen remodelling (cabinets, countertops, backsplash, sink, lighting, flooring)
- ✓ Bathroom renovation (vanity, tiles, shower, fixtures, plumbing)
- ✓ Bedroom upgrades (wardrobes, flooring, lighting, headboard walls)
- ✓ Living room transformation (feature walls, flooring, ceiling, lighting, media units)
- ✓ Flooring replacement (tile, wood, vinyl, carpet)
- ✓ Painting and wall finishes
- ✓ Lighting and electrical upgrades
- ✓ Plumbing and fixture replacement

> **Difference: Apartment Renovation vs Interior Design vs Fit-Out**
>
> | | Apartment Renovation | Interior Design | Fit-Out |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | **What it covers** | Upgrading an existing apartment — new finishes, reconfigured layout | Planning the layout, materials, colours (the blueprint) | Building a new/empty space from shell |
> | **When you need it** | Existing apartment needs a refresh or reconfiguration | Need to plan what to do before any work begins | Moving into a new/empty apartment |
> | **Deliverables** | Built and finished apartment, handover | Design drawings, 3D renders, material schedule | Built space from shell |

> **Need the layout planned first?**
> → **[Interior Design](/interior-design/)** — plan the renovation before building it.

## SECTION 8 — APARTMENT RENOVATION vs HOUSE RENOVATION

**H2:** Apartment Renovation vs House Renovation

| | Apartment Renovation | House Renovation |
|---|---|---|
| **Building constraints** | Shared walls, building rules, elevator access, restricted hours | Full control over site and schedule |
| **Access** | Material delivery via elevator/stairs; limited staging area | Direct access, open staging |
| **Noise/work hours** | Building management restricts hours (typically 9 AM–5 PM) | Flexible scheduling |
| **Scope** | Typically cosmetic to moderate structural | Full structural reconfiguration possible |
| **Timeline** | Longer due to phased work and access constraints | Faster for equivalent scope |
| **Cost** | May include building coordination fees | No building coordination fees |

## SECTION 9 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Apartment Renovation — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: How long does apartment renovation take?**

Most apartment renovations are completed in 4–10 weeks from design approval to handover. Single room: 2–3 weeks. Kitchen: 3–4 weeks. Full apartment: 6–10 weeks.

---

**Q2: Can I live in my apartment during renovation?**

Yes — we phase work room-by-room so you can continue living in the apartment while we renovate one area at a time.

---

**Q3: Do I need building management approval?**

Yes — most buildings require advance notice for renovation work, elevator booking and restricted working hours. We coordinate all of this with building management on your behalf.

---

**Q4: What is the difference between apartment and house renovation?**

Apartment renovation works within building constraints — shared walls, restricted hours, elevator access. House renovation has full site control. See the comparison table above.

---

**Q5: How much does apartment renovation cost in Lahore?**

Single room: PKR 400K–1.2M. Full apartment (5 Marla): PKR 1.5M–4M. Full apartment (10 Marla): PKR 2.5M–6M+. Exact pricing after free survey.

---

**Q6: Do you handle plumbing and electrical work?**

Yes. All plumbing (kitchen sink, bathroom vanity, shower) and electrical (lighting, outlets, switches) is designed and executed in-house.

---

**Q7: Can I renovate just the kitchen and bathroom?**

Yes. Many apartment renovations focus on kitchen and bathroom — the two rooms that add the most value. We can scope and price these individually.

---

**Q8: Do you offer a warranty?**

Yes. All Woodex apartment renovation work carries a 2-year warranty — construction, fixtures and finishes.
