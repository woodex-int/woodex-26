### 1 Kanal — The Premium Family Home

A 1 Kanal plot (1,000 sq yards / ~9,000 sq ft) is the entry point to premium residential living in Lahore. At this scale — typically 50–75 feet wide — the design possibilities open up significantly: fully separate drawing and dining rooms, a large kitchen with utility room, 4–5 bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms and walk-in wardrobes, a dedicated servant quarter with independent access, 2+ car garage, and generous garden or courtyard space.

Most 1 Kanal houses in Lahore's premium neighbourhoods (DHA Phase 6–9, Bahria Golf City, Gulberg, Model Town) are two storeys with a possible third floor or basement. The ground floor typically holds the grand entrance, drawing room, dining room, study/office, kitchen with utility, guest bathroom, servant quarter and double garage. The first floor holds the master suite (bedroom, walk-in wardrobe, en-suite bathroom, dressing area), additional bedrooms (2–3) with en-suites, family lounge, laundry and storage.

The design challenge at this scale is not fitting things in — it is getting the proportions right, managing the relationship between public and private zones, ensuring the house feels human-scale despite its size, and creating a cohesive architectural language across a large footprint. A 1 Kanal house designed without architectural oversight often becomes a collection of large rooms without flow or hierarchy.

## SECTION 3 — DESIGNING A PREMIUM HOME ON A 1 KANAL PLOT

**H2:** Designing a Premium Home on a 1 Kanal Plot

A 1 Kanal plot — 1,000 square yards, roughly 50 to 75 feet wide — is where premium residential design in Lahore truly begins. This is the scale of DHA's best plots, Bahria Golf City's premier lots, and Gulberg's established family homes. At this width, you have the luxury of genuine separation: the public entertaining zone (drawing, dining, study) can be fully distinct from the private family zone (bedrooms, family lounge). The master suite can have its own wing. The kitchen can have a proper utility room. The servant quarter can have its own entrance.

Most 1 Kanal houses are two storeys, sometimes with a basement or a third floor. The ground floor is the public and service floor: grand entrance, drawing room, dining room, study, kitchen with utility, guest bathroom, servant quarter, double garage. The first floor is the private family floor: master suite (bedroom, walk-in wardrobe, en-suite bathroom, dressing area), 2–3 additional bedrooms with en-suites, family lounge, laundry, storage.

The design challenge at this scale is hierarchy and flow. With so much space, it is easy to lose the human scale — to create a house that feels like a hotel rather than a home. The architect's job is to create zones within the house: formal entertaining, informal family living, private sleeping, service. Each zone has its own character, its own light, its own relationship to the garden. The circulation between zones must be intuitive — not a maze of corridors.

**Design priority cards:**

| Priority | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------------|
| **Grand Entrance** | The arrival sequence sets the tone — foyer, double-height volume, staircase as sculpture |
| **Public-Private Separation** | Ground floor = entertaining & service; First floor = family & bedrooms — clear, intuitive |
| **Master Suite** | Bedroom + walk-in wardrobe + en-suite + dressing — a true retreat, not just a large bedroom |
| **Kitchen + Utility** | Full kitchen with pantry, separate utility/laundry room, connection to dining and servant access |
| **Garage & Service** | 2+ car garage integrated into design; service yard, driver room, generator space — all planned |
| **Landscape Design** | Garden, courtyard, boundary wall, gate, lighting — the outdoor rooms are as important as indoor |

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** 1 Kanal House Design — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What can I build on a 1 Kanal plot?**

A 1 Kanal plot (1,000 sq yd) typically supports a 2-storey premium home with 4–5 bedrooms, master suite with walk-in wardrobe and en-suite, drawing room, dining room, study, kitchen with utility and pantry, servant quarter with independent access, double garage, and generous garden or courtyard. A third floor or basement may be possible depending on bylaws.

---

**Q2: How much does a 1 Kanal house design cost?**

Woodex charges PKR 600,000–1,000,000 for a complete 1 Kanal house design — concept plans, 3D renders, working drawings, elevations and authority submission preparation. Front elevation only is PKR 50,000–150,000. Fees are lump sum — no hidden charges.

---

**Q3: How is a 1 Kanal house different from a 10 Marla house?**

A 1 Kanal plot is double a 10 Marla plot (1,000 vs 500 sq yd). Key differences at 1 Kanal: master suite with walk-in wardrobe and dressing area, dedicated study/home office, full utility room separate from kitchen, independent servant quarter, double garage, larger garden, and grander room proportions throughout. Design fee: PKR 600K–1M vs PKR 400K–600K.

---

**Q4: How long does the design take?**

A 1 Kanal house design typically takes 6–10 weeks from the initial brief to final working drawings. The larger floor area, more rooms and greater design complexity require more development time. Authority approval is separate (2–6 weeks for LDA / society).

---

**Q5: Can I build a basement on a 1 Kanal plot?**

Yes, basements are possible on 1 Kanal plots and are increasingly popular for home theatres, gyms, additional guest suites or parking. Whether a basement is allowed depends on the water table, soil conditions and local bylaws. We assess feasibility during the site visit and include basement design in the scope if viable.

---

**Q6: Do you design for specific housing societies (DHA, Bahria, etc.)?**

Yes. We design for all major Lahore housing societies — DHA (all phases), Bahria Town, Bahria Golf City, Gulberg, Model Town, Wapda Town, and others. Each society has its own bylaws, setback requirements, height limits and approval processes. We design to the specific society's regulations and handle the relevant authority submission.

---

**Q7: Can I use my own contractor?**

Yes. Our working drawings are contractor-independent — they follow standard architectural conventions and include enough detail for any qualified construction team. You are not obligated to use Woodex for construction.

---

**Q8: Can Woodex design my 1 Kanal house AND do the interior design?**

Yes. Woodex handles architecture, interior design, fit-out and furniture (via Woodex Furniture™) under one roof. The architect works directly with the interior designer and fit-out team — no re-briefing. For everything under one contract: [Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/).
