# PART 1 — PAGE STRATEGY & SEO SPECIFICATIONS

## 1.1 Page Purpose

| Job | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **SEO** | Rank for "interior designers Lahore", "fit-out contractors Lahore", "Woodex Lahore" — the intent: someone in Lahore searching for who to fit out, renovate or design their home or office locally |
| **Education** | Help the visitor understand why Woodex is the preferred choice in Lahore — local expertise, quick response, coordinated team, PCATP compliance |
| **Conversion** | Route the visitor to service pages matching their need — fit-out (pharmacy/retail/restaurant/healthcare), renovation, architecture, interior design |

### Critical Keyword Differentiation

```
/lahore-location/          → LAHORE LOCATION (local expertise)
"Where can I find interior designers in Lahore?"

/woodex-furniture/          → FURNITURE (joinery component)
"Need custom kitchens?"

/project-case-study-template/ → CASE STUDIES (past work)
"Show me restaurant projects"
```

**Anti-cannibalisation rule:** Lahore location = "Local expertise in Lahore for your interior project." This page must never overlap with sister brand pages (woodex-furniture) or project case studies (project-case-study-template). Every paragraph must reinforce that this is about local service delivery in Lahore, not product manufacturing or past projects.

### The Woodex Lahore Differentiators

1. **Lahore heartland expertise** — Woodex is based in Model Town, serving across Lahore — quick response, site visits within hours, local licensing and authority submissions
2. **Coordinated team on-site** — Architect + Interior Designer + Fit-Out Manager + Furniture + 3D Studio working together — no third-party coordination delays
3. **PCATP-certified architects** — Registered with Punjab PCB — authority submissions handled locally
4. **In-house furniture manufacturing** — Woodex Furniture™ produces all joinery locally — custom design, local production, fast delivery
5. **Portfolio of landmark projects** — Restaurants, offices, clinics, showrooms across Lahore — local presence, local success stories
