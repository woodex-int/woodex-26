## SECTION 2 — WHAT MASTER PLANNING COVERS

**H2:** What Master Planning Covers

Master planning is the process of planning a piece of land — the big-picture layout before any individual building is designed. Whether you are laying out a housing scheme, a residential campus, a farmhouse estate, or a commercial development, the master plan decides how the land is divided into plots, how roads connect them, where parks and amenities go, and how water, sewerage, power and gas reach every plot.

Woodex prepares master plans from site analysis through to an authority-ready layout plan. We deliver: site analysis and feasibility (what can legally and practically be developed on the land), a concept master plan showing the overall vision for the land — land use zones, road hierarchy, open spaces, community facilities; a detailed plot layout plan (plot sizes, shapes, entitlements per authority bylaws), and the infrastructure plan — water supply, sewerage, storm drainage, power (including transformers), gas, and street lighting — laid out to serve every plot.

Every master plan Woodex produces is built on local requirements: LDA and town planning bylaws (plot sizes and minimum entitlements for private housing schemes in Punjab), nazul / Board of Revenue requirements (park and amenity land dedication, community facilities), road hierarchy standards (service lanes, collector roads, main arteries), and infrastructure standards (water supply design, sewerage gravity design, storm drainage for Lahore's rainfall). The result is a scheme that is legal, buildable and sellable.

**Checklist:**

- ✓ Site analysis and feasibility study
- ✓ Land use zoning and road hierarchy
- ✓ Plot layout (sizes, shapes, entitlements)
- ✓ Open spaces, parks and community amenities
- ✓ Infrastructure: water, sewerage, drainage, power, gas, street lighting
- ✓ Authority approvals (LDA, town planning, nazul, sector)

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Master Planning — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What is the difference between master planning and architecture?**

Master planning is the big-picture layout of land — plots, roads, utilities, infrastructure, phasing — for a scheme, campus or estate. Architecture is the design of individual buildings — their structure, layout, facade and authority approvals. You need a master plan to divide and service the land; you need architecture once you are designing specific buildings on it.

---

**Q2: What approvals does a private housing scheme need in Punjab?**

Private housing schemes require approvals from the relevant development authority (LDA and/or town planning authority), compliance with the Punjab Private Housing Schemes and Land Sub-Division Rules, nazul/Board of Revenue requirements for park and amenity land, and sector-specific rules. Plot sizes and entitlements (parks, mosques, schools, graveyards, commercial land) are regulated. Woodex prepares the layout and submission documents and manages the process.

---

**Q3: How many plots can I get from my land?**

The number of plots depends on the gross area (less roads, parks, amenities and community facilities), the plot size mix, and the mandatory entitlement deductions set by the authority. As a working rule, sellable area is typically 55–65% of gross area after roads, parks and amenity land. Woodex calculates the definitive plot count during feasibility and concept design.

---

**Q4: How long does a master plan take?**

A typical master plan takes 8–16 weeks from brief to final plan, depending on scheme size and land complexity. Authority approval times are separate and vary by authority — LDA scheme approvals can take several months. We manage submissions and iterate with the authority on your behalf.

---

**Q5: Do you provide infrastructure design (water, sewerage, power)?**

Yes. Infrastructure is a core part of the master plan — water supply network, sewerage (gravity design), storm drainage, power (transformers and feeders), gas and street lighting are all laid out to serve every plot. For large schemes, deeper engineering design is coordinated through qualified consultants.

---

**Q6: Can you produce a marketing/visual master plan for investors?**

Yes. Our in-house 3D Studio produces aerial visualizations, flythrough videos and a presentation master plan — plot-coloured layouts, phased development imagery — ideal for investor proposals, social media and authority presentations.

---

**Q7: Can Woodex also design the buildings on the scheme?**

Yes — and this is one of our key advantages. The same firm that plans your scheme designs its buildings: entrance gates, boundary walls, model homes, community facilities, and any commercial buildings. You deal with one team from land layout to building design. [View Architecture Services →](/architecture/)

---

**Q8: I only have a small plot. Do I need a master plan?**

For a single residential plot, you need architecture (house design), not a master plan. Master planning applies when you have a larger piece of land — a scheme, campus, farmhouse estate or commercial development — that needs to be divided and serviced. If in doubt, describe your land in the form and we will advise the right service.
