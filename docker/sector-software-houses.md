It does NOT target service-specific keywords like "office interior design" or "office fit-out" — those belong to the individual service pages. Every paragraph must end with a link to the appropriate service page.

## SECTION 6 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Software House — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: How is a software house office different from a corporate office?**

Software houses typically need open-plan layouts, collaboration zones, phone booths, acoustic pods and casual breakout areas — reflecting a creative, flat-hierarchy culture. Corporate offices tend toward private cabins, formal boardrooms and a more structured layout. We design for both.

---

**Q2: How much does a software house fit-out cost in Lahore?**

Fit-out costs depend on area and spec. For a 1,000 sq ft software house, expect PKR 2M–4M. For 2,000–3,000 sq ft, PKR 4M–8M. Interior design and renovation are quoted separately after a free site survey.

---

**Q3: Can you design a workspace for a 10-person startup?**

Absolutely. We work with startups and small teams as well as established companies. A 10-person workspace typically needs 500–1,000 sq ft and can be designed and built in 3–5 weeks.

---

**Q4: Do you handle acoustic treatment for meeting pods?**

Yes. Acoustic treatment — soundproofing, acoustic panels, phone booths, meeting pods — is a core part of our tech workspace design. We specify and install the right acoustic solutions for your needs.

---

**Q5: Do you build server rooms?**

Yes. We build the physical space — raised flooring, cable management, power distribution, cooling — for server rooms and data closets. Actual IT equipment is your responsibility.

---

**Q6: Can we continue working during renovation?**

Yes. We phase work zone-by-zone to minimize disruption to your team. For shell fit-outs, the space is typically unoccupied.

---

**Q7: What is the difference between office renovation and fit-out?**

Renovation upgrades an existing office. Fit-out builds a shell space from scratch. [Office Renovation](/renovation/) | [Office Fit-Out](/fit-out/)

---

**Q8: Do you offer turnkey solutions for software houses?**

Yes. [Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/) — one team, one contract, from design to handover.
