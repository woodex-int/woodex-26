## SECTION 2 — WHAT WE DO FOR HEALTHCARE

**H2:** What We Do for Healthcare

Woodex designs and builds healthcare facilities across Lahore — clinics, diagnostic centres, dental practices, wellness centres and small hospitals. Every healthcare space we create is built around three priorities: clinical safety (infection control, medical gas, ventilation), patient experience (calm, clean, professional), and operational efficiency (clinical workflow, equipment placement, staff circulation).

Healthcare interior projects demand specialist knowledge that generic fit-out firms do not have. Clinical-grade flooring, anti-microbial surfaces, hand-wash stations, medical gas outlets, radiology room shielding, dental suction and compressor systems, and health department compliance — Woodex handles all of this, and delivers a facility your patients and staff will trust.

**What Woodex does for healthcare:**

- ✓ Consultation room design (1 or multiple doctors)
- ✓ Reception, waiting area and patient flow planning
- ✓ Diagnostic lab and sample collection design
- ✓ Dental clinic design (chairs, suction, X-ray room, sterilization)
- ✓ Treatment and procedure room build-outs
- ✓ Infection control (anti-microbial surfaces, hand-wash stations)
- ✓ Medical gas, ventilation and shielding (where required)
- ✓ Staff areas, storage and back-of-house

## SECTION 6 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Healthcare — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What types of healthcare facilities does Woodex build?**

Clinics, polyclinics, diagnostic centres, dental clinics, wellness centres, day hospitals, pharmacies and specialty centres (eye, ENT, physiotherapy, dialysis, etc.).

---

**Q2: How much does clinic interior design cost in Lahore?**

Design fees start from PKR 150,000–250,000 for a single-doctor clinic. Fit-out costs range from PKR 1.5M (single clinic) to PKR 15M+ (day hospital). Quoted after free survey.

---

**Q3: Do you handle infection control requirements?**

Yes. Anti-microbial surfaces, clinical-grade flooring, hand-wash stations, clinical drainage and cleanable materials are designed and installed as standard.

---

**Q4: Do you install medical gas systems?**

Yes — oxygen, suction and compressed air for facilities that require them, coordinated with qualified consultants where certification is needed.

---

**Q5: Do you handle healthcare ventilation?**

Yes. HEPA filtration, pressure differentials and fresh air for operating theatres, procedure rooms and labs. Standard clinics receive appropriate general ventilation.

---

**Q6: How long does a healthcare fit-out take?**

Most healthcare fit-outs are completed in 4–12 weeks from design approval to handover. Single clinics: 4–6 weeks. Polyclinics: 6–9 weeks. Day hospitals: 9–12 weeks.

---

**Q7: What is the difference between healthcare renovation and fit-out?**

Renovation upgrades an existing facility. Fit-out builds a shell space from scratch. [Healthcare Renovation](/healthcare-renovation/) | [Healthcare Fit-Out](/healthcare-fit-out/)

---

**Q8: Do you offer turnkey solutions for healthcare?**

Yes. [Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/) — one team, one contract, from design to handover.
