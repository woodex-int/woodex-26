# PART 1 — PAGE STRATEGY & SEO SPECIFICATIONS

## 1.1 Page Purpose

This page has **three jobs** running simultaneously:

| Job | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **SEO** | Rank for "commercial renovation Lahore" and related searches — distinct from Office Renovation (single-tenant offices), Retail Renovation (trading spaces) and Fit-Out (new shell completion) |
| **Education** | Help landlords, tenants and property owners understand what commercial renovation covers — including tenant-improvement works, common-area upgrades and use-conversion |
| **Conversion** | Move the visitor from reading to requesting an assessment or property survey |

### Critical Keyword Differentiation

```
/office-renovation/        → SINGLE-TENANT OFFICE UPGRADES
"I have an office I want to modernize"

/commercial-renovation/    → MULTI-USE, LANDLORD & TENANT PROPERTY RENOVATION
"I have a commercial property — building, lobby, mixed-use, TI works"

/retail-renovation/        → TRADING / CUSTOMER-FACING RETAIL SPACES
"I have a shop or showroom I need to renovate while trading"
```

Every paragraph on this page must reinforce that this service covers **commercial properties broadly** — buildings, lobbies, mixed-use, landlord-funded TI works and space-use conversions — not single-tenant offices (see Office Renovation) or trading retail spaces (see Retail Renovation).

### Dual Audience — Landlord vs Tenant

This is the **only renovation page** that speaks to two distinct buyer types:

| Audience | Primary need |
|---|---|
| **Landlord / Property Owner** | Raise lettable value, prepare for new tenant, upgrade common areas, comply with regulations |
| **Tenant / Business** | Refurbish leased space, execute TI works, convert to new use, modernize branch |

The page must serve both audiences without confusing either. Section headers and card copy should address each group directly.

## SECTION 2 COPY — WHAT IS COMMERCIAL RENOVATION

**H2:**

> What Is Commercial Renovation?

**Paragraph 1:**

> Commercial renovation is the process of upgrading, refurbishing and modernizing a commercial property — including lobbies, common areas, tenant spaces, facades and building systems. It applies to office buildings, mixed-use properties, commercial plazas, medical centers, banks and any non-residential building that needs its interior or common areas refreshed, reconfigured or brought up to current compliance standards.

**Paragraph 2:**

> Unlike single-tenant office renovation (which focuses on one business's workspace), commercial renovation operates at the property level — addressing the building as a whole. This includes landlord-funded base-build standardization, tenant-improvement (TI) works for incoming tenants, use-conversion projects (transforming a space from one commercial use to another), and common-area upgrades that raise the building's overall lettable value and tenant appeal.

**Paragraph 3:**

> Woodex Interior structures commercial renovation in phases — by zone, by floor or out-of-hours — so occupied tenants continue operating while work progresses. This phasing is planned and documented before the contract is signed. For landlord projects, it protects tenancy timelines. For tenant projects, it protects business continuity. It is a Woodex core strength.

**Difference callout box:**

> **Commercial Renovation** → Property-level upgrades: lobbies, common areas, tenant spaces, facades, use-conversions
> **Office Renovation** → Single-tenant office modernization
> **Retail Renovation** → Trading and customer-facing retail spaces
> **Commercial Fit-Out** → Completing a new or shell commercial space for first occupation
>
> Woodex Interior offers all five — independently or combined. This page covers **property-wide commercial renovation for landlords and tenants.**

## SECTION 3 COPY — WHO THIS SERVICE IS FOR

**H2:**

> Who This Service Is For

**Subtext:**

> Woodex Interior provides commercial renovation for landlords, property owners, tenants and businesses across Lahore and Pakistan.

**Card 1 — Landlord preparing for new tenant (TI works):**

> You own a commercial property and a new tenant is moving in. The space needs base-build standardization — new ceilings, floors, walls and services — to meet the lease handover condition. You need a contractor who delivers to a programme that protects the tenancy start date.

**Card 2 — Business upgrading its branch, office or showroom:**

> Your business operates in a leased commercial space and the interior needs modernizing. You need a renovation partner who works around your operations — phased delivery, out-of-hours scheduling and a clear programme.

**Card 3 — Property owner raising rental and lettable value:**

> Your building's finishes, common areas or overall condition are below current market standards. Strategic renovation — lobby refresh, corridor upgrades, washroom modernization — can raise rental rates and reduce vacancy.

**Card 4 — Building owner updating common areas and facade:**

## SECTION 9 COPY — COST FACTORS

**H2:**

> What Affects the Cost of Commercial Renovation in Lahore?

**Intro:**

> Commercial renovation costs vary based on property type, size, scope and compliance requirements. The following are the main factors Woodex Interior evaluates when preparing your BOQ.

**Factor 1 — Property Use and Conversion Type:**

> The current use and intended use of the property determine the scope of work. A like-for-like refurbishment (office to office) is simpler and less costly than a use-conversion (retail to office, warehouse to showroom), which requires structural changes, MEP reconfiguration and compliance upgrades.

**Factor 2 — Condition of Existing Property:**

> The current condition of the building — structural, MEP, surface and compliance — determines how much must be replaced versus upgraded. Older properties typically require more MEP work and compliance remediation.

**Factor 3 — Total Area (sq. ft.):**

> The total square footage in scope is a primary cost driver. Larger properties require more materials, more trade-hours and more project management time. Cost scales predictably per square foot — but the rate varies with specification and complexity.

**Factor 4 — Scope: Cosmetic vs Structural:**

> A cosmetic refresh — paint, flooring, lighting, signage — is the most economical route. Full structural renovation — new partitions, ceilings, MEP overhaul and complete reconfiguration — costs significantly more but fundamentally transforms the property.

**Factor 5 — MEP and Compliance Requirements:**

> Electrical rewiring, HVAC upgrade, fire-system installation, plumbing and accessibility compliance are the most variable cost elements. Properties that have not been maintained typically require significantly more MEP investment.

**Factor 6 — TI vs Owner-Funded Standard:**

> Tenant-improvement (TI) works are typically scoped to the lease agreement — a base-build standard for handover. Owner-funded renovations scope to the landlord's value-add goals. The scope, specification and therefore cost differ significantly between these two models.

**Note box:**

> Commercial renovation is almost always more cost-effective than new construction or relocation. Woodex Interior prepares a detailed, itemized BOQ before any work begins — every trade, every zone, every item costed individually. No lump sums with no breakdown.
