### 2 Kanal — Estate-Scale Luxury

A 2 Kanal plot (2,000 sq yards / ~18,000 sq ft) is the largest commonly available residential plot in Lahore — the scale of family compounds, estate homes and landmark residences in DHA, Bahria Golf City, Gulberg and Cantonment areas. At 75–100+ feet wide, the design is no longer a "house" in the conventional sense — it is a small estate.

At this scale, Woodex designs homes that include: 5–6+ bedrooms with full master wings, separate guest wings, home theatres, gyms, indoor pools or pavilions, extensive staff quarters, 3+ car garages often with porte-cochère entrances, and estate-scale landscape architecture. Ground floors are organised around a grand central circulation — often a double-height entrance hall — with entertainment, family, study and service zones clearly differentiated.

The design challenge at 2 Kanal is entirely different from smaller plots: managing scale. A 2 Kanal house can easily become an oversized, cold collection of very large rooms. The architect's task is to maintain human scale — intimate family rooms coexist with grand entertaining spaces; the master wing is a private retreat; circulation is choreographed like a journey through the house; light and landscape are designed as integral elements, not afterthoughts.

## SECTION 3 — DESIGNING AN ESTATE HOME ON A 2 KANAL PLOT

**H2:** Designing an Estate Home on a 2 Kanal Plot

A 2 Kanal plot — 2,000 square yards, roughly 75 to 100 feet wide — is the largest residential plot commonly available in Lahore. It is the scale of family compounds and landmark homes in DHA, Bahria Golf City, Gulberg and Cantonment areas. At this footprint, the design is a small estate: main residence, guest accommodation, staff quarters, and estate-scale landscape organised as one coherent composition.

Ground floors at this scale are organised around grand central circulation. A double-height entrance hall sets the tone. From it, the plan unfolds into distinct zones: the formal entertaining zone (drawing, dining, study), the family living zone (family lounge, den, library), the master wing, the guest wing, the service zone (kitchen, pantry, utility, staff quarters), and the amenities (home theatre, gym, pool pavilion). Each zone has its own character and its own relationship to the garden.

The central design challenge at 2 Kanal is managing scale. A large house fails when it becomes a collection of oversized, over-cold rooms. Woodex's architects maintain human scale through careful room proportions, layering (grand public rooms lead to intimate family rooms), designed sightlines through the landscape, and a choreographed circulation experience — the journey through the house is designed, not accidental.

**Design priority cards:**

| Priority | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------------|
| **Grand Circulation** | Double-height entrance hall, primary staircase as sculptural element, intuitive journey through zones |
| **Master Wing** | Bedroom + walk-in wardrobe + dressing area + sitting room + private terrace + en-suite — a genuine retreat |
| **Guest Wing** | Self-contained guest accommodation with bedroom, bathroom, sitting area, separate access |
| **Luxury Amenities** | Home theatre, gym, indoor pool / pavilion, library — designed in, not squeezed in |
| **Staff & Service** | Multiple staff quarters, service kitchen, laundry, generator room, dedicated service circulation |
| **Landscape Estate** | Formal gardens, courtyards, driveway + porte-cochère, boundary treatment, outdoor lighting |

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** 2 Kanal House Design — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What can I build on a 2 Kanal plot?**

A 2 Kanal plot (2,000 sq yd) supports an estate-scale home: 5–6+ bedrooms with a full master wing, dedicated guest wing, home theatre, gym, pool pavilion, extensive staff quarters, 3+ car garage with porte-cochère, and estate-scale landscape. A basement and third floor may be possible depending on bylaws and soil conditions.

---

**Q2: How much does a 2 Kanal house design cost?**

Woodex charges PKR 1,000,000–2,000,000 for a complete 2 Kanal design — concept plans, 3D renders, working drawings, elevations and authority submission preparation. For larger estates, we also offer fee structures of 2–5% of construction cost. Front elevation only is PKR 50,000–150,000.

---

**Q3: How is a 2 Kanal house different from a 1 Kanal house?**

A 2 Kanal plot is double a 1 Kanal plot. Key differences: full master wing with private terrace, dedicated guest wing, luxury amenities (home theatre, gym, pool), multiple staff quarters with service circulation, porte-cochère arrival, and estate-scale landscape. Design fee: PKR 1M–2M vs PKR 600K–1M.

---

**Q4: How long does the design take?**

A 2 Kanal house design typically takes 8–12 weeks from the initial brief to final working drawings — reflecting the scale and complexity of an estate project. Authority approval is separate (2–6 weeks for LDA / society).

---

**Q5: Can I include a basement?**

Yes. Basements on 2 Kanal plots commonly hold home theatres, gyms, wine cellars, additional parking or staff spaces. Feasibility depends on the water table, soil conditions and local bylaws. We assess this during the site visit and include basement design in the scope if viable.

---

**Q6: Do you design for specific housing societies?**

Yes. We design for all major Lahore societies — DHA (all phases), Bahria Town, Bahria Golf City, Gulberg, Model Town, Cantonment, and others. Each has its own bylaws, height limits and approval processes. We design to the specific society's regulations and handle the relevant authority submission.

---

**Q7: Can I design the landscape as part of the project?**

Yes. Landscape architecture is coordinated as part of your 2 Kanal design — formal gardens, courtyards, driveway, boundary walls, gate, outdoor lighting and planting plan. Woodex coordinates landscape with the building design so the estate reads as one composition.

---

**Q8: Can Woodex design my 2 Kanal house AND do the interior design?**

Yes. Woodex handles architecture, interior design, fit-out and furniture (via Woodex Furniture™) under one roof — essential at this scale, where hundreds of design decisions must stay coordinated. The architect works directly with the interior designer. For everything under one contract: [Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/).
