## 1.1 Page Purpose

This page has **three jobs**:

| Job | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **SEO** | Rank for "residential architect Lahore", "house design Pakistan", "home architect Lahore" — the intent: a homeowner or buyer looking for someone to design their house from scratch |
| **Education** | Help the visitor understand what residential architecture covers (concept through working drawings), the difference between plot sizes, and what to expect at each stage |
| **Conversion** | Route homeowners to the sub-page that matches their plot size (5 Marla, 10 Marla, 1 Kanal, 2 Kanal, Farmhouse) or to a general consultation |

### Critical Keyword Differentiation

```
/residential-architecture/       → RESIDENTIAL ARCHITECTURE (house design)
"I need a new house designed — concept through drawings"

/residential-interior-design/    → RESIDENTIAL INTERIOR DESIGN (interiors)
"I need the inside of my house planned and styled"

/home-renovation/                → HOME RENOVATION (existing house upgrade)
"My existing house needs renovating"

/turnkey-design-build/           → TURNKEY (one contract, all-in-one)
"I want one team to handle everything"
```

**Anti-cannibalisation rule:** Residential architecture = "I am building a new house and need it designed." This page must never overlap with interior design (interiors), renovation (existing homes), or turnkey (one-contract bundles). Every paragraph must reinforce that architecture = building design for new construction.

### The Residential Architecture Differentiators

1. **Designed for the way you live** — bedroom placement, kitchen flow, natural light, privacy, parking, storage, servant quarters, rooftop access — all resolved at the design stage
2. **3D before construction** — photorealistic renders of your house before a single brick is laid; see it, approve it, build it
3. **Working drawings your contractor can build from** — AutoCAD / Revit construction drawings, not just pretty concept sketches
4. **Authority submissions handled** — LDA, CDA, and other approvals prepared and submitted as part of the service
5. **Seamless transition to interior design + fit-out** — the same team that designs your building can plan the interior and build it out; no re-briefing

## SECTION 2 — WHAT RESIDENTIAL ARCHITECTURE COVERS

**H2:** What Residential Architecture Covers

Residential architecture is the design of your house — the building itself. Before the interiors, before the furniture, before a single wall is painted, someone has to design the structure: the layout, the rooms, the flow, the elevations, the way light enters each space. That is what this service does.

Woodex designs houses for new construction across Lahore — from compact 5 Marla homes to sprawling 2 Kanal properties and farmhouses. Every design begins with your brief: how many bedrooms, how you cook, where you park, whether you need a servant quarter, how much privacy you want from the street. We translate that into floor plans, 3D renders, working drawings and authority submissions — everything your contractor needs to build your house.

The design is developed to construction-ready stage using AutoCAD and Revit. You receive a complete drawing set — plans, sections, elevations, structural details — that any qualified contractor can build from. No ambiguity, no "we'll figure it out on site." The house is designed before it is built.

## SECTION 4 — OUR RESIDENTIAL ARCHITECTURE PROCESS

**H2:** Our Residential Architecture Process

| Step | Title | What Happens |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 01 | **Brief & Site Visit** | We meet you — at our studio or on-site — and understand your requirements: rooms, lifestyle, budget, timeline. We check the plot dimensions, access, neighbouring structures and applicable bylaws. |
| 02 | **Concept Design** | We present floor plans and photorealistic 3D renders of your proposed house. You see the layout, the rooms, the elevations — in 3D, before construction. Two rounds of revisions are included. |
| 03 | **Design Development** | Once you approve the concept, we refine it — materials, structural grid, MEP zones, window sizes, ceiling heights. The design becomes technically resolved. |
| 04 | **Working Drawings** | Full construction drawings in AutoCAD or Revit — plans, sections, elevations, structural details, schedules. This is the package your contractor builds from. |
| 05 | **Authority Submission** | We prepare and submit drawings to LDA, CDA or the relevant authority. We manage the process until approval is received. |
| 06 | **Handover** | Complete drawing set delivered — digital files, printed sets if required. Ready for your construction team or contractor to execute. |

**Timeline:** A 5–10 Marla house typically takes 4–6 weeks from brief to final drawings. A 1–2 Kanal house takes 6–10 weeks. Authority approval is separate (2–6 weeks for LDA).

## SECTION 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:** Residential Architecture — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: How long does it take to design a house?**

A 5–10 Marla house typically takes 4–6 weeks from the initial brief to final working drawings. A 1–2 Kanal house takes 6–10 weeks. Front elevation design takes 5–10 working days. Authority approval times are separate and depend on the local authority — typically 2–6 weeks for LDA.

---

**Q2: What do I receive at the end of the architecture process?**

You receive a complete construction drawing set: floor plans, sections, elevations, structural details, and schedules — in AutoCAD or Revit format (digital files). Printed sets are available on request. These drawings are what your contractor builds from.

---

**Q3: Can I use my own contractor with Woodex drawings?**

Yes. Our working drawings are designed to be contractor-independent — they follow standard architectural conventions and include enough detail for any qualified construction team to execute. You are not obligated to use Woodex for construction.

---

**Q4: Do I need an architect for a 5 Marla house?**

Technically, you can build a 5 Marla house without an architect — but the design risk is entirely yours. A well-designed 5 Marla house maximises space, light and ventilation on a small plot; a poorly designed one feels cramped and dark. Our 5 Marla architecture service (PKR 200K–400K) ensures your compact home is well-designed from the start.

---

**Q5: What is the difference between concept design and working drawings?**

Concept design is the creative phase — floor plans, spatial layout, 3D renders. It answers "what will my house look like?" Working drawings are the technical phase — detailed construction drawings that answer "how will my house be built?" Both are included in Woodex's full architecture service.

---

**Q6: Do you handle LDA approval for house designs?**

Yes. Authority submission to LDA (Lahore Development Authority), CDA (Capital Development Authority) or other relevant local body is included in our full architecture service. We prepare the submission, handle the process and manage until approval is received. Government submission fees are passed through at cost.

---

**Q7: Can Woodex design my house AND do the interior design?**

Yes — and this is one of Woodex's key advantages. We are one of the few firms in Lahore that handles architecture, interior design, fit-out and furniture (via Woodex Furniture™) under one roof. The architect who designs your house works directly with the interior designer. For a fully integrated experience, our [Turnkey Design & Build](/turnkey-design-build/) service covers everything under one contract.

---

**Q8: What if I only need a front elevation — not a full house design?**

Woodex offers front elevation design as a standalone service — PKR 50,000–150,000. You receive a photorealistic 3D render and 2D elevation drawing of your house's front facade. This is ideal if your floor plans are already finalised but you want a professional, designed exterior. [View Front Elevation Design →](/front-elevation-design/)
