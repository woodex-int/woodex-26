# PART 1 — PAGE STRATEGY & SEO SPECIFICATIONS

## 1.1 Page Purpose and Strategic Role

The Turnkey Solutions page is the **highest-value service page** in the entire Woodex website.

Every other service page describes one part of what Woodex does:

- Design
- Fit-Out
- Renovation
- 3D Visualization
- Furniture

The Turnkey page describes **all of it — under one contract — managed by one team.**

This means the Turnkey page is not simply another service page. It is the **master conversion page** — the destination for any client who wants the complete Woodex capability applied to their project without the complexity of managing multiple contractors, multiple contracts and multiple handoffs.

---

## 1.2 The Turnkey Client — Specific Psychology

The turnkey visitor is different from any other visitor on the Woodex site:

```
DESIGN-ONLY VISITOR          TURNKEY VISITOR
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Wants a plan and drawings     Wants everything done
Will manage execution later   Cannot manage trades himself
Has contractor relationships  Has no contractor relationships
Budget: design fee only       Budget: complete project
Fear: wrong design            Fear: wrong contractor or chaos
Key question: What will       Key question: Can I hand this
it look like?                 over and trust it will be done?
```

The turnkey visitor is typically a **business owner, director or property manager** who:

- Has neither the time nor the expertise to coordinate multiple contractors
- Has seen or experienced poorly managed projects by others
- Is prepared to pay a premium for accountability and simplicity
- Wants one agreement, one team and one delivery

Every section on this page must speak to this psychology — **the relief of one accountable team.**

---

## 1.3 Turnkey — The Business Case for Woodex

The Turnkey service is also strategically important from a **revenue and margin perspective:**

| Service                      | Revenue type           | Margin potential |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------- |
| Design only                  | Fee-based              | Moderate         |
| Fit-out only                 | BOQ contract           | Moderate         |
| Renovation only              | BOQ contract           | Moderate         |
| **Turnkey design and build** | **Full project value** | **Highest**      |

Turnkey projects generate the highest revenue per client and the strongest gross margin — because Woodex manages procurement, coordinates all trades and delivers the complete scope under one commercial agreement.

This page must therefore do two jobs simultaneously:

1. Convert the right clients
2. Position Turnkey as the premium, preferred service — not as a more expensive version of separate services

## 1.5 The Comparison Strategy — Turnkey vs Separate

Section H2-5 is unique to this page — a **direct comparison** between the turnkey model and the conventional separate-contractor model.

This section exists because the single biggest reason clients do not choose turnkey is that they do not fully understand what it replaces — and therefore cannot evaluate whether the premium is justified.

The comparison section makes the value of turnkey tangible and specific — not abstract.

```
WHAT THE COMPARISON SECTION MUST ACHIEVE:
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Show the client exactly what they avoid when they
choose turnkey — not by criticizing competitors,
but by describing the real-world friction of
managing separate contractors on a complex project.
```

## SECTION 10 COPY — COST FACTORS

**H2:**

> What Affects the Cost of a Turnkey Project?

**Intro paragraph:**

> A turnkey project cost is a single, comprehensive figure covering the complete scope — design fees, documentation, procurement management, fit-out execution, project management, furniture supply and installation. The following eight factors directly determine the total cost of your Woodex Interior turnkey project.

---

**Factor 1 — Total Project Area**

> The total square footage of the space is the primary cost driver in any turnkey project. Every phase — design, documentation, procurement, execution and furniture — scales with the area of the project. Larger projects benefit from economies of scale in material procurement and trades management, but the total project value increases proportionally with area.

**Factor 2 — Sector Type**

> Different sectors carry fundamentally different turnkey cost profiles. A standard office turnkey project costs differently from a restaurant turnkey — which requires kitchen MEP coordination and food-safe surfaces — or a healthcare turnkey — which requires clinical-grade surfaces and specialist compliance provisions. Each sector's specific requirements are evaluated and costed individually.

**Factor 3 — Specification Level**

> Economy, mid-range and premium specification levels carry substantially different material and installation costs at every phase of a turnkey project. The choice of flooring type, ceiling specification, joinery finish, lighting brand, sanitary ware quality and furniture range all affect the total project cost significantly. Woodex Interior presents material options at multiple specification levels before the BOQ is finalized.

**Factor 4 — Design Complexity**

> A single open-plan floor with a reception and two meeting rooms requires less design time and documentation than a multi-zone environment with executive suites, a boardroom, a server room, a breakout zone, prayer facilities and a full kitchen. Design complexity affects both the fee for the design phase and the execution complexity of the build phase.

**Factor 5 — MEP Scope and Complexity**

> Electrical, HVAC and plumbing scope varies significantly by sector, space type and the condition of existing services where renovation is involved. Restaurants and healthcare facilities typically carry more intensive MEP scope than standard commercial spaces. MEP scope is evaluated during the site visit and documented in the BOQ before the contract is signed.

**Factor 6 — Custom Joinery and Millwork Extent**

> The extent of custom joinery — reception counters, feature walls, built-in storage, kitchen cabinetry, display systems and bespoke millwork — is one of the most significant cost variables in a turnkey project. Through Woodex Furniture™, all joinery is designed, manufactured and installed as part of the turnkey programme — with material and finish options at multiple price points.

**Factor 7 — Furniture Specification**

> The furniture specification — from economy commercial ranges to premium imported systems — has a significant impact on the total turnkey project value. Woodex Interior presents furniture options at multiple specification levels, confirmed in the BOQ before the contract is signed.

**Factor 8 — Programme and Timeline**

> Standard programme durations are planned to allow efficient execution across all trades without compressed scheduling. Projects requiring an accelerated timeline — due to a fixed opening date, a lease commencement date or a business event — may require additional resource allocation, which is reflected in the programme and costing before the contract is signed.

## SECTION 13 COPY — FAQ

**H2:**

> Turnkey Interior Solutions — Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q1: What does "turnkey" mean in interior design and fit-out?**

> Turnkey in interior design and fit-out means that one company manages the complete project — from design brief and 3D visualization through procurement, fit-out execution, furniture installation and formal handover. The client briefs the team once and receives a completed, furnished and inspected space. Nothing is left for the client to source, manage or coordinate independently.

---

**Q2: What is the difference between a turnkey contract and hiring separate contractors?**

> When you hire separate contractors, you manage multiple agreements, multiple programmes and multiple points of accountability. If a problem arises — a design that does not match the specification, a trade that damages another's work, a furniture item that does not fit the space — responsibility is disputed between separate parties. In a Woodex Interior turnkey contract, there is one agreement, one team and one responsibility. Every problem is managed internally — without the client becoming the arbitrator.

---

**Q3: Is turnkey more expensive than managing the project myself?**

> A turnkey contract includes a project management and coordination fee that would not appear in a collection of separate trade quotes. However, separately commissioned projects routinely carry hidden costs that a turnkey client avoids — redesign costs caused by design-to-build gaps, variation claims from trades who were not properly briefed, delays caused by poor programme coordination and the time cost of the client's own involvement in managing contractors. For most clients, the total cost of a well-managed turnkey project is comparable to or lower than the true total cost of a poorly coordinated separate-contractor project.

---

**Q4: Can I choose my own materials and finishes in a turnkey project?**

> Yes. Material and finish selection is conducted collaboratively at the design stage — with Woodex Interior presenting options at multiple specification levels and the client making all final approvals before the BOQ is finalized. The turnkey model does not remove the client from design decisions — it removes the client from coordination, procurement and project management responsibility.

---

**Q5: How does 3D visualization work within a turnkey project?**

> 3D photorealistic interior rendering is included as a standard deliverable in every Woodex Interior turnkey project. After the space plan and concept design are approved, our 3D team produces detailed renders of the complete space — typically room by room and zone by zone. You review, provide feedback and give written approval before any physical work begins. Walkthrough animations are available on request.

---

**Q6: What happens if something needs to change during the project?**

> Changes to the confirmed and approved scope are managed as formal variation orders — documented with the confirmed cost impact and timeline impact before any additional work proceeds. No scope change is implemented without written client approval. The Woodex Interior variation process is transparent, fast and documented — so there are no surprise additions to the project cost at handover.

---

**Q7: Does Woodex Interior manage furniture procurement within the turnkey contract?**

> Yes. Furniture specification, sourcing, sample approval, purchase order management, lead-time tracking and installation coordination are all managed by Woodex Interior within the turnkey programme. Through Woodex Furniture™, custom office furniture, reception counters, kitchens, wardrobes and joinery are designed, manufactured and installed as part of the same programme.

---

**Q8: How long does a turnkey project take from brief to handover?**

> Timeline depends on the size, sector and complexity of the project. A straightforward office turnkey project of 2,000–3,000 sq. ft. — covering design, documentation and fit-out — typically takes 8–14 weeks from brief to handover. Larger, more complex or more premium projects take proportionally longer. The confirmed programme and completion date are agreed before the contract is signed.

---

**Q9: Can Woodex Interior manage a turnkey project for a site outside Lahore?**

> Yes. Woodex Interior is based in Lahore and provides turnkey project delivery in Islamabad, Karachi and other cities across Pakistan. Remote project management includes regular site visits by the Woodex project manager, coordinated local trade supervision and structured reporting throughout the project. Contact us with your project location to discuss logistics and programme planning.

---

**Q10: What documentation is provided at handover?**

> At the conclusion of every Woodex Interior turnkey project, the client receives a formal handover documentation pack — including as-built drawings reflecting any site changes, MEP systems commissioning records, warranty documents for installed systems and materials, the final snagging sign-off record and a professional photograph set of the completed space.
