Cookie Policy

What this website stores in your browser and why. The short version: almost nothing, and nothing that follows you around.

Effective 19 September 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. They let a site remember things between pages and visits — for example, that a menu is open.

What this site uses today

  • Essential, functional storage only. This website is static: it stores your menu state and similar interface preferences, and nothing else.
  • No advertising cookies. None. No ad trackers run on this site.
  • No analytics cookies yet. If measurement tools (such as GA4 or Clarity) are enabled later, this page will be updated before they start, with an opt-out explained here.

Third-party links

Our WhatsApp and call buttons link to external services (for example, wa.me). Once you leave this site, the destination's own policy applies.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time; every major browser lets you do this per site. Blocking the small amount this site uses will not break anything you can see.
