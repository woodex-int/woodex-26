The structural statement

One accountable studio. Drawing to delivery.

Brand story

From a single studio in Lahore.

Woodex Interior was founded to solve the gap between design and delivery — the space where most projects lose certainty. From a single studio in Lahore, we built an integrated practice: architecture, interiors, fit-outs, 3D visualization, and furniture manufacturing under one roof. One team. One standard. One accountable partner.

Sister brand Woodex Furniture™ manufactures the bespoke pieces our drawings call for — wardrobes, kitchens, doors, joinery — so the approved drawing and the finished room stay connected.

The 7-Gate lifecycle

Seven gates. Your approval at each one.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Site survey, brief, constraints.

    You approve the brief
  2. 02

    Design

    Layout, concept, mood.

    You approve the concept
  3. 03

    Visualize

    3D renders, material boards.

    You approve the still
  4. 04

    Plan

    Budget + BOQ.

    Price locked here
  5. 05

    Build

    Construction, on-site management.

    You approve milestones
  6. 06

    Install

    Furniture, finishes, lighting.

    You approve the install plan
  7. 07

    Deliver

    Final walk-through, 30-day check.

    You sign the handover

The Stop-Gate privilege

Your renders belong to you.

If the numbers or the vision change after Gate 3, you walk away with every 3D file. The construction is optional. The design is always yours.
