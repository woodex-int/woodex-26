Definition

What Is 3D Visualization?

3D visualization is the production of photorealistic images, animations and interactive tours that show a space exactly as it will look — before it exists. Built in 3ds Max and AutoCAD, Woodex 3D Studio creates visuals indistinguishable from architectural photography — the fastest way to see, approve and sell a space before construction begins.

Woodex 3D Studio is a standalone, buyable service. The same in-house team that visualizes every Woodex project also works for external clients — architects, interior designers and developers — whether your project is designed by Woodex or by another practice.

Process

How 3D Visualization Works — 5 Steps

A structured production process — confirmed dates, agreed revisions, no surprises.

  1. 01

    Brief & Reference

    You tell us what to visualize — drawings, concept images, material references and the camera angles you need. We confirm scope, turnaround and quote.

  2. 02

    Modeling & Scene Setup

    The space is modeled in 3ds Max from your drawings or references. Furniture, fixtures and details are built to specification.

  3. 03

    Lighting & Texturing

    Materials and finishes are applied with real-world accuracy. Daylight and artificial lighting reproduce the design intent — or show the scene at its best.

  4. 04

    Render & Delivery

    Final renders are produced in marketing-ready resolution and delivered in the agreed format — on the confirmed date.

  5. 05

    Revisions & Final Output

    Up to two revision rounds are included. Materials, lighting, angles and composition are refined until the output is approved.
