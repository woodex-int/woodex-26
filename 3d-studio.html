02 — Definition

What Is 3D Visualization?

3D visualization is the production of photorealistic digital images, animations and interactive tours that show a space exactly as it will look — before it exists. Using 3ds Max, AutoCAD and advanced rendering engines, Woodex 3D Studio creates interior and exterior visuals that are indistinguishable from architectural photography. It is the fastest, cheapest way to see, approve and sell a space before construction begins.

3D visualization serves two distinct audiences. Architects, interior designers and developers commission renders to communicate design intent, win client approval and sell units before construction. Homeowners and investors commission renders to see their interior before committing to build — and to make confident material and finish decisions on screen.

Woodex 3D Studio is a standalone, buyable service — the same in-house team that visualizes every Woodex interior and turnkey project also works for external clients on a per-brief basis. Whether your project is designed by Woodex or by another practice, the 3D Studio delivers the same photorealistic quality, structured turnaround and marketing-ready output.

06 — The run

How 3D Visualization Works — 5 Steps

A structured production process — confirmed dates, agreed revisions, no surprises.

  1. 01

    Brief & Reference

    You tell us what to visualize — drawings, concept images, material references and the camera angles you need. We confirm scope, turnaround and quote.

  2. 02

    Modeling & Scene Setup

    The space is modeled in 3ds Max from your drawings or references. Furniture, fixtures and details are built to specification.

  3. 03

    Lighting & Texturing

    Materials and finishes are applied with real-world accuracy. Daylight and artificial lighting reproduce the design intent — or show the scene at its best.

  4. 04

    Render & Delivery

    Final renders are produced in marketing-ready resolution and delivered in the agreed format — on the confirmed date.

  5. 05

    Revisions & Final Output

    Up to two revision rounds are included. Materials, lighting, angles and composition are refined until the output is approved.
