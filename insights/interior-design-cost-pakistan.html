BOQ gate

Price without a BOQ is a guess

Interior design cost in Pakistan, honestly: what moves the number, what holds it, and why we quote after the survey.

14 Aug 2026 2 min read Woodex Studio · Lahore

This note will not give you a per-square-foot number, and you should distrust anyone who does before seeing your site. Interior design cost in Pakistan is real and discussable — but only after the survey. Here is what actually moves the number.

What you are paying for, in order

First site condition: what exists, what stays, what the structure and services allow. Second, joinery scope: kitchens, wardrobes, doors, paneling — joinery is where residential budgets concentrate, and where in-house manufacturing changes the price. Third, finish level: tile, stone, paint systems, lighting fixtures, sanitary ware. Same plan, three budgets — legitimately.

Why the survey comes first

A quotation written before the survey is a work of fiction with a deadline. The survey establishes dimensions, structure, damp and services — the facts that decide whether your budget spends on design or on repair. We quote after the survey. Always.

The sequence that keeps cost honest

  1. Survey — measure what exists.
  2. Design — layout and concept against a stated budget band.
  3. 3D still — approve the room, materials included.
  4. BOQ — every line item priced against the approved still.

Gate 4 is where the number locks. After that, the price does not move without your written instruction.

Gate factIf the BOQ lands above your band, the design changes — not the number's meaning. You see exactly which lines to cut, because every line is tied to something you can see in the still.

The walk-away rule, again

Because it bears repeating in any honest cost discussion: if the numbers do not work after the 3D gate, you walk away with your design files. You have paid for a design you own, not a deposit you forfeit. A studio that operates this way has no incentive to inflate the BOQ — and every reason to make the design fit the budget you actually have.
