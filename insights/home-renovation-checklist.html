Have / survey

Do not 3D a fiction

W
Woodex studio · 4 August 2026

Someone still has to live there. Survey, open up, find services. Then draw.

3D of a plan that ignores structure is fiction.

Have

What you walk in with

A lived-in house. What must stay habitable. Photos, a plan if you have one, the rooms that fail.

3D

When you see it

3D after the replan is approved — before/after of the rooms that change. Not first.
