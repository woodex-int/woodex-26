3D gate

A still is a meeting

3D visualization is not decoration for a presentation. It is the room, argued and agreed, before it exists.

18 Aug 2026 2 min read Woodex Studio · Lahore

3D visualization is the production of photorealistic images, animations and interactive tours that show a space exactly as it will look — before it exists. At Woodex it is built in-house, in 3ds Max and AutoCAD, by the same team that draws every Woodex project. Nothing is outsourced to a render farm that has never stood inside a room it was selling.

What a still actually contains

A render is not one picture. It is a set of decisions made visible: the layout, the materials, the finish schedule, the light. When you approve a still you are approving all four at once — which is why approval on screen is cheaper than approval on site. On site, a changed decision is demolition. On screen it is a revision round.

Every Woodex brief includes two revision rounds. Materials, lighting, angles and composition are refined until the output is approved — the still is a meeting, and the meeting ends with a signature.

The formats, honestly ranked

  • Interior and exterior stills — the decision picture. If you buy one thing, buy this.
  • 360° virtual tours — for remote stakeholders and pre-sales. Viewers walk the space from any device.
  • Walkthrough animations — cinematic, 30–60 seconds, for marketing and investor presentations.
  • Sciography studies — sun and shadow analysis for planning approvals and daylight questions.

The stop-gate privilege

Here is the part most studios will not put in writing: your renders belong to you. If the numbers or the vision change after the 3D gate, you walk away with every file. The construction is optional. The design is always yours.

That rule changes what a render is. It is not a hook into a build contract — it is a complete, buyable deliverable. Architects, interior designers and developers commission visualization from us even when another practice is executing the build.

Gate factBasic renders deliver in 2–5 days on a confirmed date. Files arrive as high-resolution PNG and JPG; animations as MOV and MP4.

What to bring to the first meeting

Drawings or concept references, the camera angles you need, and your deadline. From that we confirm scope, turnaround and an itemized quote within one business day. If you do not have drawings yet, start with the survey — do not 3D a fiction.
