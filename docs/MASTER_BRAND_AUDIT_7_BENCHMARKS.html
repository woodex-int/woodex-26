Brand DNA — what Woodex actually is

Do not sell interiors. Sell a path.

Most Pakistani interior sites sell rooms, catalogues and “luxury.” Woodex’s locked master is a process brand:

Master idea

Drawn. Then built.

The still is not the product. The still is the decision. The mill and the site are the product — if the brief includes them.

Promise

We turn ideas into spaces.

One partner. Concept to completion. Designers, in-house 3D, execution, Woodex Furniture — one studio, not a moodboard plus a contractor hunt.

Path

Design → Visualize → Plan → Budget → BOQ → Execute → Deliver

Stop at the gate you choose. 3D-only is a complete job. Client drawings are reviewed before they are built.

Proof that may be published

Honesty is the brand asset

500+ projects · founder ~20 years commercial interiors · execution 10+ years · ISO 9001 · three studios (Gulberg III, Clifton, F-7) · desk LG 90 Link Road, Model Town · named client only: Wellstar Pharmacy → Cosmetics → Mini Hospital, DHA Lahore. Studies stay labelled studies. No awards. No 95%. No industrial claim.

Linoxa skin, Woodex job

Benchmark 01 — Elite HTML Architect · 61/100

Semantic skeleton exists. Div factory underneath.

The brief asked for header, nav, main, section, footer — and the complete avoidance of generic div wrappers, with premium value loading without layout shift.

What is already elite

GateEvidence
Document shell66/66 pages have <header>, <nav>, <main>, <footer>. 0 missing H1. 0 missing title. 0 missing canonical. 0 missing OG.
Well-formed0 unclosed tags. 0 broken internal hrefs. 0 missing image files. JSON-LD on all 66 (BreadcrumbList on inner pages; InteriorDesignStudio on Home / About / Contact; FAQPage on Home; Article on 7 insights).
Landmarks132 nav (primary + mobile). 291 section. 351 article. 68 H1 (Home is allowed 3 — one per hero slide).
ARIAPrimary / mobile labels, mega role="menu", hero pips as tablist, WhatsApp float labelled, reduced-motion kills Ken Burns and lx-pin.

The architectural flaw

The site is a div-first composition wearing semantic clothes. Counted across 66 pages:

  • 4,389 div vs 291 section — ratio 15.1 : 1.
  • Home alone: 134 divs, 12 sections. 3D Studio: 122 divs. About: 100 divs.
  • Zero <picture>, zero <source>, zero <address>, zero <time>, zero <aside>.
  • Zero skip-to-content link.
  • Header + mega + footer are copy-pasted into every file (~8.7 KB of chrome per page × 66 ≈ 560 KB of duplicate DOM). There is no include, no component, no SSI.
  • 511 images, 0 with width + height. 221 empty alt="". loading="lazy" on 1 iframe only. fetchpriority nowhere. LCP hero images are 1408×768 JPEGs without dimensions — classic CLS.

A visitor on 4G in Lahore pays for Tailwind’s runtime CDN, Google Fonts, six CSS files on Home, then a full-viewport image that shoves the H1 after decode. Premium value is not instant. The first paint is a navy bar and a hole.

Architect SOP — do this, do not rebuild Keep the visual. Change the skeleton: (1) width/height or aspect-ratio on every img; (2) fetchpriority="high" on LCP; (3) skip link; (4) wrap the desk block in <address>; (5) stop duplicating 8 KB of mega nav — one include when the stack allows; (6) self-host Plus Jakarta + drop Tailwind CDN for a built CSS. Do not chase “zero divs.” Linoxa-grade layout needs wrappers. Chase zero anonymous wrappers: give the remaining divs roles or replace them with article / figure / ul.

Where the engine leaks identity

  • 14 images, 66 pages. Almost every JPEG is 1408×768. project-retreat.jpg is 1024×1024. Ken Burns fakes cinema. A prestige brand cannot recycle one walnut living room as residential, 3D studio, contact hero and CTA.
  • Buttons are excellent (pill + circular arrow + dual-label hover). That is owned UI. Keep it.
Identity SOP Own the tokens harder: add --bg --fg --line pairs for white-page and navy-page, not a consumer dark-mode toggle. Unify CSS load (theme + mega + one page sheet). Put light-page on every non-cine inner page. Commission or shoot 30 real stills before any restyle. Do not restyle the Home 3-slide composition or 3D Studio page — lock holds.

Benchmark 05 — Organic Lead Generation Architect · 54/100

The brief is designed. The pipe is fake.

The brief asked for a two-step framework: low-friction micro-offer, then an availability script. Semantic HTML5 contact component.

What exists

  • Three forms: Contact (#contact-form), Start (#project-form), 3D Studio brief. All preventDefault. Success note: “Frontend only.” content/brief.json points at /api/brief which does not exist.
  • Start form is commercially intelligent: have / need / area / stage / timeline / optional budget. That is the brief Woodex needs. Fields are right. Capture is not.
  • WhatsApp float on 66/66 pages, number locked 923224000768. This is the real lead channel today.
  • Call tel:+923362259477 only on About, Contact, Projects (+ case pages). Not in the global footer.
  • Hours 10:00–8:30 printed. OpeningHours schema Mon–Sat. No Sunday. No live “studio is in” indicator.
  • Contact form: HTML requires name, email, phone, message. JS only checks name, email, message. novalidate on the form. Friction loop: user thinks phone is required; JS lets them through without it — or native required is ignored.
  • Socials in every footer: Instagram / LinkedIn / Facebook all href="#". 66 pages of fake proof.
  • content/site.json still stores the old WhatsApp 9242111800800. HTML is correct. The CMS stub is a landmine.

Benchmark 07 — Data-Driven Web Systems Auditor · 52/100

No 14-day metrics. The friction loops are still visible in the markup.

The brief supplied placeholders for Unique Visitors, Bounce, Session Duration, Conversion Rate. None are instrumented on WOODEX-26. Inventing numbers would violate the same honesty lock as fake awards. Below is the technical diagnosis of what those numbers will say until the loops are cut.

Instrumentation gap

  • No Google Analytics / Plausible / GTM in any of 66 heads.
  • No conversion events (WhatsApp click, form submit, 3D CTA).
  • Forms cannot convert: they reset in the browser.
  • Therefore bounce/session/CVR over 14 days = unknown. Do not brief an agency with fiction.

Brand build — what to become

Woodex is not Linoxa in Lahore. Stop auditioning.

Positioning

The buildable design studio

Category: interior design + in-house 3D + execution + mill. Not a decorator. Not a contractor. Not an architecture magazine. Competitors sell looks. Woodex sells an approved room that can be priced and built.

Enemy

The split job

Moodboard from one firm, render farm from another, BOQ from a quantity surveyor, site from the cheapest crew. Woodex’s fight is fragmentation — said calmly, never as a smear of former partners.

Voice

Short. Specific. Unsentimental.

Rooms, gates, stills, mill, site. No “timeless blending.” No “unbeatable value.” No fake warmth. If a sentence could run on a Dubai catalogue, cut it.
