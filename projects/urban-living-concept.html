The long table in a small plan

Overview

Drawn so it can be built

An apartment that had to hold work, guests and storage without leftover sofas. Small plans fail by furnishing — a sofa for the idea of guests, a desk for the idea of working from home, and no room left for either when they actually happen.

This study takes the opposite route: one long table that is worktop, dining and gathering surface; one storage wall that absorbs everything else; and a render that proves the plan before a single piece is ordered.

Study, not a named case

Named completed work on this site: Wellstar Pharmacy, Cosmetics and Mini Hospital, DHA Lahore. Everything else stays a labelled study until a client names it.

01 · Requirement

What it had to do

What it had to do: work, guests and storage in one compact Karachi plan — no leftover sofas, no dead corners, no furniture bought for a life the residents do not live.

Had: an existing apartment with a tight floor plate, a working kitchen, and owners who both work from home.

02 · 3D

The room

The room: stills of the long table, the desk that disappears, the storage wall. Every piece drawn to its double duty — the table seats twelve at Eid and one laptop on Tuesday; the desk folds into the wall; the storage wall swallows the guest mattresses the plan has no room to display.

  • One long table dimensioned for work, dining and gathering
  • A disappearing desk drawn into the storage wall
  • Full-height storage wall with internal layout per season
  • Stills approved before any joinery order
  • Loose furniture reduced to what the render kept

03 · BOQ

Then money

Then money: joinery first, loose pieces last — the BOQ sequence that keeps a small budget honest. The storage wall is the single largest line and the single largest saving: everything the apartment does not have to store somewhere else.
