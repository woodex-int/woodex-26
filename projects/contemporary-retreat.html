Villa study

A house that can gather

A family needed guests, prayer and a kitchen for twenty — not a show flat.

Overview

Drawn so it can be built

A family needed guests, prayer and a kitchen for twenty — not a show flat. The shell existed; the plan existed; the rooms did not. This study walks the same path every Woodex engagement walks — requirement, 3D, BOQ — on a villa whose brief is dominated by gathering.

Nothing here is decoration-first. The question the study asks is architectural: where do seventy people eat, pray and circulate on the one day it matters, without the house feeling like a hall for the other three hundred and sixty-four.

Study, not a named case

Named completed work on this site: Wellstar Pharmacy, Cosmetics and Mini Hospital, DHA Lahore. Everything else stays a labelled study until a client names it.

01 · Requirement

What it had to do

What it had to do: hold three generations and their guests with dignity — a formal gathering room that does not dead-end, a prayer space with real quiet, and a kitchen scaled for twenty covers with a service route that never crosses the guests.

Had: an empty shell in Lahore and a floor plan. No finishes, no joinery, no furniture — and one non-negotiable family calendar.

02 · 3D

The room

The room: stills of living, kitchen and court — walnut, limestone, pool-house light — approved before joinery. The 3D gate did the heavy lifting: circulation tested at crowd density, the prayer room's acoustic threshold checked against the court, the kitchen drawn at service width, not showroom width.

  • Stills of living, kitchen and court — approved before joinery
  • Walnut and limestone finish schedule, tied to the still
  • Kitchen planned at service width for twenty covers
  • Prayer room separated from circulation by a drawn threshold
  • Courtyard as the house's daylight engine

03 · BOQ

Then money

Then money: after the stills. Joinery, wet areas, courtyard — each its own line group in the BOQ, each priced against something visible in the approved render. Where the number crossed the budget band, the design changed, not the number's meaning.
