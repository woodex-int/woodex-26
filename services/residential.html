What it covers

What residential design covers

A home is a set of rooms that have to work every day. Woodex designs each one — layout, joinery, lighting, finishes — as part of one coordinated scheme, so the whole house reads as one decision. Custom wardrobes, media walls and kitchens are manufactured in-house by Woodex Furniture™.

  • Space planning, room by room
  • Custom wardrobes & walk-ins (Woodex Furniture™)
  • Media walls & TV units
  • Kitchens & dining joinery
  • Home offices & study corners
  • Kids' rooms that adapt
  • Layered lighting design
  • Full-home coordinated schemes
