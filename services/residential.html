Direct answers

Questions a homeowner actually asks

Do you design one room or the whole house?
Either. The house works better when kitchen, living and storage are planned together.
Can you work with furniture we already own?
Yes. We design around what stays and only make what is missing.
When does 3D happen?
After the plan and direction are approved. We do not invent a layout in 3D and call it design.
