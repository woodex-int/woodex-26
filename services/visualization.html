What it covers

What the studio renders

Every Woodex project is approved in 3D before it is built — and the studio takes outside commissions too. Architects, designers and developers commission renders, tours and walkthroughs even when another practice executes the build.

  • Interior renders
  • Exterior renders
  • 360° virtual tours
  • Material boards
  • Lighting studies (incl. sciography)
  • Walkthrough & flyover animations
  • 4K / print-grade files
  • Two revision rounds included
