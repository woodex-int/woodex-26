What it covers

What office design covers

An office plan is a decision about how a company works. Woodex plans and designs workplaces end to end — reception to boardroom — and produces the technical drawings and BOQ the fit-out is priced from.

FAQ

Office Interior Design — frequently asked.

Plans, organizes and designs the interior of a workplace — space layout, materials, lighting, furniture, partitions, joinery and visual identity. At Woodex, designers also produce the technical drawings and BOQ required for fit-out execution.
Interior design is the planning and visualization phase — concept, 3D renders, working drawings and BOQ. Fit-out is the physical construction phase. Woodex offers both independently and as one turnkey package.
A straightforward office design for a 1,000–2,000 sq ft space typically takes 2–4 weeks from brief to approved drawings. Larger projects with multiple zones and detailed joinery take longer — confirmed per project.
