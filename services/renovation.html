Direct answers

Questions a renovation actually asks

Do we 3D the existing house first?
No. We survey. Then we replan. Then we 3D the rooms that change.
Can we live in the house during works?
Sometimes. Only if the sequence is written. We will say when we cannot.
Will you reuse our kitchen?
If it still works. We do not rip out for the photograph.
