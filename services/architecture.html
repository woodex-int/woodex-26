What it covers

What architecture covers

Architecture at Woodex is the design of the building itself — layout, structure coordination, elevations and the drawings a contractor can build from. Every project moves from brief and site survey through concept (2D + 3D) to working drawings, with the authority submission handled for you.

  • Concept design — 2D plans + 3D
  • Design development
  • Working drawings (AutoCAD / Revit)
  • Front elevation design
  • Structural coordination
  • Authority submission (LDA)
  • Site survey & measured drawings
  • Concept sketch within 5 working days

FAQ

Architecture Services — frequently asked.

An architect designs the building — layout, elevations, the spatial experience. A civil or structural engineer designs the system that holds it up — beams, columns, foundations. Both are needed; Woodex handles the architectural design and coordinates the structure.
For most individual houses on private plots in Lahore it is not mandatory — but it is a mark of professional quality. For commercial buildings, multi-unit developments or submissions requiring an architect of record, it may be. Woodex has PCATP-certified architects on the team.
A typical 5–10 Marla house takes 4–6 weeks from brief to final working drawings; a 1–2 Kanal house 6–10 weeks. Front elevation design takes 5–10 working days. Authority approval times are separate.
