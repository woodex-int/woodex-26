Definition

What Is Office Fit-Out?

Office fit-out is the physical process of converting an empty, shell or partially finished commercial space into a fully functional and furnished working environment. It covers every trade involved in completing the interior of a building — from civil works and partitions to electrical, lighting, flooring, joinery, furniture and final handover.

In Pakistan, fit-out projects range from basic conversions of empty shell floors to complete branded environments with custom joinery, imported finishes, advanced lighting systems and full furniture integration. The scope depends on the starting condition of the space and the level of finish your business requires.

Fit-out is typically a separate phase that follows interior design. The design phase produces the space plan, 3D visuals, working drawings and BOQ; the fit-out phase executes what the design specifies. Woodex Interior delivers both — separately, or as one combined design-and-build contract.

Fit-out types

Office Fit-Out Types We Deliver

From bare-shell conversion to fully branded, furnished, handed-over environments — at every level.

Type01

Shell & Core

A shell and core space is a raw structural shell with no internal finishes, partitions, services or ceilings. This fit-out brings the space to a usable base level.

Suited to: landlords, developers and building owners preparing a floor for a tenant or sale.

  • Concrete or screed floor leveling
  • Perimeter electrical and mechanical rough-in
  • Basic washroom installations
  • Main access and fire-exit provisions
  • Building-fabric waterproofing where required
Type02

Category A

Category A converts a shell space into a neutral, occupiable base — clean, functional, ready for a tenant to add their own Category B interior. No branded or tenant-specific elements.

Suited to: developers and landlords preparing multi-tenant floors for the market.

  • Suspended ceiling system
  • Basic grid lighting
  • Raised access floor — where applicable
  • Air conditioning distribution
  • Electrical distribution to floor boxes
  • Neutral internal surface painting
  • Fire detection and sprinkler connections
Type03

Category B

What most businesses mean by “office fit-out” — the complete finishing and furnishing of a space, specific to the occupying company’s brand, culture and operations.

Suited to: businesses occupying their own or leased space who need a finished, branded, furnished office.

  • Bespoke partitions and layout
  • Branded finishes and wall treatments
  • Custom ceiling design and feature lighting
  • Premium flooring selection
  • Joinery and custom cabinetry
  • Reception and meeting-room design
  • Furniture selection and installation
  • Full MEP coordination
  • AV and technology integration
  • Signage and brand elements
Type04

Turnkey Design & Build

Design and physical execution under one contract, one team, brief to handover — Woodex produces the design, prepares the BOQ, procures and builds.

Suited to: businesses that want one team, one contract and one point of responsibility — concept to move-in.

  • Everything in Category B, plus:
  • Interior design and space planning
  • Concept and mood board
  • 3D visualization and client approval
  • Working drawings and documentation
  • Material schedule and BOQ
  • Procurement management
  • Project management and site supervision

Process

The Office Fit-Out Process — 14 Steps

Every Woodex office fit-out follows the same structured delivery process — transparent, documented and managed from first site visit to signed handover.

  1. 01

    Site Visit and Brief

    Our project team visits your site, takes measurements and conducts a condition review. We discuss requirements, timeline, specification level and budget expectations.

  2. 02

    BOQ and Cost Planning

    A detailed bill of quantities is prepared from the confirmed design or agreed scope — itemized room by room, trade by trade, before any work begins.

  3. 03

    Design Approval

    Where the design phase is included, all space plans, 3D renders and technical drawings are approved by you before procurement or mobilization.

  4. 04

    Contract and Programme

    A formal contract and construction programme are issued — scope, milestones, payment schedule and completion date confirmed.

  5. 05

    Procurement and Lead Times

    Materials, fittings, joinery and furniture are procured to the BOQ and programme. Lead times are tracked to prevent site delays.

  6. 06

    Site Mobilization

    Hoardings, protection of existing areas, contractor access, site safety and material storage are organized before works begin.

  7. 07

    Structural and Civil Works

    Demolition where required, civil repair, surface preparation, block or stud-wall construction and structural modifications — the first trade on site.

  1. 08

    MEP First-Fix

    Electrical conduit, HVAC ductwork, plumbing rough-in and data conduit are installed before ceilings and walls are closed.

  2. 09

    Partitions and Ceilings

    Drywall partitions, glass systems and false-ceiling frameworks are installed and prepared for finishing.

  3. 10

    Flooring and Wall Finishes

    Flooring is laid — tiles, vinyl, wood or epoxy — and wall finishes applied: paint, panels, cladding or wallpaper as specified.

  4. 11

    MEP Second-Fix

    Fittings, light fixtures, AC units, grilles, sockets, switches and plumbing fixtures are installed and tested.

  5. 12

    Joinery and Furniture

    Custom joinery — reception counters, storage, cabinets — is installed, followed by workstations, chairs, tables and all furniture.

  6. 13

    Snagging and Quality Inspection

    A thorough snagging inspection is conducted. Defects are identified, documented and resolved before handover conversations begin.

  7. 14

    Final Cleaning and Handover

    The completed space is deep-cleaned, photographed, documented and formally handed over to the client.

Cost

What Affects the Cost of Office Fit-Out?

These factors determine what your fit-out will cost — before Woodex Interior prepares your BOQ.

Factor F1

Total covered area (sq. ft.)

The primary driver of fit-out cost. Larger spaces need more materials, more trades and more management time. Cost is typically calculated per square foot — the rate varies with scope and specification.

Factor F2

Fit-out type — Cat A, Cat B or turnkey

A bare-shell conversion to Cat A costs less per square foot than a fully branded Cat B environment. Turnkey adds design fees, procurement management and project coordination to execution.

Factor F3

Specification level

Economy, mid-range and premium carry very different material and labour costs. Flooring, ceiling system, glass type, lighting brand and joinery finish all move the budget significantly.

Factor F4

Condition of the existing space

A brand-new shell needs different preparation than an occupied office. Strip-out, demolition, civil repair and protection of existing elements all add scope and cost.

Factor F5

Rooms, zones and partitions

An open-plan office with one meeting room costs significantly less than multiple cabins, a boardroom, server room, prayer facilities, pantry, washrooms and collaboration zones.

Factor F6

MEP scope and complexity

Electrical, lighting, HVAC and plumbing scope varies by sector. Healthcare, laboratory and F&B fit-outs need more intensive MEP coordination than standard offices.

Factor F7

Furniture and joinery requirements

Custom-manufactured joinery — reception counters, storage walls, branded millwork — is separate from structural scope. Furniture specification, from economy workstations to imported executive systems, varies widely.

The Cost-Lock: an itemized BOQ before any work begins. You will not receive a lump-sum figure with no breakdown. Every line item is costed separately so you can make informed decisions about scope and specification before the contract is signed.
