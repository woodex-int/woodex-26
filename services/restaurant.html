What it covers

What restaurant fit-out covers

A restaurant lives or dies in the kitchen line. Woodex maps the full workflow — prep, cooking, plating, service — in consultation with your chef, then designs the dining room around the experience you are selling. Exhaust, drainage and food-safe finishes are designed in from day one.

  • Kitchen layout & equipment coordination
  • Dining, bar & lounge design
  • Ambient, task & decorative lighting
  • Anti-slip, easy-clean flooring
  • Acoustic & fire-rated ceilings
  • Custom joinery — bars, counters, service stations
  • Ventilation & exhaust systems
  • Wet areas & plumbing
