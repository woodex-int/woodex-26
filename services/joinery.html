What it covers

What the workshop covers

When the joinery is made by the same studio that drew the room, nothing is lost in translation. Woodex Furniture™ manufactures bespoke pieces to your exact dimensions — fitted internals, premium hardware, a finish matched to the scheme — and installs them as part of the same project.

  • Custom & walk-in wardrobes
  • Media walls & TV units
  • Kitchens & vanities
  • Display units & shelving
  • Bars, counters & service stations
  • Desks & workstations
  • Doors & wall panelling
  • Fitted internals & premium hardware
