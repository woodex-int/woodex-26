Direct answers

Questions a retailer asks

Do you start from the logo?
No. We start from how a customer walks. The logo sits on a path that already works.
Can you work to a opening date?
If the still is approved and the BOQ is written. Dates without quantities are theatre.
Will you execute another designer’s pack?
After we read it against the site. We do not build a file we have not reviewed.
