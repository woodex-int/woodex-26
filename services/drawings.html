Questions a documentation brief asks

Can you draw from a Pinterest board?
No. Drawings follow an approved plan or still. We do not invent a layout on a sheet and call it design.
Do you stamp as architect of record?
We issue interior drawings for the rooms we are contracted to. Your consultant remains your consultant.
Will the mill read these?
That is the test. If the mill cannot, the sheet is not done.
