Definition

What Is Pharmacy Fit-Out?

Pharmacy fit-out is the build-out of a bare shell or empty unit into a fully operational dispensing pharmacy — every element designed around pharmacist workflow, medication storage, regulatory compliance and customer experience.

Unlike general commercial fit-out, every decision in a pharmacy serves a specific clinical or regulatory purpose: the dispensing counter layout, controlled drug storage, temperature-controlled zones, antibacterial finishes, pharmacy-specific lighting and security systems.

Every Woodex pharmacy fit-out is developed in AutoCAD and visualized in photorealistic 3D before construction begins — you see exactly how your pharmacy will look and function before a single partition goes up. For chain operators, we develop a repeatable prototype deployed across locations with consistent quality and faster timelines.

Process

Our Pharmacy Fit-Out Process — 6 Steps

Structured, documented and managed — from first site survey to inspection-ready handover.

  1. 01

    Site Survey & Brief

    We visit your site, measure the space, check structural conditions and utilities — then discuss counter type, storage needs, branding, compliance, budget and timeline.

  2. 02

    Design & 3D Approval

    Floor plans and photorealistic 3D renders of your pharmacy — counter layout, shelving, lighting, finishes, branding. You approve before we proceed. Two revision rounds included.

  3. 03

    Licensing Compliance Check

    The design is reviewed against pharmacy licensing requirements — controlled drug storage, dispensing area dimensions, hygiene standards, signage — and adjusted before construction.

  4. 04

    Construction & MEP

    Partitions, ceilings, flooring, electrical, plumbing, HVAC and carpentry executed by Woodex site teams. Pharmacy-specific MEP — temperature zones, dedicated circuits, exhaust — coordinated from design stage.

  5. 05

    Finishing & Branding

    Antibacterial paint, anti-slip flooring, counter surfaces, shelving, signage, graphics — plus security systems: CCTV, alarms, controlled drug cabinet locks.

  6. 06

    Handover & Snagging

    Complete snagging walkthrough, corrections and handover. Your pharmacy is operational-ready — clean, compliant and fitted for inspection.

Timeline: a standard independent pharmacy (800–1,200 sq ft) takes 4–6 weeks from brief to handover. A chain prototype takes 6–8 weeks; subsequent locations 4–5 weeks each.
